# AMIU Publications — Editorial & Governance Review Log

**Internal record only. This file is not assembled into, referenced by, or
distributed as part of any of the nine published AMIU documents.** It exists
so the review history that previously appeared as "Publication Certification
Statement" sections inside the published documents is preserved somewhere,
without the published documents themselves narrating their own drafting and
correction history. The nine publications now read as final, authoritative
institutional documents; this log is where "what was found and why it was
changed" lives instead.

The per-policy citation and approval-authority fixes for the Operations,
Legal & Compliance, Marketing & Communications, and Waqf & Research
Handbooks are documented in full in each handbook's own generator script
(assets/gen_ops_handbook_body.py, gen_leg_handbook_body.py,
gen_mkt_handbook_body.py, gen_waq_handbook_body.py) rather than duplicated
here, following the same "what was found and why" standard as the entries
below.

Each entry below records what a review found, what was changed, and why —
exactly as previously disclosed inside the relevant publication's own back
matter, before that disclosure was relocated here.

---

## Strategic Implementation Blueprint (AMIU-SB-002)

Prior to publication, this Blueprint underwent two structured, independent
quality assurance reviews by the AMIU Office of Institutional Planning, with
oversight from the Board Audit & Risk Committee (Section 36) and the
University Senate. The second review was conducted as a fresh, adversarial
re-read — auditors were not shown the first review's findings and were
instructed to verify the document as though encountering it for the first
time, rather than to confirm that earlier corrections held.

Review scope: structural integrity (every Section-to-Part assignment,
Section kicker label, figure/table numbering sequence, Table of Contents
entry, and Index cross-reference checked programmatically against the
document's own numbering system, in both rounds); financial consistency
(every dollar figure, percentage, and enrollment number checked against
AMIU-MP-001, against every other citation of the same figure elsewhere in
the Blueprint, and — in the second round — against the arithmetic each
figure is supposed to satisfy); editorial review (prose read in full,
independently, in both rounds); design consistency (typography,
headers/footers, table styling, and page-level hierarchy checked for
structural consistency, with the second round specifically re-examining
covers and every page class independently).

**First review found and corrected:** several stale references to the
document's section count following a later structural addition;
dollar-figure rounding inconsistencies between sections discussing the same
reserve line at different points; two internal cross-references pointing to
the wrong section; a number of spelling-convention and capitalization
inconsistencies.

**Second, independent review found and corrected:** a Table of Contents
entry that hyphenated mid-word; an inconsistent header/footer treatment
between the front and back covers; an arithmetic reconciliation in the Waqf
& Stakeholder Reserve narrative that did not sum correctly; a
certification-cost line item whose components did not add to its stated
ceiling; three misordered Index entries; a governance-responsibility line
that misattributed a University-funded activity to the Commercial Engine;
one internally contradictory college-pairing assignment; one cross-reference
to a faculty title that did not exist elsewhere in the document; a further
round of spelling-convention, terminology, and cross-reference corrections.

**Subsequent addition (later review pass):** the 14-item Risk Register
(Section 36) had no scored entry for cybersecurity incidents or student/
financial data breaches, despite Section 37's own text conceding this
exposure was "material given a global online student body." Added as Risk
15, dated to Year 1 (founding-decade), not deferred to the Year-11+
expansion bucket. Mitigation funding stays inside the existing LMS &
Technology Infrastructure line; what changed is that the risk is now scored
and reviewed quarterly by the Audit & Risk Committee like every other
register item. The three previously-anticipated Year-11+ expansion risks
were renumbered 16-18 (were 15-17); every cross-reference (KPI count,
Section 36/37 timelines, Twenty-Year Roadmap table) was updated to match.

## Constitution (AMIU-CON-001)

Before typesetting, this Constitution underwent a structured editorial
review by the AMIU Office of Institutional Planning, examining every
Article for numbering consistency, duplicated provisions, missing or
inconsistent cross-references, table formatting, and governance ambiguity —
the same standard applied to the Blueprint.

**Issues found and corrected:**
- **Cross-document terminology.** "DVC Administration & Finance" was
  standardized to "DVC Admin & Finance" throughout, matching the term
  already adopted in the Blueprint — the one genuine inconsistency found
  between the University's two flagship publications.
- **Citation style.** Section 6.3.1.3 cited "Article 3, Section 3.3," the
  only provision in the document using that verbose form; normalized to
  "Section 3.3" to match the citation convention used everywhere else.
- **A genuine due-process gap.** Section 6.3.4.2 permitted the Board to
  "disqualify" a Trustee for cause with no defined procedure, while Section
  6.4 required a two-thirds vote and formal notice-and-hearing for "removal"
  on the *same* grounds — meaning a Board could have bypassed the hearing
  protection simply by labeling the action "disqualification." Section
  6.3.4.2 now expressly cross-references the Section 6.4 process.
- **Presentation order.** The Academic Ladder table (Section 10.1.1) was
  reordered from highest-credential-first to ascending, foundation-to-summit
  order, consistent with its own "stackable ladder" language and the
  matching Blueprint figure.

**Reviewed and deliberately left unchanged, because not errors:** the
repeated Section 501(c)(3)/170(c)(2) boilerplate across Sections 2.2, 20.1,
and 21.3 (standard, IRS-expected redundancy in real nonprofit governing
documents); and Section 5.1's numbered governance table, which could read as
placing the Senate beneath the President in a strict chain of command until
Section 8.1.3 is reached, which expressly reconciles it as a reporting line
rather than an authority ranking (Figure C1 was designed to present the
Board and Senate as co-equal chambers for exactly this reason).

**Subsequent additions (later review pass, a compound-crisis and
merger/restructuring gap analysis):**
- **Section 5.6, Continuity of Governance.** The Constitution covered
  single-office vacancies (Chairperson, President, Senate seats) but had no
  rule for a Compound Vacancy — both the Chairperson and President vacant at
  once. Added: an automatic order of precedence (Vice-Chairperson of the
  Board / senior Deputy Vice-Chancellor, with fallbacks), a 7-day emergency
  Board session requirement, a 180-day cap on interim tenure, and an express
  limit on what acting officers may do without a two-thirds Board vote.
- **Section 5.7, Merger, Acquisition & Institutional Restructuring.** No
  approval threshold existed for a merger, acquisition, or new
  branch-campus commitment beyond what the Blueprint already adopts. Added:
  a defined "Restructuring Transaction," a two-thirds Board (and Senate,
  where academic governance is affected) approval requirement, and a
  mandatory written impact assessment (mission/values, Islamic identity, the
  Revenue Allocation Framework/Commercial Engine firewall, student/faculty
  rights, accreditation status) before any vote.

## Institutional Governance Compendium (AMIU-IGC-004)

Before typesetting, this Compendium underwent a structured editorial and
data-integrity review by the AMIU Office of Institutional Planning, extended
to include a full row-by-row reconciliation of the 107-document registry
(the user-supplied source, AMIU-IGC-004 v4.0, Section 17) against every
summary and roadmap table built on top of it.

**Issues found and corrected:**
- **A genuine summary-arithmetic error.** Section 19.2's "Summary by
  Priority" stated Critical 62 (58%), Important 38 (35%), Complete 7 (7%). A
  manual tally of all 107 rows in Section 17 — cross-checked against the
  seven category subtotals in Section 18.1-18.7, all of which reconciled
  correctly to 107 — found the true counts to be Critical 49 (45.8%),
  Important 53 (49.5%), Complete 5 (4.7%). Now generated directly from
  Section 17's row data so the two can never again drift apart.
- **A matching roadmap error, plus a double-booking and a missing
  document.** Section 22.1's phase counts (Phase 2 "53," Phase 3 "38") had
  the Critical/Important totals transposed relative to Section 19.2's own
  (also incorrect) summary. Separately, documents 70-72 (Backup & Disaster
  Recovery, IT Support, Acceptable Use) were listed in *both* Phase 3 and
  Phase 4, while document 68 (LMS Technical Specifications) was not
  scheduled into any phase at all. The roadmap is now generated
  programmatically as Phase 1 (9, unchanged) + Phase 2 (40 — every Critical
  document not in Phase 1) + Phase 3 (46 — every Important document not in
  Phase 4) + Phase 4 (7, unchanged), summing to the 102 documents actually
  requiring development, with no document appearing twice or not at all.
- **A cross-reference numbering error.** Section 21.2's dependency list
  coded the Certificate of Formation as "(LEG-002)" — a code already
  assigned elsewhere in the same list to the GDPR Compliance Policy. Per
  Section 17, the Certificate of Formation is a Governance document (item 4
  of 11), so its correct category-relative code is GOV-004.
- **A missing cross-reference key.** Section 1.5 defines category-relative
  document codes (e.g. GOV-003), but Section 17's master inventory
  previously showed only the flat 1-107 reference numbers used in Sections
  17, 20, and 22, while Section 21 used category-relative codes for the same
  documents with no table connecting the two schemes. Section 17 now
  carries both side by side in a "Doc Code" column.
- **A governance-hierarchy presentation inconsistency**, directly analogous
  to one corrected in the Constitution. Section 4.1's numbered "Level 1-4"
  table placed the Senate above the Administration, which could be read as a
  strict chain of command, while Section 4.2's text and the organizational
  chart establish the Senate and Administration as parallel, co-equal bodies
  reporting through the President. Section 4.1's table is now annotated to
  make the co-equal Tier 3 explicit, and the former ASCII-art chart in
  Section 4.4 is replaced by Exhibit G1, a diagram purpose-drawn to show the
  two chambers as parallel rather than sequential.

**Reviewed and deliberately left unchanged, because not an error:** the
Growth Summary in Section 11.1 shows Year 3 enrollment (1,606) between the
figures separately cited for Year 1 (347) and Year 5 (3,493); all three
figures were cross-checked against the Constitution and Blueprint's own
enrollment projections and agree exactly.

**Subsequent additions (later review passes):**
- **Phantom "Chief Financial Officer" title.** The Year-10 committee roster
  (Section 15.3, Budget & Planning Committee) named "Chief Financial
  Officer" as chair — a title that appeared exactly once across all five
  documents; every other reference to AMIU's finance executive uses
  "Deputy Vice-Chancellor, Administration & Finance." Corrected to the DVC
  title actually in use.
- **Document 108, Institutional Effectiveness & Administrative Unit
  Assessment Framework (OPS-028).** The registry had full 5-year assessment
  coverage for academic programs (ACA-003) but none for non-academic
  administrative units (Admissions, Registrar, Finance, IT, Student Affairs,
  HR, Marketing, Library, Institutional Research, Quality Assurance, Waqf
  Administration, Student Support Services) — a gap against standard
  accreditation expectations (institution-wide, not just academic,
  effectiveness review). Added a 5-year review cycle for every listed unit,
  annual KPI monitoring between cycles, a defined evidence standard
  (objectives, KPIs, baselines, targets, outcomes, improvement actions,
  follow-up review), and an escalation path of Unit → Director → DVC →
  President → Board Audit & Risk Committee → Board of Trustees. Appended as
  item 108 — after the original 107 rows, not inserted among them — so no
  existing document's absolute number or Doc Code moved. Every summary and
  roadmap table (Sections 17-22), the priority/category tallies, the
  Twenty-Year Roadmap figure, and both Handbooks' "of 107" doc-control
  references were updated to "of 108" accordingly.

## Academic Handbook (AMIU-ACAH-001)

Before typesetting, this Handbook underwent the same structured editorial
review applied to the Constitution and Compendium: every policy's
cross-references were checked against the Compendium's corrected Doc Code
registry, every numeric figure was checked against established University
canon, and every citation to the Constitution was checked against the
Constitution's actual Article and Section headings.

**Issues found and corrected:**
- **Thirteen mismatched Constitution citations.** Nine policies (ACA-002,
  003, 004, 006, 007, 013, 014, 016, 019) cited "Constitution Article 8,
  Section 8.6" — the Senate's Voting Procedures clause, unrelated to
  curriculum development, program assessment, syllabi, course evaluation,
  committee charters, academic integrity, learning-outcomes assessment,
  remote proctoring, or virtual student support. Corrected to Article 8,
  Section 8.1 (Role and Authority), the clause establishing the Senate's
  supreme academic authority. Four further policies (ACA-008, 009, 011, 015,
  018) cited "Article 10, Section 10.1" (Academic Ladder) for subject matter
  unrelated to the credential ladder; corrected to a general Article 10
  citation. ACA-017 (Technology Requirements) cited "Article 15, Section
  15.1" (Annual Report) for a policy unrelated to financial reporting;
  corrected to Article 9, Section 9.4 (Administration Responsibilities).
  ACA-010 and ACA-012's citations to Article 10, Section 10.1 were already
  correct.
- **An overlapping honors GPA schedule.** ACA-012, Section 8.1 originally
  defined Cum Laude as 3.5-3.7, Magna Cum Laude as 3.7-3.9, and Summa Cum
  Laude as 3.9-4.0 — a student with a GPA of exactly 3.7 or exactly 3.9 fell
  inside two tiers at once. Corrected to non-overlapping bands (3.50-3.69 /
  3.70-3.89 / 3.90-4.00).
- **An incomplete credit-hour equivalency table.** ACA-010, Section 4 listed
  ECTS equivalents for five of the Academic Ladder's seven tiers, omitting
  the Postgraduate Diploma (30 CH). Added at the same 2:1 ratio the other
  tiers use.

**Reviewed and confirmed consistent:** every cross-reference to another
category's document (LMS Technical Specifications as OPS-015, IT
Infrastructure Plan as OPS-014, Mental Health Support Policy as STU-009,
Career Services Policy as STU-010, and internal ACA-0NN cross-references)
matches the Compendium's Doc Code registry exactly.

## Student Policies Handbook (AMIU-STUH-001)

Before typesetting, this Handbook underwent the same structured editorial
review applied to the Constitution, Compendium, and Academic Handbook.

**Issues found and corrected:**
- **Six mismatched Constitution citations.** STU-001, STU-006, STU-014,
  STU-015, STU-016, and STU-023 all cited "Constitution Article 12, Section
  12.2" (Rights) despite being substantively about student conduct and
  compliance obligations, not rights; corrected to Section 12.3
  (Responsibilities).
- **One mismatched Article entirely.** STU-003 (Student Recruitment Plan)
  cited "Constitution Article 11, Section 11.2" (Waqf Reserve), unrelated to
  admissions/recruitment; corrected to Article 12, Section 12.1 (Admission).
- **One mismatched Doc Code.** STU-011 (Disability Services Policy) cited
  its Non-Discrimination Policy cross-reference as "LEG-004" — the code
  already assigned to the Intellectual Property Policy. Per the registry,
  the Non-Discrimination & Title IX Policy is LEG-005; corrected.

**Flagged, not corrected here (not this Handbook's to resolve):** the
Compendium's own master registry lists "International Student Support
Policy" twice — once as STU-013 (general student-services angle) and again
as LEG-011 (immigration-compliance angle). The two documents are plausibly
distinct in subject even though they share a title, but the overlap was not
caught during the Compendium's own editorial review.

**Reviewed and confirmed intentional, not a duplication error:** STU-001
and STU-023 share substantially the same Sanctions Matrix and much of the
same disciplinary-process language by design — STU-001 establishes the
behavioral standard and its baseline enforcement mechanism, while STU-023 is
the deeper, dedicated disciplinary framework the Compendium's registry lists
as its own separate document (item 53). Retained in full in both, as
supplied, rather than cut from either.

## Cross-Document Single-Source-of-Truth Audit (all nine publications)

With all nine flagship publications in place, a separate audit pass checked
for drift between documents rather than within any one of them — the same
committee names, titles, colors, fonts, and facts should read identically
wherever they appear across the whole family.

**Issues found and corrected:**
- **Brand Style Guide (MKT-001) colors and fonts didn't match reality.**
  The policy stated the University's official colors as Royal Blue
  #1A237E, Gold #FFD700, and Red #B71C1C, and its approved fonts as
  Georgia and Times New Roman — none of which any of the nine published
  covers or tables actually use. Corrected to the exact values already in
  use throughout the suite (Navy #122A4E, Gold #B08625, cream table-tint
  #F7F3E8; Fraunces display, Source Serif 4 reading, Archivo structural),
  sourced directly from assets/assemble.py and assets/build_reference.py
  rather than re-guessed.
- **Two conflicting official taglines.** The Strategic Implementation
  Blueprint's cover, back cover, copyright/trademark notice, and Section 29
  all asserted "Spreading Islamic Education Worldwide, at Every Pace" as
  the University's tagline and the phrase filed for USPTO trademark
  protection. The Constitution (Article 3.4.1) and the Brand Style Guide
  both say the tagline is "Knowledge Without Barriers" — which is also
  what appears on the back cover of all eight other publications. Aligned
  the Blueprint to the Constitution in all four places the old tagline
  appeared.
- **A documented fix that had regressed.** An earlier review (see the
  Compendium's entry above) found and corrected a phantom "Chief Financial
  Officer" title in the Year-10 Committee Roster (Budget & Planning
  Committee) to "Deputy Vice-Chancellor, Administration & Finance" — but
  the live generator (assets/gen_igc_body.py) still had the unfixed title;
  the documented correction had never actually landed in the source that
  regenerates the document. Corrected for real this time, and re-verified
  in the rendered PDF, not just the source file.
- **The same pattern, twice more, not previously caught.** The Year-5
  Committee Roster's Information Technology Committee listed "Chief
  Information Officer" as chair — a title used nowhere else across any
  publication, where every IT-related policy and office consistently uses
  "Director, Information Technology." And the Offices Roster listed
  "Office of Internal Audit & Risk"'s head as "Director / Chief Risk
  Officer" — directly contradicting the Blueprint's own explicit,
  deliberate governance decision that "No Chief Risk Officer exists in the
  founding decade," with risk ownership assigned instead to the Board
  Audit & Risk Committee. Both corrected — the first to "Director,
  Information Technology", the second to "Chair, Audit & Risk Committee",
  matching the title already used five times across the Waqf & Research
  Handbook's risk-trio policies.

**Method:** doc-code cross-references were checked programmatically against
igc_data.py's registry across all six policy handbooks (164 references
checked, only two false positives from docstring prose, zero real
mismatches); titles, colors, fonts, and the tagline were checked by direct
grep across every source file rather than sampling. This is an ongoing
process, not a closed one — later passes may find more.
