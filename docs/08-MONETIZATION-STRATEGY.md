# 08 — Monetisation Strategy

## 1. Governing Principle

Monetisation must never compromise access to core worship functionality or content integrity.
The free tier is a complete, dignified spiritual tool on its own — not a deliberately crippled
demo. This is both an ethical commitment (Shariah governance) and a growth strategy (trust drives
the retention metrics in the [PRD](01-PRD.md#8-success-metrics-north-star--supporting)).

**Hard rule:** No advertising is shown anywhere in the app. Ads in a worship context read as
disrespectful to the target audience and would undermine the luxury positioning outright.

## 2. Revenue Streams

| Stream | Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Misbaha Plus** (consumer subscription) | $2.99/mo or $19.99/yr (localized pricing per market PPP) | Cloud sync across devices, premium reciter audio packs, premium visual themes, family circles (up to 6), advanced journal analytics/export |
| **Misbaha for Institutions** | Seat/tenant-based B2B SaaS, tiered by congregation size | White-label branding, bulk seat management, institutional analytics dashboard |
| **Physical companion hardware** | One-time purchase, BLE smart ring/counter co-branded with Misbaha | Premium hardware accessory line, luxury unboxing consistent with brand |
| **Waqf / Sadaqah Partnership Layer** (opt-in, non-transactional in-app) | Referral partnership with vetted, licensed charity platforms | App surfaces "Continue your good deed" links to external licensed donation platforms; Misbaha does not process donations directly, avoiding financial-services regulatory scope creep |
| **Content licensing** | B2B licensing of the verified adhkar/Qur'an content API to third-party halal apps | Leverages the Governance Board's trust asset as a standalone product |

## 3. What Remains Free, Forever

- Full tasbeeh counter, all default target counts
- The entire core Adhkar library (Morning/Evening/Post-Salah/Travel/Sleep/Ruqyah) with at least
  one verified translation and standard audio
- Full Qur'an text and at least one reciter
- Local journal/streaks (device-local; cloud sync is the premium upgrade, not history itself)
- All accessibility features (never paywalled — matches inclusive-design commitment)

## 4. Institutional Pricing Tiers (illustrative)

| Tier | Congregation size | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Community | < 500 members | Branded circle, basic aggregate analytics |
| Mosque | 500–5,000 | + custom branding, bulk onboarding, priority support |
| Waqf/Enterprise | 5,000+ / multi-branch | + SSO, dedicated success manager, custom content curation within Governance Board rules, SLA |

## 5. Pricing Ethics & Regional Fairness

- Purchasing-power-adjusted pricing across launch markets (KSA/UAE priced at premium parity;
  South/Southeast Asian markets priced to remain accessible) — access to spiritual tools should
  not be gated by income disparity between Gulf and non-Gulf Muslim-majority markets.
- Family/community sponsorship: institutions can gift Plus subscriptions to members in need
  (waqf-style sponsorship mechanic), reinforcing brand values over pure ARPU maximization.

## 6. Forecast Assumptions (directional, for planning only)

- Target 3–5% free-to-Plus conversion at maturity (above freemium-utility-app norms, justified by
  trust premium and habit-formation stickiness).
- Institutional revenue expected to become >40% of total revenue by Year 3 as B2B contracts scale
  faster and more predictably than consumer ARPU — see [Five-Year Roadmap](11-FIVE-YEAR-ROADMAP.md).
