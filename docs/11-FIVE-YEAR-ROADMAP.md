# 11 — Five-Year Product Roadmap

## Year 1 (2026–2027) — Foundation: Android Excellence

- Ship Android MVP → v1.0: Counter engine, verified Adhkar library, offline-first sync, AR/EN,
  accessibility baseline, GCC public launch ([Deployment Plan Phase 0–1](10-DEPLOYMENT-LAUNCH-PLAN.md#4-phased-market-launch)).
- Establish the Islamic Content Governance Board as a standing operational body, not a one-time
  launch exercise.
- Launch Misbaha Plus subscription and first institutional pilots (3–5 flagship mosques).
- Ship BLE smart-ring pairing (hardware partnership, not yet own-branded hardware).

## Year 2 (2027–2028) — Scale & Platform Breadth

- MENA + South Asia + Southeast Asia market expansion (Urdu, Malay/Indonesian localization).
- Institutional self-serve onboarding + Admin Dashboard GA.
- Begin Kotlin Multiplatform extraction of `core-domain`/`core-model`
  ([Architecture §1](04-ANDROID-ARCHITECTURE.md#1-guiding-constraints)) to de-risk iOS.
- iOS launch (feature parity with Android core loop) and responsive Web app (read-mostly Library
  + account management; full counter experience web-enabled by year end).
- Own-branded physical Misbaha smart-ring hardware line (first monetized hardware SKU).

## Year 3 (2028–2029) — Depth & Ecosystem

- Wear OS + watchOS companion apps (tap-to-count on wrist, complications showing daily progress).
- Institutional revenue becomes >40% of total revenue ([Monetisation §6](08-MONETIZATION-STRATEGY.md#6-forecast-assumptions-directional-for-planning-only)).
- Content API licensing product launched for third-party halal-app partners
  ([Monetisation §2](08-MONETIZATION-STRATEGY.md#2-revenue-streams)).
- Western diaspora market push (UK, US, Canada, France, Germany) with convert-focused onboarding.
- Advanced personalization: prayer-time-aware smart reminder tuning based on on-device usage
  patterns (fully on-device ML, no behavioral data leaves the device for this feature).

## Year 4 (2029–2030) — Ambient & Automotive

- Android Auto / CarPlay integration: voice-first dhikr counting for commute use, hands-free.
- Android TV / tvOS app: family/masjid-hall ambient display mode (large-format Qur'an reading,
  communal dhikr counters for iftar gatherings).
- Cross-device continuity: start a session on phone, continue seamlessly on watch/TV/car.
- Regional data-residency GA for all major markets (KSA, EU, Indonesia) to satisfy maturing local
  data-sovereignty regulation.

## Year 5 (2030–2031) — Platform Maturity & Category Leadership

- 10M+ MAU target across all surfaces, positioning Misbaha as the reference luxury Islamic
  digital product category-wide (the explicit north-star positioning from
  [Brand Identity §2](03-BRAND-IDENTITY.md#2-brand-positioning)).
- Deepened Vision 2030-aligned institutional partnerships (potential integration touchpoints with
  Haramain digital services, subject to official partnership agreements).
- Explore adjacent, carefully-scoped verticals only after rigorous Governance Board and legal
  review (e.g., Islamic learning/tajweed coaching) — explicitly not zakat/payments or social-feed
  features, which remain permanently out of scope per the [PRD §7](01-PRD.md#7-out-of-scope-v1)
  unless a future dedicated regulated product line is spun up separately.

## Cross-Cutting Themes (every year)

- Accessibility and localization depth increase every release, never treated as "done."
- Security/privacy posture re-certified annually (penetration test, DPIA refresh).
- Governance Board scholar roster grows in step with new markets to maintain madhhab-balanced
  review capacity.
