## 3. FINDING OF FACT — CR-03 resolves on internal consistency

`[CALCULATED FROM RECOVERED DATA]`

The Council's own four-section architecture is: التمهيدي G1–G2 · الابتدائية
G3–G6 · الإعدادية **G7–G9** · الثانوية **G10–G12**.

ACD-007's stages are: … الإعدادية **٧·٨·٩** · الثانوية والتتويج **١٠·١١·١٢**.

**G7–G12 align exactly with ACD-007's numbering.** Alignment at the top forces
alignment at the bottom: **G1 = ACD-007's الصف ١, indicative age 6.**

Two consequences follow, and neither was visible before:

**(a) The روضة is abolished, not mapped.** Had را١→G1 been a true remapping,
ACD-007's grades ١١ and ١٢ would have had no destination — الثانوية القرآنية
and شهادة التخرج, the two capstone gates, would have fallen off the ladder.
They have not. The compression therefore removed the pre-stage.

**(b) The قاعدة at §4.4 governs a stage that no longer exists.** The 10-hour
early-dismissal rule is written for الروضة, «تناسب **سن التأسيس**» — ages 4–6.
G1 is age 6, which is where ACD-007 *ends* التأسيس and *begins* الصفوف.

**The proposal at §1 is therefore rejected.** The «7 حصص» figure is a روضة
figure. It was never a statement about grade 1, and ledger v2 §5.1 attached it
to grade 1 in error. Both that error and the earlier v1 error — a "650-minute
unexplained remainder" — have the same root: reasoning about G1–G2 from a
number without first establishing which stage the number described.

**Working position for the lenses below:** G1–G2 are الابتدائية الدنيا grades
1–2, ages 6–8, carrying the **full week — 19 حصص, 19 hours 20 minutes**.
Status `[BLACK]` pending ratification; the lenses now test it.

## 4. INDEPENDENT REDLINE — ten lenses

Each lens examined the working position without sight of the others' conclusions.

### L1 · Qur'an, ḥifẓ and tajwīd

The Qur'an hour is **5 hours a week at every grade**, G1 to G11 alike. Under
the rejected 7-حصة reading its share of the infant week was 49%; under the full
week it is 26%, identical to G8. **Neither share was chosen; both are residues.**

`[RECOVERED]` §4.2 fixes the hour's anatomy: ١٥ سبقي · ٢٠ منزل · ٢٠ سبق
بتصحيحه · ٥ أداء. **20 of those 60 minutes are منزل — the accumulated retained
portion.** A pupil in the first term of grade 1 has no منزل. The recovered
anatomy is therefore not executable as written at G1, and one third of the
protected hour has no content at entry. Referred as **CR-11**; the Council
notes this is not a case for shortening the hour but for stating its G1 anatomy.

Against the acquisition evidence already on file — oral talqīn ≈ 260 minutes
per āyah, reading-based ≈ 40 — an unresolved question stands as to whether the
G1 memorisation target is reachable inside 5 hours a week. **The lens declines
to estimate it** until the G1 target is restated against the corrected week.

### L2 · Arabic pedagogy and classical sequencing — **gravest finding**

`[RECOVERED]` التأسيس delivered **التهيئة الصوتية واللعبية** — phonological and
play-based preparation, ages 4–6. `[RECOVERED]` الابتدائية الدنيا begins **فك
الحرف** and its exit gate is **بوابة القراءة at grade 1**.

The compression abolished التأسيس. **G1 must therefore reach the reading gate
in one year, from age 6, with the two-year phonological preparation that
preceded it deleted.** The registry's own law is «لا عربية بلا قراءة».

This is not a timetable problem and no number of minutes solves it. Either the
صوتية preparation is rebuilt inside G1, or بوابة القراءة moves, or entry to G1
presumes phonological readiness acquired elsewhere — which the institution does
not control. **Referred as CR-13.**

### L3 · Islamic sciences

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 §3.2 registers **التربية الإسلامية (ر–٦)** as a single
undifferentiated subject to grade 6, with the stated reason «الدين محبة قبل أن
يكون علومًا مسماة». The 26-subject universe places **فقه تطبيقي** and **توحيد
عملي** as separate claimants at G1–G2.

Layer A holds Programme 3 deliberately whole at G1–G2; Layer B fragments it.
**Both are preserved; the contradiction is named.** `[RED]` **CR-08.** The lens
records that the earlier direction «do not invent fragmentation» points one way
and the 26-subject universe the other, and that this is a decision for the
Director General, not an arithmetic reconciliation.

### L4 · Curriculum science

Under the rejected 7-حصة week the distribution was 1/1/1/2/2 across five days —
**at most two contacts a week for any subject**, below the threshold at which
spaced retrieval operates for this age. Under the full week, 19 حصص distribute
3/3/3/5/5, and three or more contacts become possible for several subjects.

The lens notes that **no subject in the 26 lifecycles states its required
number of weekly *contacts*** — every one states a number of periods. Frequency
and volume are different quantities, and only volume has ever been ledgered.
**Referred as CR-14: the ledger counts minutes and has never counted contacts.**

### L5 · Child development and cognitive load — **gravest finding**

The ledger has only ever measured SHRS's own slice. `[RECOVERED]` §3.1 states
the pupils attend conventional schooling in the mornings. Costing the **child's
whole week**, on an indicative 08:00–14:00 conventional day:

| | Conventional | SHRS | Day total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon–Wed | 6 h | 4 h | **10 h** |
| Thu–Fri | 6 h | — | 6 h |
| Sat–Sun | — | 6 h | 6 h |

**Weekly total: 54 hours. Seven days. No day free of structured education. At
age 6.**

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` — the conventional day length is indicative, not
recovered; the *structure* is recovered. Even at a shorter conventional day the
finding survives: الخميس والجمعة are described as «راحة الأسبوع», but they are
rest from SHRS only. **The institution has no rest day at all, and has never
known it, because no document costs the other school.**

Separately: the **40-minute حصة** is an institutional slot, not a lesson. At
age 6, sustained single-activity attention is on the order of 10–15 minutes.
The slot should stand; what happens inside it must be sub-divided. See §6.

### L6 · Assessment and examination

`[RECOVERED]` التأسيس carried **«لا مقاعد امتحان»** — no examination seats —
with a gate of documented readiness observation. `[RECOVERED]` الابتدائية
الدنيا carries **بوابة القراءة at grade 1**, a real gate.

Abolishing التأسيس moves a six-year-old from a stage constitutionally free of
examination into a stage that gates at the end of its first year. **The
no-examination protection was deleted along with the stage that carried it, and
nobody decided to delete it.** Referred as **CR-15**.

Further: the 26 lifecycles repeatedly claim "independently assessed" for
subjects requesting no period. Assessment consumes slot time. It is nowhere
ledgered.

### L7 · Timetable and school operations

The rejected early-dismissal rule carried operational load nobody costed:
mid-session dismissal at 16:30 and 11:35, supervision, guardian collection —
and, because `[RECOVERED]` §4.4 states prayer times are **seasonal** and
administered inside the drawn ʿAṣr and Ẓuhr bands, **a dismissal time that
moves through the year**. Rejecting the rule removes that risk; the Council
records it in case any reduced-week option is revived.

On the full week the operational question is staffing: 19 حصص at G1–G2 means
G1–G2 teachers are engaged for the whole week, where the روضة reading freed
them after حصة ١. Whichever is ruled, **the staffing model changes**, and no
staffing model has been drawn at all.

### L8 · International curriculum and institutional standards — **gravest finding**

`[RECOVERED]` §3.1: «منظومة شرعية لغوية **مسائية وعطلية** … لذلك **لا تكرر
المنظومة مواد الصباح**».

By its own constitution SHRS is a **complementary institute**, not a standalone
school: it deliberately does not teach mathematics, sciences or English, and
depends on another institution to do so. Yet the architecture is presented as a
twelve-grade **school** ladder G1–G12 with certificates at grades 3, 5, 9, 11
and 12.

**A complementary institute cannot award a school-leaving certificate over a
curriculum that omits the core subjects, and no international standard will
recognise one.** The awards are defensible as institute awards in Qur'an,
Arabic and Islamic sciences; they are not defensible as a school ladder.
`[BLACK]` **CR-05** — and the lens records that the grade labels G1–G12
themselves import the claim. This must be settled before any certificate,
prospectus or verification page is produced.

### L9 · Editorial and book architecture

Book count follows the timetable, not the subject register. Under the rejected
7-حصة week G1 supports roughly four books; under the full week, more. With CR-08
unresolved — one التربية الإسلامية or several named subjects — **the G1 title
list cannot be fixed**, and any book commissioned now risks being the wrong book.
The production bar in the Charter §3 applies: no G1 textbook production.

### L10 · Adversarial red-team

Four attacks the architecture does not presently survive:

1. **"Which year is grade 1?"** — answered only in §3 of this session, by
   inference from section alignment. **No document states G1's entry age.** An
   examiner asking it would have received three different answers this month.
2. **"You deleted a stage and kept its gate."** — CR-13 and CR-15 together: the
   preparation was removed, the gate and the examination-free protection were
   not adjusted.
3. **"Your own ledger disagrees with your own ledger."** — §4.4's journey table
   gives الروضة 780 hours for two years, i.e. exactly 600 minutes a week; the
   §4.4 dismissal rule as written yields 610. Thirteen hours across the two
   years. `[RED]` **CR-06.** Small, but it is a document disagreeing with itself
   on the same page, and it is the kind of thing a hostile reader finds first.
4. **"Whose 54-hour week is this?"** — L5. The institution cannot answer a
   child-welfare question about its own pupils.

## 5. SECOND-ORDER REVIEW — the Council on the reviews

**(a) Three lenses inherited one premise.** L2, L6 and L10-attack-2 are all
downstream of the single fact that التأسيس was abolished. They are not three
findings; they are **one structural event with three consequences**. Counting
them as three would overstate the evidence. Counting them as one would
understate the damage — each consequence needs its own remedy.

**(b) L5 and L8 converge from opposite directions**, which strengthens both.
L5 reached "the child has no rest day" from cognitive load; L8 reached
"the institution is complementary, not standalone" from accreditation. **Both
are consequences of §3.1's two-schools model, and §3.1 has been read until now
as a statement of modesty rather than as the constitutional constraint it is.**
It is the most load-bearing sentence in the corpus.

**(c) L4's silence was itself a finding.** The lens found no contact-frequency
data because none exists. The Council records that **an absent number is not a
zero**, and CR-14 stands.

**(d) L1 declined to compute, and was right to.** Estimating G1 memorisation
feasibility against a target that must itself be restated would have
manufactured a figure. The Charter's ignorance rule is satisfied.

**(e) What no lens found.** No lens defended the rejected 7-حصة proposal on its
merits. The Council notes this is weak evidence — the working position was
already stated before the lenses ran, and that ordering may have suppressed a
defence. **A reduced infant week may still be right for reasons other than the
روضة rule**, and L5's 54-hour finding is the strongest argument for one. That
argument is now put on the record *against* the Council's own working position.

constitutional, not arithmetic. It is put to the Director General as **CR-04**,
and the Council states plainly that **Option 1 is the arithmetically
comfortable answer and L5 is the reason to distrust that comfort.**
