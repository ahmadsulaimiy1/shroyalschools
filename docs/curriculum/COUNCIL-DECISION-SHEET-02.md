## 1 · P2-01-h — the G7 transition remedy

**① Question.** Which remedy should SHRS adopt for the already-diagnosed G7
transition problem?

**② Recovered evidence.** ACD-007 Part 1, الانتقالات:
> الحكم: **انتقالان حرجان** · البينة: «جسر السادس محكم؛ **قفزة السابع (١١ مادة
> دفعة) غير ممهدة**» · موضع الإصلاح: «**تمهيد الفصل الأول من السابع**»

With §3.2: التربية الإسلامية **ر–٦**, «الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن يكون علومًا
مسماة»; the six sharʿī sciences bounded **٧–١١**.

**③ Competing remedies.**

| | Remedy |
|---|---|
| **H1** | Retain late naming; **bridge** the eleven-subject transition within Term 1 of G7, as prescribed |
| **H2** | **Dissolve** the transition by moving named subjects progressively downward through G1–G8 |
| **H3** | **Graduated naming**: التربية الإسلامية remains the child-facing umbrella G1–G6 while its internal strands are developed and sequenced G3–G6; formal differentiation at G7; **and the Term-1 G7 bridge is implemented** |

**④ Consequences.**

| | H1 | H2 | H3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| §3.2 principle | retained | **withdrawn** | retained |
| Part 1 remedy | implemented | **discarded** | implemented |
| G1–G6 child-facing subjects | 1 religion subject | 5–6 named sciences | 1 religion subject |
| Primary reporting | 1 mark | 6 marks from G3 | 1 mark + strand annexe |
| Staffing G1–G6 | one class teacher | 5 specialists, or one teacher presenting 5 subjects | one teacher + structured guide |
| G7 transition | real, bridged | none | reduced **and** bridged |
| G8 pressure (Phase 2: +6 claimants) | unchanged | **caused by this option** | unrelieved |
| Matrix impact | ~6 rows collapse for G1–G6 | none | ~6 rows become strand records for G1–G6 |

No option deletes a subject from the institution.

**⑤ Recommended: H3.**

**⑥ Council vote.**
> **APPROVE / REJECT:** *The sharʿī sciences are carried as internally named and
> internally sequenced strands within a single child-facing التربية الإسلامية
> from G1 to G6, reported as one mark with a strand annexe, and become separate
> timetable subjects at grade 7. ACD-007 §3.2's principle «الدين محبةً وعادةً
> قبل أن يكون علومًا مسماة» is retained. Part 1's Term-1 grade-7 bridge is
> implemented.*
>
> *If H2 is approved instead, the Council additionally minutes (i) the
> withdrawal of that principle and (ii) the discarding of Part 1's prescribed
> remedy.*

**⑦ Unlocked if adopted.** P2-01-a becomes evaluable against a settled
architecture · the G1–G6 band of the matrix rebuild · the primary reporting and
certificate model · RR-01's G9 pair acquires a governing principle.

## 2 · P2-01-a — قواعد اللغة الوظيفية

**Not to be decided before H.**

**① Question.** Restore as a distinct subject, integrate under Arabic, or retire
by explicit decision?

**② Recovered evidence.** §3.2: **قواعد اللغة الوظيفية (٤–٦)** · «**القاعدة
تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن**» · outcome «وعي تركيبي عملي **يهيّئ لمتون
الإعدادية**». §2.1: grade ٦ is **معهد اللغة**, «الجسر», gated by بوابة الجسر
بلجنة ثلاثية. Part 1, علوم العربية العليا: «النحو والصرف كانا **مطويين في
«العربية»**» → remedy «**كرسيان مستقلان بمتونهما**».

*The argument "النحو is already at G5, so this subject is unnecessary" is set
aside as circular: النحو sits at G5 because this subject is absent.*

**③ Competing remedies.** **A1** restore as a distinct subject at G4–G6 ·
**A2** named strand inside اللغة العربية · **A3** retire by explicit decision.

**④ Consequences.**

| | A1 | A2 | A3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| «تُلمس قبل أن تُسمى» | preserved | preserved only if the strand stays inductive | **withdrawn** |
| Part 1 precedent | followed | **contradicted** — re-folds what Part 1 unfolded | n/a |
| النحو entry | returns toward ٧ | returns toward ٧ | stays G5 |
| بوابة الجسر at ٦ | has a subject behind it | has a strand behind it | **has nothing distinct behind it** |
| Matrix | 27th subject, G4–G6 only | no new row | no change |
| Staffing | a primary teacher carries it — that is the point | same | G5 needs a النحو teacher |

**⑤ Candidate recommendation: A1 — pending, and conditional on H.** Under H1 or
H3, A1 is near-mandatory. **Under H2, A3 becomes coherent**, because H2 has
already withdrawn the «تُلمس قبل أن تُسمى» family of reasoning.

**⑥ Council vote.**
> **APPROVE / REJECT:** *قواعد اللغة الوظيفية is restored as a distinct subject
> at grades 4–6, carrying «القاعدة تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن»,
> standing behind بوابة الجسر at grade 6; النحو's entry returns toward grade 7.*
>
> *If A3, minute the withdrawal of the principle and state what stands behind
> بوابة الجسر.*

**⑦ Unlocked if adopted.** The Arabic-language architecture end to end —
اللغة العربية → قواعد الوظيفية → النحو → الصرف · the النحو and الصرف entry
grades · بوابة الجسر's content · the Phase 2 §11 text-sequencing findings
(الآجرومية، متن البناء) become resolvable.

> الحكم: «**الثاني عشر خفيف**» · البينة: «بين التدقيق العشري و WAEC **فراغ غير
> مبرمج**» · موضع الإصلاح: «**برمجة خدمة التتويج والتحرير الامتحاني (الجزء
> الثاني) خارج WAEC**»

then **شهادة التخرج (١٢)** · §3.1: G12 is the one year duplicating morning
subjects, for WAEC · Part 7: شهادة التخرج = الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج +

**③ Competing remedies.** **A** reduced terminal year · **B** full teaching year

| Test | A | B | C |

| Phase 2's five G12 demotions | explained | unexplained | explained |
| Staffing G12 | supervisors | ~13 subject teachers | supervisors, examiners, WAEC tutor |

**⑤ Recommended: C** — the only option surviving all tests. Its objection is

**⑥ Council vote.**
