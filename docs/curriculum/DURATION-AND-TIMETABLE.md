**This is confirmed, not contradicted, by the recovered corpus.** ACD-007

**Operational consequence for الفقه and أصول الفقه:** the teaching method is
**الفقه المقارن بالدليل** — the ruling is taught with its evidence, and where
the schools differ the difference is named and its grounds explained. A single
school may be used as the *teaching frame* for a given topic for coherence,
and may differ between topics; no frame is constitutionally binding.

**Consequence for العقيدة and التوحيد:** creed is taught as أهل السنة والجماعة
بالأدلة, without exclusive affiliation to a named kalām school. This is
consistent with the creed booklist directed in an earlier sitting.

## 2. The two design rules

**Rule 1 — the cycle lengthens as the grade rises.**

| Grades | Cycle | Slots in cycle | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1–G9 | **one week** | **19 حصص** | few subjects, seen often |
| G10–G12 | **two weeks** | **38 حصص** | many subjects, each seen properly |

A fortnightly cycle at الثانوية is what makes 23–25 subjects possible **without
shortening the recovered 40-minute حصة.** The alternative — keeping a weekly
cycle — would force periods down to 30 minutes at G11–G12, which cannot carry
ألفية ابن مالك or أصول الفقه. **The period is protected; the cycle flexes.**

**Rule 2 — few subjects × many contacts early; many subjects × few contacts late.**

This is the Chairman's rising-load principle expressed in time: the
total is constant at 19 حصص, and what changes is how it is divided.

| Subjects holding a block | 3 | 7 | 23 |
| Contacts for the busiest | **7/week** | 3/week | 2/fortnight |

## 7. Two-week cycle — how it runs at G10–G12

Weeks are labelled **أ** and **ب**. A subject with 2 حصص/fortnight is taught once
in each week; a subject with 1 is taught in أ or ب only. The Qur'an hour, ʿAṣr,
Ẓuhr, فسحة and مراجعة are identical in both weeks — **only the subject slots
alternate**, so the day's shape never changes for staff or pupils.

corpus. It is introduced to carry the Chairman's rising-load ruling
without breaking the recovered حصة or the operating week, and is recorded as a
new decision rather than presented as recovered.
