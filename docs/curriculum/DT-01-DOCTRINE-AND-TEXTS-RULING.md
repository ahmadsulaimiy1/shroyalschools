# قرار مجلس العقيدة والنصوص — DT-01
## Booklist conformity with the non-exclusivity constitution · ADOPTED, not tabled

---

## 1. The ruling

**A single-school core text may be used as a declared teaching frame
(إطار تعليمي مُعلَن) — never as an affiliation (انتساب), and never the same
school across all sciences.**

The draft booklists are struck **not because they contain Mālikī and Najdi
texts, but because they contain only those, undeclared, with no counterweight
the pupil ever holds in his own hands.**

### Why

**The constitution forbids التزام, not استعمال.** A madhhab is a body of قول
*plus* an act of binding. The constitution removes the binding; it cannot remove
the قول, because فقه does not exist outside somebody's arrangement of it. Using
a matn is pedagogical; professing one is constitutional.

**The counter-argument is granted in full, and it governs the lower school.**
A child of eleven must pray a determinate prayer. **There is no comparative
wuḍūʾ.** A curriculum that hands a G7 pupil three positions on مسح الرأس has
not taught him tolerance — it has taught him the religion has no floor, and it
produces the pupil who resolves every question by asking which answer is
shortest. **«بلا تمييع» forbids that outcome by name.** Determinacy is required
by **صحة العمل**, not by **حجية المذهب** — and that distinction is the whole
ruling.

**The opposite error is also rejected.** A pupil taught rulings from نصوص with
no worked system believes he is deriving when he is following the last thing he
heard. **You cannot see *why* jurists differ if you have never watched one
jurist's system run end to end.** مدارك الخلاف presupposes مَدْرَك. A matn is
not a concession to the constitution — it is a precondition of it.

### The four marks that make a frame a frame

| | Teaching frame | Madhhab affiliation |
|---|---|---|
| Declared? | Named at the head of every scheme of work | Carried in the word «عندنا» |
| Revisable? | **By simple Board minute, no creedal consequence** | Only by constitutional amendment |
| Uniform across sciences? | **No — chosen per باب on عرف, مصلحة, teacher availability** | Yes, by definition |
| Attached to دليل? | Every ruling with its evidence from day one | Ruling taught, evidence assumed |

**The revisability test is the sharp one.** The Board can move the ʿibādāt frame
from Mālikī to Shāfiʿī next year by a one-page minute and nothing in the
school's creed changes. If that were not true, it would be an affiliation.

### The ladder already runs four schools

Mālikī in فروع العبادات · **Shāfiʿī** in أصول الفقه (الورقات), الفرائض
(الرحبية) and مصطلح (نخبة الفكر) · **Ḥanbalī** in the first legal ḥadīth corpus
(عمدة الأحكام) and the first systematic creed (لمعة الاعتقاد) · **Ḥanafī** in
the creedal spine (الطحاوية) and القواعد.

> **A pupil who completes this ladder has been taught by four schools whether or
> not anyone tells him so.** The defect was never that the draft used schools —
> it was that it hid the ones it used and monopolised the one it liked.

### Why the ʿibādāt frame is Mālikī — and on what grounds

**عرف** (his prayer must be continuous with his father's) · **توفر المعلم** (a
frame no one can teach is not a frame) · **صلاحية المتن** (short, versifiable,
confined to العبادات).

**None of the three is a claim that Mālik is more correct. All three are
administrative — and that is to be printed in the scheme of work in those words.**

## 2. Comparative method begins at **G8**, not G11

Moving comparison to G11 produced five monolithic years followed by one year of
comparison. **That does not make a comparativist. It makes a Mālikī who has
heard of others.**

| Stage | Grades | The pupil | May NOT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **طور التمكين** | G6–G7 | One determinate practice; **every ruling with its دليل from the first lesson**; the word مذهب is not used as a badge | — |
| **طور التنبيه** | G8 | Meets the *fact* of خلاف — 4 designated masāʾil/year. **أدب الخلاف before مدارك الخلاف** | Be asked which is correct |
| **طور المقارنة** | G9–G10 | Holds a second and third book **in his own hands**; learns سبب الخلاف | Adjudicate — he **reports** the ʿulamāʾ's ترجيح |
| **طور الاستدلال** | G11–G12 | Theorises خلاف (ابن رشد); may weigh under supervision, **naming whose ترجيح it is** | Issue فتوى; select on grounds of ease |

**The G8 masāʾil are ruled, not left to the teacher:** القبض والسدل · القنوت في
الصبح · الجهر بالبسملة · رفع اليدين · الصلاة خلف المخالف.

> He watches the Tijānī, the Izāla-influenced, the NASFAT congregation and the
> old Ilorin masjid pray differently in the same street. Leaving these unnamed
> does not protect him — **it is precisely what produces the takfīr reflex at
> sixteen, because an unnamed difference is experienced as a deviation.**

### Two prohibited outcomes, named so an inspector can look for them

- **التعصب** — exits able to state one position and regard the rest as error. *This is الغلو.*
- **تتبع الرخص** — exits able to state four positions and select the lightest. *This is التمييع.*

**On متن أبي شجاع, the pivot:** the pupil already *owns* this content from
الأخضرية. A second arrangement of material he has mastered costs almost nothing
to read and teaches everything — **that a book is an arrangement, that
arrangements differ, and that both are Islam.**

**On ابن عاشر, where the earlier impasse is resolved by ruling rather than
avoided:** it is the text the pupil's grandparents learned from. Excluding it
makes the graduate illiterate in his own society's scholarship — **that is غلو.**
Adopting it as core would import a creed through a fiqh door. **So it is read as
a document:** its three strands are identified and named aloud, its fiqh is
usable, its creed goes to العقيدة for descriptive treatment. *That is «بلا غلو
ولا تمييع» executed on a real text.*

**فقه الأسرة:** the school's adopted قول on النكاح بلا ولي، الطلاق الثلاث بلفظ
واحد، حضانة الأم بعد الزواج **must be minuted by the Board, not left to the
teacher** — in Nigeria each is litigated.

## 4. أصول الفقه · G8–G12

**G8–G9 carries no دلالات** — only الأحكام التكليفية الخمسة، الفرق بين الدليل
والتعليل، مراتب الأدلة. دلالات الألفاظ cannot precede قطر الندى competence.

**الورقات** (الجويني, Shāfiʿī) is the core matn at G10–G11, with **نظم الورقات**
(العمريطي) memorised on the المحفوظات channel. **Its selection is itself a proof
of non-exclusivity: the fiqh frame is Mālikī and the uṣūl matn is Shāfiʿī, by
design.**

Teacher spine **علم أصول الفقه** (خلاف); student comparative **الوجيز** (زيدان);
**الأصول من علم الأصول** (ابن عثيمين) retained but **teacher-side** as drafted.
G12: **مقاصد الشريعة** (ابن عاشور) as spine, **الموافقات** (الشاطبي) in two or
three prepared extracts only — *read cold at G12 it turns مقاصد into a licence,
which is تمييع with a scholarly accent.* **شرح القواعد الفقهية** (الزرقا) and
**مجلة الأحكام العدلية، المواد ١–٩٩** — the latter chosen because it shows the
pupil **fiqh written as statute**, the register a Nigerian Sharia Court of Appeal
actually speaks.

**G11 divergence unit (no new text):** الاستحسان · المصالح المرسلة وسد الذرائع ·
عمل أهل المدينة · قول الصحابي · العرف. **This proves the schools differ
*upstream* of the rulings — without it the pupil concludes jurists differ from
carelessness.**

## 5. العقيدة · G7–G12 — the Najdi core problem, ruled plainly

**الأصول الثلاثة — RETAINED AS CORE at G7**, under two conditions: cited by its
author's name aloud, and never the pupil's only text in a year. Its propositions
are disputed by **no** Sunnī school; what is "Najdi" about it is its provenance
and the system it usually arrives inside, not its content.

**كتاب التوحيد — NOT CORE. Demoted to a bounded G11 unit.** The Board's most
consequential reversal, made on **pedagogy, not content**:

> Its structure is not descriptive but **diagnostic** — its أبواب are organised
> around identifying شرك in the practice of nominal Muslims. Handed to a
> fifteen-year-old as his spine, in a society with living ṭarīqa practice,
> tomb-visiting and real syncretic residue, it produces — predictably and
> observably — **a pupil who exits the year able to classify his own
> grandmother.** That outcome is «غلو» by the constitution's own word.

It runs only **after** كف الحكم على المعيّن is established, in selected أبواب,
never run through.

**نواقض الإسلام — REMOVED from G5 entirely**, re-sited to G10 inside
«التكفير: شروطه وموانعه», in which **the موانع are taught first.** *A ten-item
list of ways to leave Islam, handed to a child in a mixed-practice society, is
the single most misusable artefact in the corpus.*

**كشف الشبهات — teacher reference only, at no grade.** It presents the شبهة at
full strength before answering it. *Given to a G11 pupil it plants doubts he did
not have.*

**أم البراهين and جوهرة التوحيد — the "read about, not adopted" marking is
STRUCK.**

### The spine

**العقيدة الطحاوية** — a Ḥanafī author's creed accepted by Ashʿarīs, Māturīdīs,
Atharīs and Salafīs alike. **The only text in the corpus whose adoption as spine
*is itself* a statement of the constitution.** Staged G9 (الإلهيات والنبوات) and
G12 (السمعيات، الصحابة، الإيمان).

| Grade | Text | School |
|---|---|---|
| G7 | **«متن الأدلة»** — school-compiled sheet of creedal نصوص, memorised | **none — this is the point** |
| G7 | الأصول الثلاثة وأدلتها | Najdi/Athari |
| G8 | القواعد الأربع (مضمّن) · **لمعة الاعتقاد** — *declared as such aloud* | Najdi · **Ḥanbalī/Athari** |
| G9 | **العقيدة الطحاوية** — الإلهيات والنبوات | Ḥanafī author, broadest consensus |
| **G10** | **أم البراهين (السنوسية)** *and* **العقيدة الواسطية** — **same year, same term, same teacher, both declared** | **Ashʿarī** · **Athari** |
| G10 | نواقض الإسلام, inside «التكفير: شروطه وموانعه» | Najdi |
| G11 | **الاعتصام** (الشاطبي) — **البدعة taught from a Mālikī, deliberately** | Mālikī |
| G11 | كتاب التوحيد — bounded unit, selected أبواب, after the above | Najdi |
| G11–G12 | **كتاب الفِرَق** — school-authored, each group described **in the terms its own adherents use**: Qādiriyyah, Tijāniyyah, Izāla, NASFAT, Ahmadiyyah, Ansar-ud-Deen | descriptive |
| G12 | الطحاوية completed | — |

**On the G7 memorised creed:** *There is no school-neutral creedal منظومة in
existence.* عقيدة العوام and جوهرة التوحيد are Ashʿarī; سلم الوصول is Salafī. To
memorise any of them at G7 is **to adopt a kalām school at age twelve by
default.** The only honest first memorised creed for this institution is **the
نصوص themselves** — which is what «أهل السنة بالأدلة» literally says.

**On the G10 pairing:** two arrangements of الأسماء والصفات, both declared, both
with their own evidence, taught by the same teacher in the same term. **This
single pairing is the constitution made operational in العقيدة.**

### The G12 علم الكلام unit — resourced documentarily, never systematically

الإبانة (الأشعري, one extract — *and the teacher states that its recensions are
disputed, which is itself a lesson in نقد النص*) · مقالات الإسلاميين (الأشعري —
a Sunnī heresiography that **reports before it judges**; the model for the unit's
posture) · كتاب التوحيد (الماتريدي — **the title clash with the Najdi work is
taught deliberately**: two books, one title, four centuries and one continent
apart) · إلجام العوام (الغزالي — a master of kalām on the *limits* of kalām) ·
تحريم النظر في كتب الكلام (ابن قدامة — the opposing position, by its own holder,
**at equal length**) · درء التعارض (ابن تيمية, the مقدمة's statement of the
problem only) · جوهرة التوحيد **read as a document** — *the graduate must be able
to read the poem his Ibadan teachers memorised.*

> **Assessment rule, printed on the paper:** «اذكر قول الأشاعرة في كلام الله
> وقول أهل الأثر، كلًّا بألفاظ أهله، واذكر ثلاثة أمور اتفقا عليها.»
> **The pupil is never asked to adjudicate, and a paper that asks him to is a
> defect against the examiner.** That, not a disclaimer in a preface, is what
> "study about, not study of" means.

## 6. الحديث النبوي and مصطلح الحديث

**الحديث:** الأربعون النووية + زيادات ابن رجب (G7) · **عمدة الأحكام** (G8–G9 —
restricted to what البخاري and مسلم agree on, *so the authenticity question is
settled by the selection criterion itself*) · الأدب المفرد (G9) · **الموطأ (G10
— added; a real gap)** · بلوغ المرام (G10–G11) · الشمائل (G11) · البخاري ومسلم
in the original (G12) · **جامع العلوم والحكم** (G12 capstone — a full classical
شرح on the fifty ḥadīths he has held since G5) · شرح معاني الآثار (الطحاوي,
teacher reference — *without it the pupil silently concludes the Ḥanafīs had no
ḥadīth*).

**On الموطأ:** the school teaches a Mālikī fiqh frame and never once put الموطأ
in the pupil's hand. Read beside بلوغ المرام it shows **عمل أهل المدينة
functioning as a source alongside رواية** — the clearest demonstration available
of why two schools reading the same ḥadīth reach different rulings.

**مصطلح:** embedded vocabulary only at G8 (no matn) · **البيقونية** memorised
G9 · **نخبة الفكر** + the author's own **نزهة النظر** G10–G11 · تخريج practicum
on بلوغ المرام G11 · **الرفع والتكميل** (اللكنوي — chosen twice over: a
non-Shāfiʿī voice in جرح وتعديل, and *a work about excess in criticism — the
discipline's own statement of «بلا غلو»*) · G12: **the مقدمات of the الكتب
الستة themselves**, so the last thing he does is watch a muḥaddith state his own
method in his own voice.

**Ruled negatively:** *there is no classical matn for G1–G4 in this discipline.*
G3–G4 use a school-compiled selection. **Inventing an attribution to fill a
column is a scholarly failure, not a convenience.**

## 7. «مِعيار عدم الحصر» — the operational conformity test

**Purpose: to make "no exclusive school" fail loudly rather than degrade quietly
into "one school by default, unminuted."** Every clause is presence/absence or
arithmetic. None requires the inspector to hold an opinion about fiqh.

**C-1 · Frame Declaration.** Every scheme of work in الفقه، أصول الفقه، العقيدة
carries at its head: «الإطار التعليمي لهذا الباب: [school] · المتن: [title] ·
الدليل المعتمد لكل مسألة: مرفق». **A scheme without this line is not approved
and the class does not run.**

**C-2 · Two-Frames Rule.** From G8, no باب may be examined unless one مسألة in it
has been taught from two or more schools and entered in the **سجل مدارك الخلاف**
— a bound, numbered, non-detachable book. Quota: **4 masāʾil/year at G8–G9, 6 at
G10–G12.** Its eighth column records **the teacher's ترجيح with the name of the
scholar it belongs to** — *a ترجيح with no owner is the teacher legislating, and
it is a reportable fault.*

**C-3 · Counting Rule.** Across G7–G9 and across G10–G12 the register must show
**all four schools at least twice and no single school above 50%.** *This stops
"two frames" collapsing into Mālikī versus Mālikī-with-a-footnote. **An inspector
counts it in ten minutes without knowing any fiqh.***

**C-4 · Examination Clause.** From G10, every terminal fiqh paper carries a
compulsory «اذكر قولين، ودليل كل قول، وسبب الخلاف» question at **not less than
15%**, and **an answer naming only one school's position cannot exceed half marks
even when that position is correct.**

> **This clause does more work than the other six combined. Declarations change
> documents; mark schemes change teaching.**

**C-5 · Anti-تمييع Clause.** At least two entries a year in which the school
taught a ruling that is **not the lightest available قول**, with the reason
minuted. *Without this, "comparative" is indistinguishable from تتبع الرخص after
three years.* And: a pupil below G11 recorded as having *chosen* between schools
is **a defect against the teacher, not the pupil**.

**C-6 · Silent-Default Alarm.** «هل استُعمل لفظ «المذهب» أو «عندنا» لتقرير حكم
بلا تسمية المذهب؟» Three recorded instances against one teacher triggers
mandatory retraining. *This is how a frame becomes an affiliation — and it
happens in speech, not in documents, so it must be policed in speech.*

**C-7 · Who checks.** Termly: the head of department signs the register.
**Annually: an external مُراجع who is not on staff and _not of the frame
school_.** Every three years: full re-ratification, and **a booklist returning
unchanged for three consecutive cycles is itself a recorded finding requiring
explanation.**

> **The external reviewer's non-affiliation is the load-bearing clause of the
> whole test. An internal reviewer trained in the frame school cannot see the
> frame; it is the water he swims in. The check must be adversarial by
> construction, or it is decorative.**

## 9. الفرائض — confirmed and improved in four respects

**(1) It is not a وحدة.** A وحدة sharing a mark line with الفقه is the thing cut
when the term runs short — *and this science has already been compressed to a
passing arithmetic note once.* **It is a ring-fenced named باب with its own
examination and its own line on the certificate**, G11 theory + G12 application.

**(2) The arithmetic prerequisite is stated, not assumed.** The Mathematics
department must certify, **before** the G11 unit runs, that the cohort holds LCM,
equivalent fractions and common-denominator addition **in both the Arabic and
English notations the school uses.**

> **الرحبية does not fail in schools for fiqh reasons. It fails for arithmetic
> ones — and in a bilingual school it fails at the notation boundary.**

**(3) Text:** **متن الرحبية** (الرحبي, Shāfiʿī tradition) memorised at G11, with
**شرح سبط المارديني** as teacher reference — *verify the printed edition; several
شروح circulate under similar titles.* **That the farāʾiḍ frame is Shāfiʿī while
the fiqh frame is Mālikī is deliberate and must be minuted.**

Four divergences must be taught as named differences and the school's applied
position minuted:

| Masʾalah | Divergence |
|---|---|
| **ذوو الأرحام والرد** | Ḥanafīs and Ḥanbalīs give the residue to ذوو الأرحام / apply الرد; the classical Mālikī and Shāfiʿī mashhūr assigns it to **بيت المال** — with later authorities of both permitting الرد **when بيت المال is not منتظم**. **There is no بيت المال in Nigeria. The Board must minute the applied rule. This single question decides real distributions every week.** |
| الجد مع الإخوة | Abū Ḥanīfah excludes the brothers; the other three apply المقاسمة |
| المشرَّكة (الحِمارية) | Mālikīs and Shāfiʿīs share; Ḥanafīs and Ḥanbalīs do not |
| الرد على أحد الزوجين | Excluded in the mashhūr of all four |

**(4) The G12 applied block — «الفرائض التطبيقية والقضاء في السياق النيجيري»**,
one term, no classical matn: real anonymised estates worked to a distribution
sheet a family could take to court · the Sharia Court of Appeal of a State under
the 1999 Constitution · **the Marriage Act problem** — a statutory monogamous
marriage and the English-derived administration route that can displace the
Qurʾānic shares, *the single most consequential live issue for a Muslim family in
Lagos or Ibadan, and most graduates of most Nigerian madāris have never heard of
it* · **Yorùbá customary succession — ìdí-ìgì and orí-ọjọ́rí** set against the

neither the classical matn nor the statute names* · drafting a وصية within the
third · مجلة الأحكام العدلية as fiqh written as enforceable statute.

## 11. Declared uncertainties — stated rather than guessed

Every text named as core or comparative specimen above is one the Board is
confident exists under the stated title and author. These carry qualification:

1. **شرح سبط المارديني على الرحبية** — commentary and author certain; **printed
   title form varies.** Verify the copy.
2. **الفوائد الشنشورية** — believed to exist; **title-author pairing not
   certified.** Not built into the architecture.
3. **شرح العقيدة الطحاوية** attributed to **ابن أبي العز** — the attribution is
   standard but **questioned in modern literature, and its register is polemical
   against the Ashʿarīs.** If used: teacher reference, character declared, and
   **at least one non-Atharī شرح held alongside** — the Board does not certify a
   specific alternative from memory.
4. **الخريدة البهية** (الدردير) — believed to exist. **Not placed.**
5. **التجريد الصريح** — real; **compiler's full name not certified here.**
6. **الإبانة** (الأشعري) — real; **recensions disputed.** One extract, with that
   fact stated.
7. **«منظومة التوحيد والإيمان»**, appearing at G3 in earlier drafts — **the Board
   cannot identify a classical matn under this title and does not guess.** It may
   be a locally circulating poem, which would be legitimate — **but it must not be
   entered in the register as a classical matn.**
8. **Nigerian statutory citations** — **substance certified, section numbers
   NOT.** Verified by counsel before any pupil sees them.
9. **Nigerian textbooks on Islamic succession** — the Board declines to name one
   from recollection.
10. **ألفية العراقي** — real; outside the ladder either way.
