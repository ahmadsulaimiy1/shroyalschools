## المجموعة (ب) — أسئلة متوسطة · أربعة أسئلة · كلها إلزامية · [٢٢ درجة]

**١١.** سافر **خالد** في رمضان سفرًا طويلًا فأفطر **يومين**، ثم رجع فمرض مرضًا يُرجى

&nbsp;&nbsp;**(ب)** ذُكرت في زيادة الثمن لأجل التأجيل **مقالتان لأهل العلم**.

**١٤.** &nbsp;**(أ)** اذكر **معنى الصداق**، ومتى يجب كاملًا للزوجة. &nbsp;**[٢]**
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(ب)** لِمَ شُرِعت **العدة**؟ اذكر حكمتين. &nbsp;**[٢]**
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(ج)** طلَّق رجلٌ زوجتَه طلقةً واحدةً رجعيةً، ثم أراد مراجعتها **بعد

# PART II — سلم التصحيح · MARK SCHEME

## Marking conventions — binding on every marker

1. **لا أنصاف درجات.** No half marks anywhere on this paper.
2. **Positive marking.** Credit what is there. A candidate who gives four correct
   points where two were asked scores the maximum, not a bonus, and is not penalised
   for length.
3. **الخطأ المتعدي لا يُعاقب مرتين — error carried forward.** In question 15, a
   candidate whose share identification in (ب) is wrong but who then divides *his own
   shares* correctly in (ج) **receives the (ج) method marks in full**. The error is
   charged once.
4. **الأقوال المعتبرة كلها مقبولة.** Wherever a ruling is genuinely disputed among
   the schools of أهل السنة, **any recognised position is fully credited**, provided
   the candidate attaches evidence or names a holder. Marks are for reasoning and
   attribution, never for matching the setter's madhhab.
5. **Quotation of نصوص.** A نص correct in meaning but imperfect in wording receives
   the mark; a نص attributed to the wrong source does not. **A candidate is never
   penalised twice for the same misquotation.**
6. Every mark below is labelled with its **domain (د)** and its **AO (هـ)**. A marker
   who cannot locate a mark he has awarded in this scheme has not awarded it.
7. **Language.** Grammatical error is not penalised unless it changes the meaning. If
   it changes the meaning, the answer is marked on the meaning actually written.
8. **سقف النصف — THE HALF-MARK CAP (`SPEC-GRID` §3a).**
   > **On group (د) — and on group (د) only — an answer naming only ONE school's
   > position may not exceed 8 of 16, even where that position is correct.**

   The cap is applied **as a ceiling, after totalling**, never by deducting from
   correct content. The marker writes **«سقف §3a»** beside the total wherever it
   bites, so that moderation and any appeal can see that the ceiling was applied
   rather than that the content was under-marked. The trigger table at `SPEC-GRID`
   §3a is binding; a marker unsure whether the cap applies **refers rather than
   guesses**, because inconsistent capping is the fastest way to lose an appeal.

## What the three scripts establish, stated for the standardisation meeting

| Boundary | The single discriminator |
|---|---|
| **٨٠ — امتياز** | **Names holders, adduces a دليل for each, and locates the سبب in a specific word or text.** Not "knows more" |
| **٧٠ — جدارة** | Names holders and one دليل; narrates the disagreement without locating it |
| **٦٠ — إجادة** | Two positions stated but unattributed; case-work right on familiar patterns |
| **٥٠ — مقبول** | **Rulings held, reasons not held.** The فرائض money right without the مقام |
| **below 50** | **Knowledge that cannot be put on a case.** Recall may even be above a passing candidate's |
| **§3a cap** | **cuts across all five bands.** A one-school answer is capped at 8/16 whatever its quality, and that is an 8-mark swing on a 100-mark paper |

**The two commonest marking errors on this paper, to be stated aloud at
standardisation:**

1. **Over-rewarding group (أ).** Recall is 25 marks and no more. A marker who is
   generous in (أ) and mean in (د) will pass candidate 9152 and fail candidate 9117,
   which is the precise inversion of what this paper was built to measure.
2. **Mis-applying §3a.** The cap is triggered by **the absence of a second position**,
   not by weak attribution, not by a preferred position, and not by a wrong second
   position. It is applied **as a ceiling after totalling**, it is **annotated on the
   script**, and the uncapped total is recorded beside it. Every capped script goes
   into the stage‑4 moderation sample **without exception**, because this is a new
   rule in its first cycle and its consistency is not yet established.
