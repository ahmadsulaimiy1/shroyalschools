# QUR'AN EXAMINATION PROTOCOL — بروتوكول الامتحان القرآني
## بوابة القرآن (٨/١٠) بالصف التاسع · والثانوية القرآنية بالصف الحادي عشر
## Sultan Royal Schools / GACAIS — لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية · v1.0

> **This is the institution's highest-stakes and most challengeable instrument.**
> It is live, oral, unrepeatable, judged by ear, and it gates two certificates. It
> therefore carries heavier controls than any written paper the institution sets:
> two independent examiners on every candidate, an audio record retained for years,
> a published error taxonomy with published penalties, and an appeal route that can
> reach the recording.
>
> **A gate nobody can challenge is not rigorous; it is merely unaccountable.**

**`[EXECUTIVE COUNCIL RULING]`** The G11 gate certifies **TWELVE juz متقنة**, not
fifteen. Fifteen is **مرتبة الامتياز**, recorded on the same certificate. This

## 0.1 What these instruments are NOT — stated first, because each confusion is live

1. **The gate is not the حفظ subject mark.** حفظ القرآن الكريم is a `●` مستقل
   subject carrying its own **Section A** line, assessed 30/70 like any other, and
   reporting **progress in acquisition**. The gate reports **security of what is
   held**. *A candidate can hold a fine Section A mark and fail the gate.* This is not
   a contradiction, and `ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` Part 9 requires it to be explained to
   parents in those words.
2. **8/10 is not a mark.** It is a **threshold measure** (`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md`
   §6.3). 10/10 buys nothing that 8/10 does not. The surplus is recorded in the
   candidate's Qur'an record and has **no certificate value whatever**.
3. **The gate is not a quantity test.** Neither instrument asks «how much have you
   memorised». Both ask «**is what you claim to hold, secure?**» Quantity is reported
   separately and honestly, in juz (Part 5.4).
4. **«مُجاز بلجنة» is not إجازة بالسند.** These are two different things and the
   institution confers only one of them. Part 7 states exactly what each means and
   what the institution is committing itself to.
5. **The G9 gate is not a رواية examination.** أصول رواية حفص عن عاصم enters التجويد
   at G9 (`MASTER-ALLOCATION-MATRIX-v4.md`, missing-content table) and is examined
   **inside the G9 التجويد assessment**, not at this gate. The gate examines
   **performance**, not doctrine.

# PART 1 — THE PANEL — لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية

## 1.1 Constitution

| Role | Number | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **رئيس اللجنة** | 1 | chairs, does not mark, rules on procedure during the sitting, signs the record |
| **الممتحن الأول** | 1 | marks independently |
| **الممتحن الثاني** | 1 | marks independently, **without sight of the first examiner's sheet** |
| **الضابط** (recorder) | 1 | operates the recording, draws the lots, keeps the clock, **does not mark and does not comment** |

**Four people. The candidate faces two markers, not a room.** At G11 the chair must
be a **مُقرئ مُجاز بسندٍ متصل** (Part 7.3).

## 1.2 Examiner qualification — شروط الممتحن

**A person may not mark at either gate unless he satisfies ALL of the following:**

| # | Requirement | G9 | G11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ١ | Holds **not less than the portion being examined**, secure, himself | **≥ 13 juz** | **الختمة كاملة** |
| ٢ | Holds **تحفة الأطفال and المقدمة الجزرية** from memory and can adduce their lines as evidence for a ruling | required | required |
| ٣ | Certified at this institution's **إتقان التلاوة** standard (the G10 terminal practical) **or** an equivalent external certification | required | required |
| ٤ | **إجازة بسندٍ متصل** in حفص عن عاصم | desirable | **required of the chair; desirable of the two markers** |
| ٥ | Completed this protocol's **examiner training** (§1.3) within the preceding **24 months** | required | required |
| ٦ | Achieved **agreement within tolerance on the standardisation recordings** in the current cycle (§1.3) | required | required |
| ٧ | **No conflict:** did not teach this candidate's حلقة in the examined year; is not a relative; has no private tuition relationship with the family | **absolute** | **absolute** |

**On requirement 7 in a small institution.** The corpus records that G9 pupils
themselves take supervised تسميع from G1–G2 children, and that G12 pupils run
حلقات — teaching relationships are dense here by design. Where **no** unconflicted
examiner exists for a candidate, the institution **buys one in** from outside for that
candidate. It does **not** waive requirement 7, and it does **not** let the chair mark
"to make up the numbers". **The cost of an external examiner is the price of the gate
being worth anything.**

## 1.3 Examiner training and standardisation — التدريب والمعايرة

**Annual, mandatory, before any candidate is heard.** No certificate without it.

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| **١ · التأصيل** | this protocol read in full; the taxonomy (Part 3) rehearsed term by term, with each examiner producing an example of every sub-type from his own recitation |
| **٢ · التمييز** | **the جلي/خفي boundary drilled on live examples until agreement is reached.** This is the single most consequential judgement in the instrument and the commonest source of examiner disagreement |
| **٣ · المعايرة** | **six standardisation recordings**, pre-marked by the Board, spanning the bands. An examiner whose total is more than **0.5** from the agreed score on any recording, or who mis-classifies a جلي as خفي or the reverse on any recording, **is re-trained and re-tested before he marks** |
| **٤ · الأدب** | conducting the sitting: how to give a cue, how to prompt, how to stop, how to sit with a candidate who breaks down, what silence means and what it does not |
| **٥ · المحظورات** | what an examiner may never do: teach during the sitting, correct aloud, sigh, frown at an error, compare candidates in the room, discuss a candidate outside the panel |

**A record of each examiner's standardisation performance is kept for 5 years and is
disclosable on appeal.** An appellant is entitled to know whether the people who
heard him were within tolerance that year.

# PART 2 — WHAT IS SAMPLED, AND HOW

## 2.1 The declared corpus — المحفوظ المصرَّح به

Neither gate examines "the Qur'an". Each examines a **declared corpus**.

1. **Six weeks before the sitting**, the candidate's حلقة teacher lodges a
   **بيان المحفوظ** — a signed statement, juz by juz, of what the candidate holds.
2. The candidate **signs it himself**. A candidate who believes the statement
   overstates or understates him says so **then**, in writing, and the حلقة record is
   reviewed by the chair.
3. **The declared corpus is frozen at lodgement.** Nothing is added or removed in the
   six weeks, and the panel examines **only** from it.
4. **The declaration is the candidate's own act and it carries a consequence.** A
   candidate who declares thirteen juz is sampled across thirteen. A candidate who
   declares eight is sampled across eight — and **is not penalised in the gate score
   for declaring eight**, because the gate measures security, not quantity. The juz
   count travels separately (Part 5.4).

**This is the design decision that makes the whole instrument defensible.** Sampling
an undeclared corpus tests the teacher's optimism. Sampling a declared one tests the
candidate, and lets him be certified honestly for what he actually holds.

## 2.2 Sampling — G9 بوابة القرآن

| | |
|---|---|
| **عدد المواضع** | **ثمانية مواضع** (8 prompts) |
| **الطول** | **ten آيات or approximately half a page each**, whichever the موضع naturally yields; the chair may extend by up to three آيات to reach a natural stop |
| **من أين** | **من المحفوظ المصرَّح به وحده** |
| **كيف تُختار** | **بالقرعة أمام المرشَّح** — by lot, in front of the candidate, from a sealed set |
| **التوزيع** | the declared corpus is divided into **eight equal bands by page count**, and **one موضع is drawn from each band**. A candidate cannot be sampled eight times from one juz, and cannot avoid any part of what he declared |
| **بداية باردة** | **all eight are cold starts** — the cue is the موضع's own opening, or a stated reference, **never the preceding line** |
| **موضعان منها** | **two of the eight are «صِل الآية»** — the examiner recites a line from the middle of the موضع and the candidate continues. This is what distinguishes retrieval from serial recitation |
| **الزمن** | **20–25 minutes** per candidate |

## 2.3 Sampling — G11 الثانوية القرآنية

| | |
|---|---|
| **عدد المواضع** | **اثنا عشر موضعًا** — one drawn from each of the twelve certified juz |
| | **+ ثلاثة مواضع إضافية** for a candidate presenting for **مرتبة الامتياز** at fifteen juz — drawn from juz 13, 14 and 15 |
| **الطول** | **صفحة كاملة** each |
| **السرد المتصل** | **plus one continuous سرد of not less than TWO pages**, drawn by lot, unbroken, to test stamina and breath planning — a thing eight short prompts cannot test |
| **التلاوة من المصحف** | **plus ONE unseen page, sight-read from a مصحف**, to certify applied tajwīd independently of memory. Every stop taken and every stop refused must be **defensible on request** (the G8 وحدة الوقف والابتداء is examined here) |
| **الاختيار** | by lot, in front of the candidate, one per juz; no juz omitted, no juz doubled |
| **الزمن** | **45–55 minutes** per candidate, in **two sittings on different days** — one for the twelve مواضع, one for the سرد and the sight-read |

**Why two sittings at G11.** A single hour-long sitting measures endurance under
examination stress as much as it measures إتقان, and the certificate does not claim
endurance. Splitting also means an illness or a bad day costs a candidate one sitting,
not a certificate.

## 2.4 Conduct of the sitting — إدارة الجلسة

- The candidate is **seated**, given **water**, and told the شكل of the sitting before
  it begins: how many مواضع, how they are drawn, what a prompt means.
- **The lot is drawn in front of him and read aloud by the ضابط.**
- **الاستفتاح:** الاستعاذة والبسملة where the موضع requires it. An omission here is
  **noted, not penalised**, unless the candidate is told and repeats it.
- **التلقين — prompting.** On a stall, the examiner waits **five seconds in silence**,
  then gives **one prompt** (the first word). If the candidate does not resume within
  a further five seconds, the موضع is **closed** and scored on what was recited.
  **Maximum two prompts per موضع.** A third stall closes the موضع.
- **No correction, no instruction, no commentary, no facial signalling during the
  sitting.** The examiner's face is as much part of the instrument as his ear.
- **Self-correction — الرجوع.** A candidate who corrects himself **immediately and
  unprompted, before proceeding past the word**, is **not penalised**. A candidate
  who corrects after proceeding is penalised **once**, at the reduced rate (Part 3.4).
  *Rationale:* self-monitoring for لحن خفي without prompting is itself the G9
  التجويد objective; penalising it would punish the competence being certified.
- **The recording runs from before the first lot to after the last word**, and the
  candidate is told it is running.
- **Breaks.** The candidate may request a pause at any point. The clock stops. **No
  inference of any kind is drawn from a pause**, and examiners are told so.

# PART 3 — THE ERROR TAXONOMY AND ITS PENALTIES

## 3.1 The governing distinction

> **اللحن الجليّ** — an error that **breaks the word or the meaning**: it changes a
> letter, a vowel-with-grammatical-effect, or the text itself. **It is an error every
> competent listener hears as wrong, and it is an error in the Qur'an, not merely in
> the recitation of it.**
>
> **اللحن الخفيّ** — an error that **leaves the word intact but breaks the
> discipline**: a rule of tajwīd is misapplied, a مد is mis-measured, a صفة is lost.
> **It is an error the trained ear hears and the untrained ear does not.**

**Zero لحن جلي is the pass condition at both gates.** This is `[RECOVERED]` from the
institution's own mastery threshold — «a juz is counted as محفوظ only if recitable
with ≤٢ أخطاء خفيّة per page and **zero لحن جليّ**, from a cold random cue» — and it
is preserved exactly.

## 3.2 Taxonomy of اللحن الجليّ

| Code | Sub-type | Definition | Worked example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ج١** | **إبدال حرف** | a letter replaced by another, or a مخرج so displaced that a different letter is produced | «الصراط» ← «السراط» when الصاد is intended · ضاد ← ظاء · قاف ← كاف |
| **ج٢** | **زيادة أو نقص** | a letter, word or phrase added or dropped | dropping «مِن» · adding «وَ» · omitting a word of the آية |
| **ج٣** | **تغيير حركة** | a vowel changed such that إعراب or بناء changes | «أَنعَمْتَ» ← «أَنعَمْتُ» · «اللهَ» ← «اللهُ» in ﴿إِنَّمَا يَخْشَى اللَّهَ مِنْ عِبَادِهِ الْعُلَمَاءُ﴾ |
| **ج٤** | **تبديل كلمة** | a word replaced by another, whether or not the sense survives | «غفور رحيم» ← «عزيز حكيم» at a موضع where the text reads otherwise |
| **ج٥** | **تركيب آية على آية** | jumping from one آية to a similar one elsewhere (**انتقال المتشابهات**) and continuing, so that a text is produced that is not in that موضع | crossing between the parallel آيات of الشعراء or of البقرة/آل عمران |
| **ج٦** | **الوقف أو الابتداء القبيح المُحيل للمعنى** | stopping or starting at a place that yields a meaning the آية does not bear | stopping on ﴿فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ﴾ · starting at ﴿وَلَا تَقْرَبُوا الصَّلَاةَ﴾ |

> **ج٦ is the boundary case, and it is classified جلي deliberately.** A قبيح stop is
> not a technical imperfection; it produces a false statement about what God said.
> A stop that is merely **not ideal** — a وقف حسن where a وقف كافٍ was available — is
> **خفي (خ٦)**, not جلي. **Examiners are drilled on this distinction at training
> stage 2 and it is the commonest source of disagreement between markers.**

## 3.3 Taxonomy of اللحن الخفيّ

| Code | Sub-type | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **خ١** | **الغنة** | ghunnah omitted, shortened, lengthened, or produced where it does not belong | النون المشددة والميم المشددة · الإخفاء |
| **خ٢** | **مقادير المد** | a مد short or long by one حركة or more, or inconsistent across a tempo change | المد الطبيعي ≠ 2 · المتصل/المنفصل inconsistent · العارض للسكون wandering |
| **خ٣** | **أحكام النون والميم** | إظهار / إدغام / إقلاب / إخفاء misapplied | إظهار where إخفاء is due · إدغام بغنة where بغير غنة is due |
| **خ٤** | **التفخيم والترقيق والقلقلة** | اللام and الراء mis-weighted; قلقلة absent, excessive, or vowel-coloured | ترقيق of a mufakhkham راء · قلقلة rendered as a full vowel |
| **خ٥** | **الصفات والمخارج الدقيقة** | a صفة weakened — همس، شدة، استعلاء، إطباق — **without producing a different letter** | a soft طاء still recognisably طاء · a slack همس on التاء |
| **خ٦** | **الوقف والابتداء غير المُحيل** | a stop of a lower grade than the place afforded, or a breath taken at a poor but not meaning-changing place | stopping at a وقف حسن mid-clause where كافٍ was one word away |
| **خ٧** | **اضطراب الأداء** | hesitation, repetition of a word to steady oneself, unstable tempo, breath taken **inside a word**, audible ترجيع | |

**Breathing inside a word (خ٧) is a single, defined offence** and is not double-scored
as a وقف قبيح, unless the resumption itself produces a meaning the آية does not bear
— in which case it is **ج٦**, scored once, as جلي.

## 3.4 Penalties — سلم الخصم

Both gates are scored **out of 10.00**, to two decimal places, and **rounded once at
the end** to one decimal place.

| Class | Deduction per instance |
|---|---|
| **لحن جليّ — any of ج١–ج٦** | **−1.00** each, **AND the automatic-fail rule below** |
| **لحن خفيّ — any of خ١–خ٧** | **−0.20** each |
| **خفيّ مُصحَّح متأخرًا** (self-corrected after proceeding past the word) | **−0.10** |
| **خفيّ مُصحَّح فورًا** (before proceeding past the word) | **0.00 — not penalised** |
| **تلقين — prompt given** | **−0.30** per prompt |
| **موضع مغلق** — closed after two prompts / third stall | **−1.00** for the موضع, and it is **not** additionally charged for its uncommitted errors |
| **ترك الاستعاذة أو البسملة** where due, after notice | **−0.10**, once only in the sitting |

### The three governing rules that sit above the arithmetic

> **القاعدة الأولى — شرط الجليّ.**
> **ANY لحن جليّ that is not self-corrected immediately fails the gate, whatever the
> arithmetic says.** A candidate with one uncorrected ج-class error is recorded
> **غير مستوفٍ** even on a computed score of 9.2. `[RECOVERED]` from the institution's
> own «zero لحن جليّ» threshold.
> **A لحن جلي corrected immediately and unprompted is recorded as ج، مُصحَّح، and
> penalised −0.30, and does not trigger this rule.** A candidate who hears his own
> gross error and repairs it before moving on has demonstrated precisely the
> self-monitoring the certificate asserts.

> **القاعدة الثانية — سقف الخفيّ لكل موضع.**
> **No single موضع may lose more than 1.00 for خفي errors.** Without this cap one bad
> page — a cracked voice, a cough, a moment of panic — swamps a sitting. The cap
> converts the instrument from *error-counting* into *sampling*, which is what it is.

> **القاعدة الثالثة — لا يُحسب الخطأ مرتين.**
> One physical act produces one entry. A mis-measured مد at a وقف is **خ٢** or
> **خ٦**, never both; the examiner records the **more serious** category and moves
> on. **Double-charging is the commonest marking error at this gate and is checked at
> reconciliation (Part 6.3).**

**The sheet is the evidence.** A deduction with no line on the sheet does not exist,
and is removed at reconciliation. **«وصف بعبارة الممتحن» is mandatory**: a code
without a description cannot be reviewed on appeal and is struck.

# PART 4 — THE MARK SCHEME — سلم الدرجات

## 4.1 G9 — بوابة القرآن — out of 10, gate at 8.0

The 10 points are **not** ten equal marks; they are four weighted components, each
reduced by the deductions attributable to it.

| Component | Weight | What it measures | Deductions charged to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **١ · الحفظ والاسترجاع** | **٤٫٠** | text produced correctly and retrievably from a cold cue, including the two صِل الآية prompts | ج١–ج٥ · prompts · closed مواضع |
| **٢ · التجويد التطبيقي** | **٣٫٠** | أحكام realised in performance, not recited as theory | خ١–خ٥ |
| **٣ · الوقف والابتداء** | **١٫٥** | stops and starts placed and defensible | ج٦ · خ٦ |
| **٤ · الأداء والطلاقة** | **١٫٥** | tempo held, breath planned, غنة duration stable, no hesitation-driven disruption | خ٧ · tempo instability |
| | **١٠٫٠** | | |

**A component cannot go below zero**; deductions exceeding a component's weight stop
at zero and **do not spill into another component**.

**الحاصل = ١٠٫٠ − مجموع الخصومات المحمولة على كل مكوّن، بحدّ أدنى صفر لكل مكوّن.**

| Result | Recorded |
|---|---|
| **≥ 8.0** and **zero uncorrected لحن جلي** | **مستوفٍ — بوابة القرآن** |
| **≥ 8.0** but an uncorrected لحن جلي | **غير مستوفٍ** — القاعدة الأولى — with the specific موضع and code recorded |
| **< 8.0** | **غير مستوفٍ** |

**No grade, no band, no تقدير is attached.** The gate is met or not met
(`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §6.3). The numeral is recorded in the candidate's Qur'an
record and printed on the transcript **labelled «قياس عتبة لا درجة مُدرَّجة»**.

## 4.2 G11 — الثانوية القرآنية

Scored on the **same 10-point scheme**, with two additions and a different
threshold structure.

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| ١ · الحفظ والاسترجاع (12 مواضع) | **٣٫٥** |
| ٢ · التجويد التطبيقي | **٢٫٥** |
| ٣ · الوقف والابتداء — including defending stops on the **sight-read page** | **١٫٥** |
| ٤ · الأداء والطلاقة — including the **سرد المتصل** | **١٫٥** |
| **٥ · التلاوة من المصحف** (unseen page) | **١٫٠** |
| | **١٠٫٠** |

**Thresholds at G11 — conjunctive, all four required:**

1. **الحاصل ≥ 8.5**;
2. **zero uncorrected لحن جلي** across the whole examination;
3. **per-juz condition:** **each** of the twelve juz, taken from its own موضع, at
   **≤ 2 أخطاء خفيّة for that موضع** and **zero جلي** — `[RECOVERED]` from the
   institution's own mastery threshold. **A candidate at 8.7 overall with one juz
   carrying four خفي errors has not secured twelve juz; he has secured eleven.** In
   that case **the juz is named** and he is offered the re-sit of Part 8 on that juz
   alone;
4. **zero لحن جلي on the sight-read page**, which is the applied-tajwīd condition.

**مرتبة الامتياز — fifteen juz.** Awarded where, **in addition to all four above**,
the candidate presented juz 13–15 and met the per-juz condition on all three. It is
**recorded on the certificate as a rank**, in these words:

> **«أُجيز بإتقان اثني عشر جزءًا، وأُحرز مرتبة الامتياز بخمسة عشر جزءًا.»**

**A candidate who presents fifteen and fails the per-juz condition on juz 13–15 still
holds the certificate at twelve.** Presenting for امتياز carries **no risk to the
base certificate** — a rule stated to candidates in writing before they elect, so that
no one is deterred from trying.

# PART 5 — WHAT THE CERTIFICATE ASSERTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

## 5.1 The G11 certificate wording

> **الثانوية القرآنية**
> شهدت لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية بمدارس السلطان الملكية أن الطالب ………
> **حافظٌ لاثني عشر جزءًا من القرآن الكريم إتقانًا**، سُمِع منها بالقرعة في جلستين
> بتاريخ ……، **بتجويدٍ مُجازٍ بلجنةٍ** على رواية **حفص عن عاصم** من طريق الشاطبية،
> ☐ وأحرز **مرتبة الامتياز** بخمسة عشر جزءًا.
> **وهذه إجازةٌ مؤسسيةٌ بالأداء، وليست إجازةً بسندٍ متصل.** (انظر البند ٧)

## 5.2 Twelve, not fifteen — and why the certificate is stronger for it

`MASTER-ALLOCATION-MATRIX-v4.md` §"Unresolved" ¶2 records the arithmetic: ACD-007
priced «٢٥٤٠ ساعة» of Qur'an across 13 funded years; two Council decisions removed
two of them; funded acquisition is **2,145 h = 84.6% = 12.7 juz**. The Council's own
options were to certify at twelve with fifteen as امتياز, or to restore the years.

**The Executive Council has ruled: twelve متقنة, fifteen as امتياز.**

The assessment consequence is the important one. **An institution that certifies
fifteen on funding for 12.7 must either lower what «متقنة» means or fail most of its
cohort.** Historically institutions do the former, silently, and the word «إتقان»
becomes decorative — the precise failure mode that competency 10's «لا زينة فيها»
names in another context. **Certifying twelve lets «متقنة» keep its meaning**, and the
per-juz condition of §4.2(3) is what that meaning now is, in numbers, publicly.

## 5.3 What the certificate does NOT assert

- **Not** that the holder has memorised the whole Qur'an.
- **Not** that he holds an **إجازة بالسند** (Part 7).
- **Not** that he is qualified to give فتوى, to lead تراويح of the full ختمة, or to
  teach unsupervised beyond what the G11 وحدة الإقراء certifies separately.
- **Not** a claim about the thirteenth juz onward in any direction, unless امتياز is
  ticked.

## 5.4 The honest juz count — reported everywhere, gated nowhere

`[RECOVERED]` At G9 the institution issues **«a certified, panel-verified statement of
exactly how many juz are held at what إتقان level — issued honestly, in juz, never as
pass/fail»**. That statement is **separate from the gate** and accompanies it:

```
بيان المحفوظ المصدَّق — الصف التاسع
الأجزاء المصرَّح بها: ١٣     الأجزاء المسموعة بالقرعة: ٨ مواضع موزعة عليها كلها
الأجزاء المستوفية لشرط الإتقان (≤٢ خفي/موضع، صفر جلي): ١١
الأجزاء المحفوظة دون مستوى الإتقان: ٢ — (الجزء ٩، الجزء ١٢)
بوابة القرآن: ☐ مستوفٍ  ☐ غير مستوفٍ        الحاصل: …/١٠ (قياس عتبة)
```

**This statement is not a gate, carries no consequence, and is never averaged.** It
exists so that the institution never has to choose between telling a family the truth
and issuing a certificate.

# PART 6 — DOUBLE MARKING AND RECONCILIATION

## 6.1 Independence

- **Both examiners hear the candidate live, at the same sitting**, and mark
  **independently on separate sheets**.
- **They do not speak to each other during the sitting**, do not look at each other's
  sheets, and **do not discuss the candidate until both sheets are signed and handed
  to the chair**.
- The chair **collects both sheets before any discussion begins.** A sheet amended
  after collection is void and the candidate is re-heard from the recording by a third
  examiner.

## 6.2 Tolerance and resolution

| Divergence between the two totals | Action |
|---|---|
| **≤ 0.3** | **mean of the two**, recorded |
| **0.4 – 0.7** | the two examiners **reconcile against the recording**, item by item, chaired. If they agree, the agreed total stands **with the reconciliation minuted**. If not, third examiner |
| **> 0.7** | **third examiner marks from the recording, blind to both sheets.** The **median** of the three stands |
| **Disagreement on whether an error is ج or خ** | **automatically a third examiner**, whatever the totals — because this single judgement can flip a pass to a fail under القاعدة الأولى |
| **Disagreement on whether the candidate passed at all** | **always a third examiner**, whatever the totals |

## 6.3 Mandatory reconciliation checks

Before any result leaves the room, the chair checks **every** sheet for:

1. **Double-charging** (القاعدة الثالثة) — one act, one entry.
2. **Deductions with no description** — struck.
3. **The per-موضع خفي cap** (القاعدة الثانية) — applied.
4. **Component floors** — no component below zero, no spill.
5. **The جلي flag** — recorded explicitly as yes/no, never left blank.
6. **Arithmetic** — re-added by the ضابط, who marks nothing.

## 6.4 Automatic escalation — العيّنة الإلزامية

**Every one of the following goes to a third examiner from the recording, without
anyone asking:**

- every candidate scoring **7.5 – 8.4** at G9 (within 0.5 of the gate);
- every candidate scoring **8.0 – 8.9** at G11;
- **every** candidate recorded **غير مستوفٍ**;
- **every** candidate failed under القاعدة الأولى on a single جلي;
- **every** candidate heard by a first-year examiner;
- **every** candidate for whom requirement 7 (conflict) required a waiver — **there
  are none, but the check is run so that the absence is evidenced**;
- a **random 10%** of all other candidates.

# PART 7 — «مُجاز» — WHAT THE INSTITUTION IS COMMITTING ITSELF TO

> **This Part exists because one word on a certificate can commit an institution to
> something it cannot deliver, and «مُجاز» is that word.**

## 7.1 The recovered wording and its correct reading

`[RECOVERED]` The G11 outcome is «خمسة عشر جزءًا راسخة **بتجويد مُجاز بلجنة**».

**Read it with the قيد attached.** The wording is **«مُجاز بلجنة»** — *certified by a
panel*. It is **not** «مُجاز بسند», not «إجازة بالرواية», not «إجازة بالسند المتصل
إلى النبي ﷺ». **The قيد «بلجنة» names the authority and therefore bounds the claim.**

## 7.2 The two things, side by side

| | **الإجازة المؤسسية بالأداء** — what this certificate confers | **الإجازة بالسند المتصل** — what it does not |
|---|---|---|
| **Who confers** | **لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية**, as an organ of this institution | **a مُقرئ, in his personal capacity**, who himself holds a سند |
| **On what basis** | **a sampled, double-marked, recorded examination against a published protocol** | **سماع or عرض of the entire portion, person to person, in an unbroken chain** |
| **What it asserts** | *this person recited to this standard, on these dates, before these examiners, on this sample* | *this person received this text from me, as I received it from my shaykh, back to the Prophet ﷺ* |
| **Transmissible** | **no** — it licenses nothing onward | **yes** — the holder may in principle transmit |
| **Reviewable** | **yes** — recording, sheets, appeal | **no** — it is a personal testimony, not an examination result |
| **Revocable by the institution** | **yes**, on proof of malpractice | **no** — it is not the institution's to revoke |

## 7.3 The سند basis — stated exactly

**The institution holds no سند. Institutions do not hold أسانيد; people do.**

What the institution does hold, and what grounds the word «مُجاز» on its certificate,
is this and only this:

1. **The chair of لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية at G11 must himself be مُجاز بسندٍ متصل in
   حفص عن عاصم**, and **his سند is named, with its chain summarised, in the panel
   register**, which is a permanent record.
2. **The رواية and the طريق are named on the certificate** — حفص عن عاصم من طريق
   الشاطبية — so that the standard against which the candidate was heard is a defined
   and checkable one, not «good recitation».
3. **The standard applied is textual, not personal**: تحفة الأطفال and المقدمة
   الجزرية, by which the panel's own examiners are themselves bound (§1.2 req. 2).
4. **The certificate says in its own body what it is** (§5.1, last line), so that no
   reader can be misled by the word.

**Therefore the institution's claim is:** *a panel, chaired by a man who himself
holds a connected chain in this rendering, heard this candidate against the received
technical standard of that rendering, and certifies his performance.* **That is a
real and defensible claim. It is not a chain, and the certificate says so.**

## 7.4 The parallel إجازة track — recorded, not conferred

`[RECOVERED]` The إجازة بالسند is a **parallel, opt-in, pace-based track** (redline 2),
separately governed, **never a grade gate**. Under this protocol:

- **The institution facilitates it and does not confer it.** سرد بالسند sessions run
  at G11–G12 under a qualified مُقرئ, on the candidate's own pace.
- Where a candidate obtains an إجازة, **the institution records the fact** on the
  transcript in **Section C** — «حصل على إجازة بالسند من الشيخ ……، بتاريخ ……» — and
  **files a copy**.
- **The institution does not verify the chain and does not warrant it.** It records
  what it was shown, attributed to the شيخ who gave it, and says so.
- **No candidate's certificate, gate, average or standing is affected in any way by
  holding or not holding an إجازة.** It is not a condition, not a mark, not an
  advantage. A protocol that let it become one would convert a personal transmission
  into a school prize, which is precisely what it is not.

## 7.5 What the institution promises a holder of «مُجاز بلجنة»

Stated plainly, so a graduate can repeat it accurately when asked:

- **We will verify this certificate to any enquirer, for life**, from the permanent
  certificate register (`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` Part 13).
- **We will produce, for five years, the recording and both examiners' sheets** on
  which it rests.
- **We will state to any enquirer, in writing, exactly what it means** — §7.2 — and
  **we will not let it be represented as an إجازة بالسند**, by the holder or by
  anyone else. **Misrepresentation of it by a holder is a matter for the Director
  General**, because the institution's word is what the certificate is made of.

# PART 8 — RE-SIT, DEFERRAL, AND ACCESS

## 8.1 Re-sit

| | G9 | G11 |
|---|---|---|
| **Re-sits available** | **two** | **two** |
| **Interval** | not less than **six weeks** | not less than **eight weeks** |
| **What is re-sampled** | **fresh lots across the full declared corpus** — never the same مواضع | as G9 |
| **Partial re-sit** | not available | **available**: where the only failure is the per-juz condition on named juz (§4.2(3)), the candidate re-sits **those juz alone**, two مواضع each |
| **Declared corpus** | may be **re-declared** before a re-sit | may be re-declared; **امتياز may be added or dropped freely** |
| **Panel** | **at least one examiner must be different** from the first sitting | as G9 |

**A candidate who fails twice is not failed by the institution; he is re-taught.** The
chair writes a **خطة معالجة** naming the error codes that recurred and the drills that
address them, and it goes to the حلقة teacher. **An error taxonomy that does not
produce a teaching plan is bureaucracy.**

## 8.2 Deferral

Illness, bereavement, or a documented circumstance on the day: the sitting is
**deferred**, not failed, on the chair's decision, recorded with reasons. **A deferral
is never counted as a re-sit.**

## 8.3 Access arrangements at the gate

Governed by `ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` Part 12. Applied here:

| Granted | Never granted |
|---|---|
| extra time; the clock stopped for breaks | a reduced number of مواضع |
| a quiet room, familiar surroundings | a reduced portion or an easier corpus |
| a longer silence before a prompt is given | extra prompts beyond two per موضع |
| the lot drawn and read more slowly, or shown in writing | advance sight of the مواضع |
| a familiar adult present, silent, out of the candidate's eyeline | a lowered threshold |

**The recitation demand itself is never reduced.** A candidate who cannot recite the
sampled مواضع has not met the gate, and pretending otherwise devalues it for every
candidate who did.

## 8.4 The articulation baseline — تأصيل النطق

**For a candidate with a documented speech or articulation condition**, and **only**
for such a candidate:

1. **Before** the examination series, a **baseline recording** is made with the حلقة
   teacher and the chair, establishing which articulations that candidate cannot
   produce and which he produces with a consistent, characteristic variation.
2. The baseline is **lodged with the panel and the panel is told of it before the
   sitting**, without being told anything else about the candidate.
3. **Codes ج١ (إبدال حرف), خ٤ and خ٥ are assessed against that baseline**, not against
   the general standard: a variation that is the condition is **not** an error; a
   variation that is **not** the condition is an error and is charged normally.
4. **No other code is adjusted.** Memory, retrieval, أحكام, مد measures, stops and
   fluency are assessed identically to every other candidate.
5. **The certificate is identical** and carries **no annotation of any kind.**
6. **The baseline is confidential**, retained with the access-arrangement file, and
   disclosable only to the panel and on appeal.

**Why this is «removing a barrier» and not «lowering the standard».** The construct
this gate certifies is **حفظ متقن بتجويد مُجاز**, and a fixed articulatory limitation
is not a failure of ḥifẓ or of tajwīd knowledge — it is a property of the instrument
the candidate recites with. Charging it as ج١ measures the condition, not the
competence. **[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]** The corpus does not settle this; the board
rules it here, and records that a candidate so certified should be told plainly what
his certificate does and does not say about his articulation, so that he is never
surprised by it later.

# PART 9 — APPEALS AGAINST A QUR'AN GATE DECISION

> **This is the strongest appeal route in the institution, because this is the only
> instrument that is judged live, by ear, and cannot be re-read.**

## 9.1 Grounds

The five general grounds of `ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §11.1, **plus four specific to
this instrument**:

6. **خطأ في التصنيف** — an error classified ج where the appellant says it is خ, or
   vice versa (**the single most consequential judgement here**, because of
   القاعدة الأولى);
7. **خطأ في العيّنة** — the lot was not drawn as prescribed, or a موضع fell outside
   the declared corpus, or the eight bands were not honoured;
8. **مخالفة في إدارة الجلسة** — prompting outside the rule, correction during the
   sitting, examiner signalling, a conflict of interest not declared;
9. **خلل في التسجيل** — the recording is absent, incomplete or inaudible. **Where the
   recording is missing or unusable through no fault of the candidate, and the
   decision was غير مستوفٍ, the sitting is VOID and is re-run at the institution's
   cost, with no re-sit counted.** The institution's own evidentiary failure is never
   charged to a candidate.

## 9.2 Stages

| Stage | Deadline | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **١ · مراجعة السجل** | **10 working days** | Examinations Officer | both sheets re-added; codes checked for double-charging, missing descriptions, cap and floors; **no re-listening** |
| **٢ · إعادة السماع من التسجيل** | **15 working days** | **a third examiner who did not sit on the panel**, marking **blind** — without the sheets, without the totals, without knowing the outcome | a full independent re-mark from the recording |
| **٣ · اللجنة الاستئنافية** | **20 working days** of the stage‑2 outcome | **three**: a Board member · a **مُقرئ external to the institution** · one panel chair who did not chair this sitting | procedure, classification and merits. **The appellant may attend and may be accompanied.** **The recording is played in the hearing wherever classification is in dispute** |

**Outcome at stage 2 or 3** is the **median of the marks now on the table**, and it
**may be lower**. The appellant is told this in writing and consents before stage 2
proceeds.

**A stage‑3 decision is final within the institution.**

## 9.3 The appellant's entitlements

An appellant is entitled, on request and without charge, to:

1. **both examiners' error sheets**, in full, with every description;
2. **the reconciliation minute**, and the third examiner's sheet where one was used;
3. **the standardisation record of the examiners who heard him** for that cycle
   (§1.3) — *whether the people who judged him were within tolerance is his business*;
4. **to listen to his own recording**, in the presence of an officer;
5. **written reasons**, naming the موضع, the code, and the rule applied, for every
   deduction that made the difference between his score and the threshold.

**He is not entitled to a copy of the recording**, which is retained under Part 10 and
not released.

## 9.4 Appeals against الثانوية القرآنية specifically

The same route, with one addition: where the appeal is against **the per-juz condition
on a named juz** (§4.2(3)), the stage‑2 re-listen is **confined to that juz's موضع**,
and a successful appeal yields **either** the certificate at twelve **or** a partial
re-sit on that juz alone (§8.1), **at the appellant's election**.

# PART 11 — THE ANNUAL REPORT OF THE PANEL

After each cycle the chair reports **in writing** to the Assessment Board and the
Chairman:

1. candidates heard, met, not met, deferred, re-sat — **and the same for each re-sit**;
2. **the error profile of the cohort by code** — which of ج١–ج٦ and خ١–خ٧ actually
   occurred, and how often. *This is the single most useful teaching document the
   institution produces in a year, and it exists nowhere else;*
3. **examiner divergence statistics** — how often tolerance was exceeded, by whom, on
   what;
4. **how often the ج/خ classification went to a third examiner**, which is the health
   indicator for training stage 2;

6. **juz counts across the cohort against the funded projection of 12.7**, so the
   Council's arithmetic is checked against reality every year rather than once;
7. a recommendation on whether any threshold, penalty or tolerance in this protocol
   should change — **with evidence, and never mid-cycle.**
