# ASSESSMENT POLICY — سياسة التقويم والامتحانات
## Sultan Royal Schools / GACAIS — Assessment Board · v1.0

**Status:** adopted instrument. This is the first assessment policy the institution
has possessed. It supersedes nothing because nothing existed; it binds everything
written after it.

**Scope.** All 28 subjects of the three programmes — القرآن وعلومه · اللغة العربية
وعلومها · الدراسات الإسلامية — across الروضة and G1–G12, and all five rungs of the
certificate ladder.

**Governing documents.** ACD-007 (constitution, §§2.1, 3.1, 3.2, 4.4, Part 1,
Part 7), ACD-010, `MASTER-ALLOCATION-MATRIX-v4.md`, `P2-01-RULINGS.md`,
`COUNCIL-DECISION-SHEET-01/02.md`, `MASTER-TIMETABLE-LEDGER.md`.

**What this institution is, for assessment purposes.** SHRS/GACAIS is a
**supplementary** institution. Pupils attend a conventional school in the morning
and this institution in the afternoon and at weekends. It does not teach
mathematics, science or English and **must never assess them**, with the single
exception of WAEC, which is a *certificate condition* discharged through the
morning stream and the G12 revision programme — never a subject of this
institution's own marking. Every rule below is written for an institution whose
pupils are already carrying a full external examination load elsewhere. **Assessment
volume is therefore a rationed good, and the rationing is the policy's first
substantive act.**

# PART 1 — THE FIRST PRINCIPLE

> **مبدأ الاقتصاد في التقويم**
> *لا يُمتحن ما لا يُدرَّس مستقلًّا، ولا يُدرَّج ما لا يُقاس، ولا يُقاس ما لا يُدرَّس.*

A prior board ruled that **only 8–9 subjects may carry independent terminal
assessment in any one year**. That ruling is now operationalised. Twenty-four
child-facing subjects at G9 cannot produce twenty-four terminal papers for a
fifteen-year-old who is also sitting a morning school's examinations. The
institution's answer is not to examine less rigorously but to **examine fewer
things, harder, and to report everything else by a different and honestly named
mechanism**.

Three mechanisms exist and only three:

| # | Mechanism | What it produces | Where it is reported |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **درجة مُدرَّجة** — a graded mark | a number 0–100 and a band | Transcript **Section A** |
| **2** | **تقدير إنجاز** — a completion judgement | إتمام / عدم إتمام + a descriptor | Transcript **Section B** |
| **3** | **استيفاء شرط** — a condition satisfied | مستوفٍ / غير مستوفٍ + date + panel | Transcript **Section C** |

**Nothing is assessed by any fourth means, and no instrument may be reported in
more than one section.** A thing that is measured twice is counted twice, and a
curriculum that counts twice is lying about its own load.

# PART 2 — THE FORM→CONSEQUENCE DOCTRINE

The allocation matrix assigns every subject in every grade one of six
**instructional forms**: `● مستقل · I مدمج · E مضمّن · U وحدة · R دوراني · s strand`.
This notation has been used across twenty documents **as a description of teaching
and never once cashed out as an assessment rule.** That is the gap this Part closes.

**The doctrine, stated once:**

> **الصورة التعليمية تُحدِّد أثر التقويم تحديدًا لازمًا لا اختياريًّا.**
> Instructional form determines assessment consequence, necessarily and not at the
> teacher's discretion. A subject's form in a given grade **is** its assessment
> entitlement in that grade. Where the matrix and a scheme of work disagree, **the
> matrix governs**, and the scheme of work is amended.

## 2.1 The six forms and their exact assessment consequence

### ● مستقل — independent

- **Teaching:** own periods, own book, own teacher of record.
- **Assessment:** **entitled to** — not guaranteed — an independent terminal paper.
  Entitlement becomes a paper only where the grade's paper budget (Part 3) permits.
- **Reporting:** one own line, Section A, contributing to the aggregate average.
- **Constraint:** a مستقل subject **denied** a paper in a given year (because the
  budget is full) does not vanish. It is assessed **continuously** (Part 4) and
  reported in Section A with the annotation «تقويم مستمر — بلا ورقة نهائية».
  It contributes to the average at the same weight as a papered subject.
- **Prohibition:** a مستقل subject may never be reported only as a strand of another.

### I مدمج — merged · **the form the institution keeps getting wrong**

- **Teaching:** two sciences taught in one block, jointly, by design.
- **مدمج is SYMMETRIC.** `RR-01` in the matrix records that v3 wrote
  `السيرة I / التاريخ ●`, which is not a merge at all but **hosting** — and that the
  recovered remedy «دمج السيرة بالتاريخ» was therefore never actually implemented.
  A merge in which one partner is ● and the other is I **is not a merge**; it is a
  مضمّن relationship mislabelled, and it silently deletes the smaller science.
  **Both partners of a مدمج pair carry `I` or the pair is void.**
- **Assessment:** **ONE paper, bearing both names**, e.g. «السيرة والتاريخ»,
  «التفسير وأصوله», «الفقه وأصوله». Inside that paper each science is a
  **named, separately-weighted, separately-totalled SECTION**. The section weights
  are published in the specification grid before teaching begins and may not be
  varied by the paper-setter.
- **Reporting:** **one mark line** in Section A under the joint name, with the two
  section sub-marks printed beneath it in smaller type. The **combined** mark enters
  the aggregate; the sub-marks do not enter separately.
- **Compensation inside a merged paper:** a candidate **may not** compensate freely
  across the two sections. Each section carries a **section floor of 40%** of its own
  marks. A candidate above the paper pass mark but below a section floor is recorded
  as «ناجح بمعالجة في [القسم]» and is required to clear that section's floor in the
  re-sit before the certificate issues. *Rationale:* without a floor, a candidate can
  pass «الفقه وأصوله» while knowing no أصول whatever, and the merge would have
  destroyed exactly what it was created to protect.

### E مضمّن — embedded

- **Teaching:** delivered inside a named **host** subject's periods, at zero
  additional timetable cost. The host **must be named** in the scheme of work;
  an unnamed host is how embedded sciences silently evaporate, and how the
  constitutionally ring-fenced Qur'an hour gets raided.
- **Assessment:** **مضمّن may never set a paper of its own. Ever.** It has two, and
  only two, sub-forms, and the matrix's `E` must be qualified with one of them in
  every future scheme of work:

  | Sub-form | Definition | Consequence |
  |---|---|---|
  | **E‑س · مضمّن مستتر** (silent) | assessed only through the host's own items | **no separate mark, no separate line.** Its evidence sits in the host's mark |
  | **E‑ع · مضمّن مُعلَّم** (flagged) | carries a **named, separately-weighted section inside the host's paper** | a **sub-mark printed beneath the host's line**, not entering the average separately |

- **The worked case that settles the sub-forms:** أصول الفقه is `E` at G8 **and** `E`
  at G9, and the two are not the same thing. At G8 it is **E‑س**: the five أحكام
  تكليفية replace the حلال/حرام binary and every ruling is taught with its نص beside
  it, with *no أصول terminology whatever* — there is nothing to report separately
  because there is no discipline yet, only a habit. At G9 it is **E‑ع**: the four
  أدلة are named and ordered, and the student is given a ruling he holds and must
  state its دليل. **That is a markable performance, it has a name, and it therefore
  takes a named section inside the الفقه paper** — which is precisely why the G9
  paper is titled «الفقه وأصوله» and not «الفقه».
- **Prohibition:** E‑ع may not exceed **35%** of the host paper. Above 35% the
  embedded science is no longer embedded and must be re-declared مدمج at the next
  matrix revision.

### U وحدة — bounded unit

- **Teaching:** a closed block of defined length inside a host or standing alone —
  «وحدة الوقف والابتداء» (التجويد G8), «وحدة الفرائض» (الفقه G9),
  «وحدة الإقراء وتصحيح التلاوة» (التجويد G11), «وحدة علم الكلام» (العقيدة G12).
- **Assessment:** by a **single terminal task appropriate to the unit** — a
  practicum, a defended performance, a case-set, a portfolio — **not** by an
  examination paper of its own.
- **Reporting:** **Section B.** إتمام / عدم إتمام plus one descriptor. **A unit does
  not contribute to the aggregate average.**
- **The one exception, stated explicitly so it is not invented later:** where a
  وحدة is declared in a specification grid as a **content domain of a papered
  subject**, it is examined inside that paper and reported in Section A with it,
  and it does **not** also appear in Section B. وحدة الفرائض at G9 is exactly this:
  it is domain د٥ of the «الفقه وأصوله» paper, carrying 14 of 100 marks. **It is
  counted once.**

### R دوراني — rotational

- **Teaching:** the subject appears on a rota across a cohort or across terms; not
  every pupil meets it in the same week, and some meet it in one term only.
- **Assessment:** **portfolio and participation record only.** A rotational subject
  may **never** set a terminal paper, because the candidates have not had a common
  syllabus exposure and a common paper would measure the rota, not the pupil.
- **Reporting:** Section B, إتمام / عدم إتمام. No mark. No contribution to average.

### s — strand (G1–G6 only)

- **Teaching:** التوحيد · الفقه · الحديث النبوي · السيرة النبوية are **internal
  strands of one child-facing subject, التربية الإسلامية**, from G1 to G6.
  التاريخ الإسلامي joins as a fifth strand at G6 only.
- **Assessment:** **ONE mark for التربية الإسلامية and nothing else.**
- **Reporting:** that one mark in Section A, **plus** a subordinate **strand annexe**
  carrying a three-point can-do descriptor per strand (see
  `PRIMARY-STRAND-DESCRIPTORS.md`). **The annexe carries no numbers of any kind.**
- **Absolute prohibitions, because each has been attempted somewhere in the corpus
  and each destroys the design:**
  1. **No strand may be given a separate mark**, percentage, or sub-percentage —
     not on a report card, not in a mark book, not in a parent conversation.
  2. **No strand may be ranked against another strand or against other children.**
  3. **No strand may be aggregated across children** into a class average.
  4. **No strand may set its own examination**, in-class test, or end-of-term paper.
  5. **No strand may be reported as a "grade" in words that function as numbers**
     («ب+», «٤ من ٥»). The three descriptors متقن / نامٍ / مبتدئ are the entire
     permitted vocabulary.
  *Rationale:* four separately-marked Islamic sciences at age seven produces four
  examination anxieties, four remediation labels, and four opportunities for a
  parent to conclude that a seven-year-old is bad at توحيد. The institution's
  answer to "how is he doing in فقه?" is a sentence describing what the child can
  do, and the institution refuses to convert that sentence into a number.

## 2.2 The doctrine as a single table

| Form | Own paper? | Own mark line? | Enters average? | Reported in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ● مستقل | yes (budget permitting) | yes | **yes** | A |
| ● مستقل, unpapered | no — continuous | yes | **yes** | A |
| I مدمج | **shared**, named sections | one **joint** line + sub-marks | **yes** (combined) | A |
| E‑ع مضمّن مُعلَّم | no — section inside host | sub-mark under host | no (inside host) | A |
| E‑س مضمّن مستتر | no | no | no (inside host) | A (host only) |
| U وحدة | no — terminal task | no | **no** | B |
| U وحدة *declared as a paper domain* | inside host paper | no | inside host | A |
| R دوراني | **never** | no | **no** | B |
| s strand | **never** | **never** | **no** | annexe to A |
| شرط condition | **never a contributory mark** | no | **no** | C |

# PART 3 — THE PAPER BUDGET

## 3.1 The ceiling

**No grade may set more than nine terminal papers.** G1–G3 may set no more than
four. This is a hard ceiling and a paper over the ceiling is invalid, not merely
unwise.

## 3.2 The papers of the certificate years

**الشهادة الإعدادية — end of G9 — seven papers** (ruled by the prior board and
confirmed here):

|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | **النحو والصرف** | النحو ● + الصرف ● | I |
| 3 | **التفسير وأصوله** | التفسير ● + أصول التفسير U (علوم القرآن) | ●+U‑domain |
| 4 | **العقيدة والتوحيد** | العقيدة ● + التوحيد E‑ع | ●+E‑ع |
| 5 | **الفقه وأصوله** | الفقه ● + أصول الفقه E‑ع | ●+E‑ع |
| 6 | **الحديث ومصطلحه** | الحديث النبوي ● + مصطلح الحديث U | ●+U‑domain |

Plus, **outside the papers and outside the average**: بوابة القرآن ٨/١٠
(Section C, Part 9) and the Section B units and rotations

`I` at G7 and G9 in matrix v4 precisely because v3's asymmetric notation had
silently deleted السيرة. If Paper 7 is ever set as a history paper with a sirah
question in it, the merge has failed again and the policy has been ignored.
Each science carries a named half — **السيرة ٥٠ · التاريخ ٥٠** — each with the
40% section floor of §2.1.

# PART 4 — WEIGHTINGS

## 4.1 Within a graded subject in a year

| Component | Weight | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **التقويم المستمر** continuous | **30%** | term tests, set written work, oral تسميع, class performance — *recorded, not impressionistic* |
| **الورقة النهائية** terminal paper | **70%** | the paper set against a published specification grid |

**For a مستقل subject with no paper** (budget-excluded): continuous assessment is
**100%**, and must comprise at least six separately dated recorded judgements in
the year, at least two of which are written.

**For subjects whose defining performance is not written** —
حفظ القرآن · التجويد · البحث والخطابة · الترجمة (oral component) · وحدة الإقراء —
the "paper" is the **specified practical performance**, assessed live against a
published rubric by a trained assessor, and the 30/70 split applies unchanged.

## 4.2 In the certificate years

| Certificate | What aggregates | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **الشهادة الابتدائية** (G5) | mean of the G5 graded subjects, external examiner countersigning | see Part 6 |
| **شهادة الجسر اللغوي** (G6) | **not an average** — بوابة الجسر بلجنة ثلاثية, a gate | pass/fail on the gate criteria |
| **الشهادة الإعدادية** (G9) | **المتوسط العام** of the seven papers (equal weight, 1/7 each) **+ بوابة القرآن ٨/١٠** | Part 6 + Part 9 |
| **الثانوية القرآنية** (G11) | **no average at all** — a single Section C condition | Part 9 |
| **شهادة التخرج** (G12) | **no average at all** — ten competencies + مناقشة + WAEC, all Section C | Part 7 |

**The seven G9 papers carry equal weight.** [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] The
alternative — weighting العربية or الفقه higher — was considered and rejected: any
unequal weighting is a statement about which science matters more, the institution
declines to make that statement, and an equal weighting is the only one that
survives a challenge from a parent.

# PART 6 — PASS MARKS AND COMPENSATION

## 6.1 Pass marks

| Level | Subject pass | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any single paper or graded subject | **50** | |
| Section floor inside a merged or E‑ع paper | **40% of that section's marks** | §2.1 |
| **الشهادة الإعدادية** overall | **المتوسط العام ≥ 55** | [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] — a certificate average set at the bare subject pass mark certifies a candidate who passed nothing well |
| **الشهادة الابتدائية** overall | **المتوسط العام ≥ 50** | |
| **بوابة القرآن** | **8/10** | `[RECOVERED]`. **Not compensable. Not averageable. Not negotiable.** |

## 6.2 Compensation — قاعدة التعويض

A candidate for **الشهادة الإعدادية** whose overall average is ≥ 55 but who has
fallen below 50 on a paper may be compensated, **once**, subject to **all five** of
the following:

1. **One paper only.** Two sub-50 papers cannot both be compensated.
2. **Not below 40.** A mark under 40 is never compensated; it is re-sat.
3. **Surplus must exist.** The candidate must hold at least **10 marks of surplus
   above 55** distributed across the other papers.
4. **العربية is never compensated.** Arabic is the language in which every other
   paper is written and every other science is held. A candidate who has failed
   العربية has not narrowly missed one subject; he cannot read the corpus.
5. **Section floors must still be met.** Compensation operates on paper totals and
   never repairs a breached section floor. A candidate compensated on «الفقه
   وأصوله» while below the أصول floor is not compensated; he is re-sat on أصول.

**Never compensable, under any circumstance, by any authority:**
بوابة القرآن ٨/١٠ · الثانوية القرآنية · any of the ten competencies · مناقشة التخرج
· WAEC completion · خدمة التتويج.

## 6.3 THE CONDITION RULE — القاعدة الحاكمة

> **الشرط لا يحمل درجة، ولا يدخل في متوسط، ولا يُعوَّض، ولا يُجبَر بفائض.**
> **A certificate CONDITION can never carry a mark.**

A condition of a certificate is reported **مستوفٍ / غير مستوفٍ** — met or not met —
with a date and the name of the panel that so found. It never appears as a number
in Section A, never contributes to an average, and can never be traded against
strength elsewhere.

**The near-miss that this rule must survive:** بوابة القرآن is expressed as **8/10**,
and 8/10 looks like a mark. It is not. It is a **threshold measure** — a number
whose only function is to sit on one side or the other of a line. The distinction
is operative and testable:

| | contributory mark | threshold measure |
|---|---|---|
| enters an average | yes | **no** |
| can be compensated | sometimes | **never** |
| surplus above the line has value | yes | **none whatever** |
| reported in | Section A | **Section C** |

A candidate scoring 10/10 at the Qur'an gate has **exactly the same certificate
entitlement** as one scoring 8/10. The surplus is real, it is worth recording in
the candidate's Qur'an record, and it buys nothing. Any proposal to let a 10/10
gate lift a weak فقه paper is a proposal to abolish the gate, and is refused in
advance.

**خدمة التتويج is the same rule from the other side.** Competency 10 is «شرط استلام
شهادة التخرج **لا زينة فيها**». Because it is a condition, it **cannot be marked**,
cannot be graded امتياز, and cannot be averaged into anything. It is verified —
executed, impact-documented, conduct record at جدارة (70) or above — and recorded as
met. **A service project that is marked has been converted into a subject, and a
service project that is a subject is exactly the «زينة» the constitution forbids.**

**Rules of the transcript:**

1. A transcript with a mark in Section B or Section C is **void** and is reissued.
2. A transcript on which a strand carries a number is **void** and is reissued.
3. The aggregate average is computed from Section A only, and the arithmetic is
   printed on the document so it can be checked by the holder.
4. **The three sections are printed even when empty.** An empty Section C on a G7
   transcript states truthfully that no certificate condition was in play that year.
5. A candidate who has met every condition but failed the average holds **no
   certificate**, and a candidate who has passed every paper but failed one
   condition holds **no certificate**. The sections are conjunctive.

# PART 8 — MODERATION AND THE EXTERNAL EXAMINER

## 8.1 Internal moderation — the four-stage cycle

| Stage | When | Who | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **١ · تصديق المواصفة** | before teaching begins | Assessment Board | the **specification grid** is approved and published. **No paper may be set against an unpublished grid** |
| **٢ · تدقيق الورقة** | ≥ 6 weeks before sitting | a subject scrutineer who did **not** set the paper | grid-conformity check, item-by-item; ambiguity, cueing, out-of-syllabus and load check; the mark scheme is checked at the same time and never later |
| **٣ · التصحيح التجريبي** | ≥ 2 weeks before sitting | setter + scrutineer, on 5 real scripts from a prior cohort or a trial sitting | mark scheme amended **before** live marking, never during |
| **٤ · تثبيت المعايير** | after marking, before results | all markers of that paper | sample re-mark of **10% or 10 scripts, whichever is greater**, plus **every** script within 3 marks of a grade boundary, plus **every** script marked at band 5, plus **every** script from a first-year marker |

**Marking rules.** Scripts are marked **anonymously by candidate number**. Where a
paper has more than one marker, marking is **by question across all scripts**, not by
script across all questions. A marker may not mark his own teaching group's scripts
where an alternative marker exists; where none exists, that group's scripts go
whole into the stage‑4 sample.

**Tolerance.** Two markers within **3 marks** on a 100-mark paper: accept the mean.
4–7 marks: a third marker, and the middle mark stands. **8 or more: the whole
question is re-marked for every candidate**, because a disagreement that large is a
fault in the mark scheme and not in the markers.

## 8.2 The external examiner — بروتوكول الممتحن الخارجي

`[RECOVERED]` The الشهادة الابتدائية (G5) and الشهادة الإعدادية (G9) are
**«بممتحن خارجي»**. That word is now given content.

**Who may serve.** A scholar or teacher of the relevant science, holding a
recognised qualification in it, **with no employment, family, financial or
governance relationship to this institution in the three years preceding**, and no
pupil of his own in the cohort. He is appointed by the Assessment Board, in writing,
for a named cycle, and is paid a fixed fee that does not vary with outcomes.

**What he does — and this is the whole of it:**

1. Receives the **specification grid, the draft paper and the draft mark scheme**
   at stage 2, and may **require** amendment of any of the three. His requirement is
   not advice; an unamended requirement blocks the sitting.
2. Receives, after stage 4, a **sample of at least 20% of scripts** spanning every
   band, including **all** band‑5 scripts and **all** boundary scripts.
3. **Countersigns or refuses** the grade boundaries. He may move a boundary by up to
   **3 marks** in either direction on his own authority, with written reasons; beyond
   3 marks he refers to the Board.
4. Writes a **signed report** naming: whether the paper matched the grid; whether the
   mark scheme was applied consistently; whether standards are comparable to the
   prior cycle; and any syllabus area where the cohort is weak.
5. **Countersigns the certificate list.** A certificate issued without his signature
   is not a certificate of this institution.

**What he may never do.** He may not teach the cohort, may not see candidate names,
may not alter an individual candidate's mark (he alters boundaries, which is a
cohort act), and may not be reappointed for more than **three consecutive cycles**.

**The Qur'an panel is a different body.** الثانوية القرآنية is awarded by **لجنة
الامتحانات القرآنية**, not by the external examiner, and that panel's constitution,
qualification and double-marking rules are in `QURAN-GATE-PROTOCOL.md`. **شهادة
التخرج** is awarded by **مجلس المناقشة برئاسة الإدارة**. Three distinct awarding
bodies, deliberately, so that no one body certifies the whole child.

# PART 9 — THE QUR'AN GATE IN RELATION TO THIS POLICY

`[RECOVERED]` الشهادة الإعدادية = **المتوسط العام + بوابة القرآن ٨/١٠**.

Its full instrument is `QURAN-GATE-PROTOCOL.md`. Within this policy:

- It is a **Section C condition**, not a Section A subject (§6.3).
- It is **conjunctive** with the average: both, or no certificate.
- It is **the most challengeable instrument the institution owns**, because it is
  live, oral, unrepeatable and judged by ear. It therefore carries double-marking,
  recording, and an appeal route stronger than any paper's (Part 11).
- **حفظ القرآن الكريم also carries a Section A line** — a graded subject with its own
  continuous and terminal assessment — and the gate is **not** that line. The two are
  separate instruments measuring different things: the Section A line reports
  *progress in acquisition*, the gate reports *security of what is held*. A candidate
  can hold a fine Section A mark and fail the gate. **This is not a contradiction and
  must be explained to parents in these words.**
- The **juz count is reported honestly, in juz, and never as pass/fail** — at G9 a
  panel-verified statement of exactly how many juz are held at what إتقان level.

# PART 10 — MALPRACTICE · سوء الأمانة في الامتحان

**Framing.** In this institution malpractice is a breach of **أمانة**, which is
competency 10's own word. It is treated as a matter of formation, not only of
regulation — and it is nevertheless regulated, because a certificate nobody can
cheat is the only kind worth holding.

## 10.1 Categories and standard outcomes

| Category | Examples | Standard outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **أ · مخالفة إجرائية** | materials in the room, talking, overrunning, wrong seat | warning recorded; mark unaffected unless advantage gained |
| **ب · غش** | copying, communicating, unauthorised notes, phone | **that paper voided**; re-sit at next sitting; recorded |
| **ج · انتحال** | presenting another's work as one's own in the research paper, khutbah or service report | **whole component voided**; re-submission; **مناقشة التخرج deferred one cycle** |
| **د · تزوير** | forged evidence, falsified service documentation, impersonation, altered scripts | **whole sitting voided**; barred one cycle; **referred to the Director General** |
| **هـ · مخالفة موظف** | teaching a paper's contents, altering marks, coaching in a live gate | removal from all assessment duties; disciplinary referral; **affected instrument re-run for the whole cohort** |

## 10.2 Process

Report in writing within **48 hours**; candidate **completes the paper** (never
removed mid-paper except for disruption); written statements from invigilator and
candidate; a **panel of three**, none of whom taught or invigilated that paper; the
candidate is **heard in person and may bring an adult**; decision in writing with
reasons within **10 working days**; appeal under Part 11.

**Standard of proof: على الأرجح الأظهر** — the balance of probabilities — **except
for categories د and هـ**, where the standard is **بيّنة واضحة** (clear and
convincing), because the consequences are lasting and reputational.

**Two protections that are not negotiable.** (1) **No candidate is accused in front
of a room.** (2) **No malpractice finding may be recorded against a pupil in G1–G6.**
A child under twelve who copies is a teaching problem and is handled as one.

# PART 11 — APPEALS · التظلمات

## 11.1 Grounds

An appeal lies **only** on one or more of:
1. **خطأ حسابي أو نقل** — arithmetic or transcription error;
2. **مخالفة إجرائية** — a procedure in this policy was not followed;
3. **خطأ في تطبيق سلم التصحيح** — the mark scheme was misapplied;
4. **ظرف طارئ موثّق** — documented circumstance not considered at the sitting;
5. **تحيز** — a conflict of interest not declared.

**Not a ground:** disagreement with the professional judgement of a properly trained
assessor applying an approved scheme, standing alone.

## 11.2 The three stages

| Stage | Deadline | Who | What is done | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **١ · مراجعة** clerical | **10 working days** of results | Examinations Officer | totals re-added, transcription checked against the script | none |
| **٢ · إعادة تصحيح** re-mark | **15 working days** of results | a marker who has **not seen the script**, blind to the original mark | full re-mark against the approved scheme | refundable deposit; **refunded on any change** |
| **٣ · استئناف أمام اللجنة** | **20 working days** of the stage‑2 outcome | panel of three: one Board member, one subject specialist unconnected to the marking, **one member external to the institution** | procedure, application of scheme, and merits | none |

**The mark can go down at stage 2**, and the candidate is told this in writing
before consenting. **A stage‑3 decision is final within the institution.**

## 11.3 Appeals against the Qur'an gate and against conditions

- The Qur'an gate has its own appeal route, including **review of the audio record
  by a third examiner who did not sit on the panel** — `QURAN-GATE-PROTOCOL.md` §9.
- A **condition** (WAEC, competency 10, مناقشة التخرج) is appealed on **procedure
  and evidence only**. There is no mark to re-mark. The question is always: *was the
  condition met, and was it judged by the process this policy prescribes?*

# PART 12 — ACCESS ARRANGEMENTS · تيسيرات الوصول

**Principle.** *يُزال العائق ولا يُخفَّض المطلوب.* Remove the barrier, never lower
the standard. An access arrangement lets a candidate show what he knows. It does
not change what he must know.

**Granted on documented, standing evidence of need** — normal classroom practice for
at least one term, plus a professional report where one exists. **Applied for by the
teacher, approved by the Examinations Officer, recorded on the candidate's file,
and never announced to the cohort.**

| Need | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| Reading or processing difficulty | **+25% time**; enlarged, well-spaced paper; a quiet room |
| Vision | enlarged print (18pt+), Braille where held, a reader who **reads only what is printed** |
| Hearing | written instructions for everything spoken; for oral instruments, an assessor positioned for lip-reading; **never a reduction in the oral demand itself** |
| Physical / writing | a scribe writing **verbatim**, or a keyboard with dictionary and autocorrect **disabled**; rest breaks with the clock stopped |
| Speech / fluency | **extra time on oral instruments**; no penalty whatever for hesitation, repetition or stammer; assessors are told this is not a fluency test unless fluency is the declared construct |
| Documented anxiety | a separate small room; a familiar invigilator; rest breaks |
| Temporary injury or illness | a scribe, extra time, or a deferred sitting |

**Never granted:** extra marks; a reduced syllabus; a different pass mark; advance
sight of questions; help with content in any form; a pass on a condition that was
not met.

**On the Qur'an gate specifically.** Time is extended and the room is made quiet.
**The recitation demand itself is never reduced** — a candidate who cannot recite
the sampled مواضع has not met the gate, and saying otherwise devalues the gate for
every candidate who did. But where a documented speech condition affects articulation
and not memory, the **error taxonomy's articulation categories are assessed against
that candidate's own established baseline**, recorded in advance, by a panel told so
before the sitting. `QURAN-GATE-PROTOCOL.md` §8.4.

# PART 13 — EVIDENCE RETENTION · حفظ البيّنات

| Evidence | Retained | Held by | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Answer scripts, non-certificate | **18 months** | Examinations Office | covers a full appeal cycle plus one |
| Answer scripts, **certificate years (G5, G9)** | **5 years** | Examinations Office, locked | external challenge, verification requests |
| Mark sheets, boundary decisions, stage‑4 records | **5 years** | Examinations Office | reconstructing how a grade was arrived at |
| Specification grids, papers, mark schemes | **permanently** | Assessment Board archive | comparability across cycles; a school with no archive re-sets standards every year by accident |
| External examiner reports | **permanently** | Assessment Board archive | the institution's own accountability record |
| **Qur'an gate audio recordings** | **3 years** (G9) · **5 years** (G11) | Qur'an Examinations Panel, access-controlled | **the only reviewable evidence a live oral instrument leaves** |
| Qur'an panel score sheets, both markers | **5 years** | Qur'an Examinations Panel | double-marking must be auditable |
| Service project (competency 10) evidence | **5 years** | Graduation Council | a condition of a certificate must remain provable |
| Research paper + referee reports | **5 years** | Graduation Council | plagiarism and challenge |
| Strand annexes and primary report cards | **3 years** | School office | continuity of teaching judgement |
| Access arrangement files | **duration + 3 years** | Examinations Officer, confidential | |
| Malpractice files | **7 years** | Director General's office, confidential | |
| Certificate register (all issued certificates) | **permanently** | Director General's office | verification for life |

**Handling.** Scripts and recordings are confidential. A candidate (or a parent of a
minor) may **inspect** the candidate's own script in the presence of an officer; no
copy is released. Recordings are not released. Evidence under appeal is **frozen**
and not destroyed on schedule until the appeal closes. Destruction at end of term is
**recorded**, with what, when and on whose authority.

# PART 14 — WHAT THIS POLICY DOES NOT SETTLE

Recorded, not concealed:

1. **The 15-juz target.** `MASTER-ALLOCATION-MATRIX-v4.md` §2: funded acquisition
   yields **12.7 juz**, against a certificate wording of fifteen. This policy makes
   the G11 instrument a **quality** gate on a **declared** corpus so that it remains
   valid under either resolution — but **the number on the الثانوية القرآنية
   certificate is a Director General's decision, not an assessment decision.**
2. **G12 load.** The Director General's ruling stands. This policy's Part 3 ceiling of
   four internal G12 papers is built for the certification year the corpus describes;
   if the ruling changes, Part 3 is amended.
3. **WAEC's zero timetabled minutes** while being a certificate condition. Part 6
   treats it as a Section C condition; **it remains unfunded in the timetable.**
4. **The booklist/madhhab conflict.** الفقه booklists are monolithically Mālikī while
   the constitution declares أهل السنة with no exclusive school. **Assessment cannot
   fix this and this policy does not pretend to** — but it does refuse to let the
   examination entrench it: **every فقه paper from G9 upward must contain at least one
   مسألة خلافية requiring the candidate to name more than one position and give سبب
   الخلاف** (`SPEC-GRID-G9-fiqh-usul.md`, AO4). A comparative paper on a monolithic
   booklist will expose the conflict every single year, which is the correct
   behaviour for an examination.
5. **The child's week** — 54 hours in institutions at age six. Assessment load has
   been rationed as hard as this policy can ration it; the remaining hours are a
   timetable question.
