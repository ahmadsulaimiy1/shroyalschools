## 1. THE FORM BASIS OF THIS PAPER

This is not a فقه paper with some أصول in it. It is a **two-section paper** whose
structure is dictated by the allocation matrix, as follows:

| Science | Form at G9 (matrix v4) | Assessment consequence | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **الفقه** | **● مستقل** | entitled to a terminal paper; carries the Section A line | **القسم الأول — ٧٠** |
| **أصول الفقه** | **E مضمّن** — sub-form **E‑ع مضمّن مُعلَّم** | may never set its own paper; carries a **named, separately-weighted section inside the host's paper** | **القسم الثاني — ٣٠** |

**Why 70/30 and not something else.** أصول الفقه at G9 is taught inside الفقه
lessons at **zero additional periods** — it has no timetable footprint of its own.
`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §2.1 caps an E‑ع section at **35%** of the host paper, above
which the science must be re-declared مدمج at the next matrix revision. Thirty
percent sits below that cap with deliberate headroom, and it is proportionate to a
science that this year is «قراءةً لا إنشاءً» — read, not performed.

**The section floor applies.** Under `ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §2.1, the أصول section
carries a floor of **40% of its own marks = 12/30**. A candidate at or above the
paper pass mark but below 12 in the أصول section is recorded
**«ناجح بمعالجة في قسم الأصول»** and must clear the floor at re-sit before the
certificate issues. **The floor is what prevents a candidate passing «الفقه وأصوله»
while knowing no أصول at all.**

**وحدة الفرائض is counted once.** الفرائض is a `U` unit inside الفقه at G9. Under
`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §2.1, a وحدة **declared in a specification grid as a content
domain of a papered subject** is examined inside that paper and reported in Section A
with it, and does **not** also appear in transcript Section B. **It is declared here
as domain د٥, carrying 14 marks.** Any transcript showing الفرائض in Section B *and*
inside this paper is void.

**Conditional clause, carried forward from the lifecycle record.** وحدة الفرائض at
G9 is conditional on the 26/28-subject register not containing a separate علم
المواريث entry. It does not (matrix v4). **The unit therefore stands, and د٥ is
live.** If a future register creates علم المواريث as a subject, د٥ is surrendered to
it and its 14 marks are redistributed د٣ +6 / د٤ +8.

## 2. SYLLABUS COVERED BY THIS PAPER

**Set text:** **الرسالة — ابن أبي زيد القيرواني** (completion of the core at G9),
standing on **المقدمة الأخضرية** completed at G7.

**Madhhab posture — binding on every item in this paper.** The institution is
**أهل السنة with no exclusive school**, and فقه is taught **comparatively, with
evidence**. الرسالة is the **set text**, not the **arbiter**. Therefore:

- An item may require a candidate to state **what الرسالة says**. It may never
  require him to state «الحكم» as though one school's position were the only one.
- Where an item asks for a ruling in a genuinely disputed area, the mark scheme
  **must credit any position held by a recognised school of أهل السنة**, provided the
  candidate attaches evidence or names the holder. **Marks are for the reasoning and
  the attribution, not for landing on the setter's own madhhab.**
- **At least one item every year (المجموعة د) must require a مسألة خلافية** — the
  positions, who held what, and **سبب الخلاف**. This is mandatory and is the single
  instrument by which this examination refuses to entrench the booklists' monolithic
  Mālikī default while the constitution declares otherwise
  (`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` Part 14 §4).

### OUT OF SYLLABUS — an item touching any of these is struck by the scrutineer

1. **Any أصول terminology not listed above.** No العام والخاص, no المجمل والمبين,
   no الظاهر والمؤول, no النسخ, no التعارض والترجيح, no حد العلم والظن. **الورقات
   begins at G10.** These are the commonest way a G9 أصول section drifts into a G10
   paper.
2. **القياس مُنشَأً.** The student **reads** قياس this year; he does not **perform**
   it. No item may require a candidate to construct a new قياس on an unseen case.
3. **بحث حجية الإجماع** — its authority as a contested question.
4. **علم المواريث beyond the unit:** no الحجب, no العول, no الرد, no ذوو الأرحام,
   no المناسخات, no الجد والإخوة. **Simple division cases only.**
5. **Sexual detail** anywhere in فقه الأسرة. The G9 treatment is descriptive and
   institutional: who the parties are, what the conditions are, what the periods are.
6. **Personal فتوى.** No item may invite the candidate to rule on a real, named or
   identifiable person's situation, or to advise a party.
7. **التكفير and نواقض الإسلام.** These belong to العقيدة under its own protective
   framing. **No item in this paper may ask a candidate to classify a person.**
8. **Arithmetic beyond the candidate's own hand.** الفرائض cases are constructed so
   that the common denominator is ≤ 24 (see §5, د٥).
9. **Content from the morning national stream.** The institution does not teach and
   does not examine maths, science or English. A فرائض item that is really a
   fractions test is struck.

## 3. THE ASSESSMENT OBJECTIVES — أهداف التقويم

Four objectives. They are exhaustive: **every mark on this paper belongs to exactly
one AO**, and the mark scheme labels each mark with its AO.

| AO | العربية | English | Definition — what the candidate must actually do | **Weight** | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **هـ١** | **الاسترجاع** | **Recall** | Reproduce accurately, from memory, a definition, a condition, a count, a division, a named category or a quoted نص. **No processing is required and none is credited.** | **٢٥٪** | **25** |
| **هـ٢** | **الفهم والبيان** | **Comprehension** | Explain a term, distinguish two things the candidate might confuse, restate a ruling's content in his own Arabic, say *why* a condition exists. | **٢٥٪** | **25** |
| **هـ٣** | **التطبيق** | **Application** | Take a **described, fictional** case and reach the correct outcome by applying held rulings — including the division of an estate. **The case must be one the candidate has not met.** | **٢٨٪** | **28** |
| **هـ٤** | **التحليل والاستدلال والخلاف** | **Analysis, evidence and disagreement** | Attach a ruling to its دليل and name the دليل's type; state a مسألة خلافية, **name who held what**, and explain **سبب الخلاف**; weigh two positions and give reasons. | **٢٢٪** | **22** |

**Tolerance on AO weights: ±2 marks per AO.** A paper outside tolerance is returned.

**Reading the AOs correctly — the three errors setters make:**

- **هـ١ is not "easy".** A candidate who cannot list أصحاب الفروض or the أركان of
  النكاح has not "lost easy marks"; he does not hold the science. 25% is a deliberate
  floor and it is not to be quietly eroded into هـ٢ by writing recall items with
  explanatory tails.
- **هـ٣ requires an UNSEEN case.** A case worked in class, in the textbook, or in a
  past paper is **هـ١ wearing a story**. The scrutineer checks every هـ٣ stem against
  the schemes of work of the three teaching groups.
- **هـ٤ is not "a hard هـ٣".** هـ٤ is about **evidence and disagreement**, not about
  difficulty. An item is هـ٤ only if a correct answer must name a دليل, a holder of a
  position, or a سبب خلاف.

**Domain tolerance: ±2 marks per domain, ±1 per cell, provided AO totals stay
within their ±2 and the paper totals exactly 100.**

**Why د٤ is the largest domain.** فقه الأسرة is the year's own new content — the
completion of الرسالة's core — and every other domain is either cumulative (د١ د٢ د٣)
or a bounded unit (د٥). A paper in which the year's own teaching is not the largest
block is testing the wrong year.

**Why د١ is only 12%.** It is three years of cumulative عبادات. It is sampled, not
surveyed. A paper that gives العبادات 30% because there is a lot of it has converted
a G9 certificate paper into a G7 revision paper.

**Why د٥ carries 8 of its 14 marks in هـ٣.** الفرائض is division. A candidate who can
recite أصحاب الفروض and cannot divide an estate has not learnt the unit. **The unit's
own defining performance is the calculation**, and the grid must say so or a setter
will write six recall items about it and call the unit assessed.

**Paper totals: 24 response points · 100 marks · 150 minutes.**

**Why group (ز) carries no choice.** The أصول section is 30 marks across four
domains. An option between a د٨ question and a د٩ question would let every candidate
avoid one of the two, and a domain that half the cohort never answers is a domain the
grid claims to assess and does not. **The section is too small to carry optionality.**
Group (ز) is therefore one compulsory question in two parts, one part per domain.

### Rules governing the structure

1. **Choice exists in exactly ONE place on the whole paper — group (د) — and nowhere
   else.** Every other item is compulsory. *Rationale:* wide choice destroys
   comparability between candidates and lets a candidate avoid a whole domain. A
   single choice, **between two options of identical tariff, identical AO profile and
   identical content domain**, preserves fairness for the candidate who prepared one
   dispute deeply without letting anyone skip anything the grid claims to assess.
2. **Paired options must be of equal demand AND of the same domain.** Both options in
   group (د) carry 14 marks, both sit in **د٤ فقه الأسرة**, both are هـ٤-dominant at
   exactly 8 marks, and both demand positions + holders + سبب الخلاف + an applied
   case. A setter offering a 14-mark analysis against a 14-mark narration, or a د٤
   option against a د٣ option, has set a trap and broken the domain reconciliation;
   the paper is returned.
3. **Group (ج) is never optional.** الفرائض is the year's unit and every candidate
   attempts the division.
4. **Mark-per-minute discipline.** The paper runs at **0.67 marks/minute**. A
   scrutineer times the paper by writing a full-mark answer himself: if it takes him
   more than **50 minutes**, the paper is too long for a fifteen-year-old writing in
   Arabic and is returned.
5. **The أصول section is printed on its own pages, after a page break, under its own
   heading** «**القسم الثاني — أصول الفقه — ٣٠ درجة**», with its own total printed
   at the end. The candidate must be able to see that it is a separate, separately
   weighted section — because it is, and because the section floor will be applied
   to it.

## 6. ITEM-WRITING RULES — قواعد صياغة الأسئلة

These are the rules that make two setters' papers comparable. They are checked
item-by-item by the scrutineer at stage 2.

### 6.1 Command words — أفعال الطلب

**A setter uses these words and no others.** Each carries a fixed AO and a fixed
expectation. A paper using a command word outside this table is returned.

| Command | AO | What the candidate must produce | Typical tariff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **اذكر / عدِّد** | هـ١ | a list, no explanation credited | 2 |
| **عرِّف** | هـ١ | a definition in the technical sense | 2 |
| **اكتب نص…** | هـ١ | an accurate quotation | 2 |
| **بيِّن / اشرح** | هـ٢ | an explanation with a reason | 2–6 |
| **فرِّق بين … و…** | هـ٢ | both terms + the operative difference | 2–6 |
| **لماذا اشتُرط…** | هـ٢ | the purpose the condition serves | 2–6 |
| **ما حكم … مع بيان السبب** | هـ٣ | the ruling + the rule applied | 2–6 |
| **اقسم التركة** | هـ٣ | a worked division with stated steps | 12 |
| **طبِّق على الحالة الآتية** | هـ٣ | an outcome reached on an unseen case | 6–12 |
| **استدلّ لـ… وسمِّ نوع الدليل** | هـ٤ | the نص + naming it كتاب/سنة/إجماع/قياس | 6–8 |
| **اذكر الخلاف في… ونسبة كل قول لقائله، وبيِّن سبب الخلاف** | **هـ٤** | **positions + holders + سبب الخلاف** — **the mandatory formula** | **14** |
| **وازن بين القولين** | هـ٤ | two positions weighed with reasons | 10–14 |

### 6.2 Stems

- **Every stem is in Arabic, fully formed, and vowelled where a vowel changes the
  meaning** (e.g. «طَلَّقَ» vs «طُلِّقَ»). A candidate must never lose a mark to a
  typographical ambiguity.
- **No negative stems.** Not «أيّ هذه **ليس** من أركان النكاح». Negatives measure
  reading under time pressure, which is not an AO here.
- **No double-barrelled stems at tariff 2.** One question, one demand.
- **No cueing.** A stem may not contain the answer to a later item. The scrutineer
  reads the paper backwards once, specifically for this.
- **Fictional persons in applied cases carry neutral names** — زيد، عمرو، هند،
  فاطمة، خالد — and are **never identifiable**. No pupil, teacher, family or local
  figure appears in a case, ever.
- **No case invites a judgement about a person's faith, standing or intention.**
  Cases are about acts, contracts and estates.

### 6.3 Presentation

- **Arabic-Indic numerals (١٢٣) for question numbers and marks.** Marks printed in
  brackets at the right of every part: `[٢ درجات]`.
- Font not below **16pt**, line spacing not below 1.5 — the paper is sat by
  fifteen-year-olds after a full morning of school.
- **Running totals printed at the foot of every group** so a candidate can budget.
- **No mark is ever awarded for handwriting or neatness on this paper.** Where a
  script is genuinely illegible, it is read aloud to the marking panel by the
  Examinations Officer and marked on what is heard.

## 7. COMPARABILITY CONTROLS — ضوابط المقارنة بين الدورات

So that the 2027 paper is the same examination as the 2026 paper:

1. **Domain marks are fixed** at §4, within ±2. A setter may not decide that this
   year الفرائض deserves 20.
2. **AO weights are fixed** at §3, within ±2.
3. **Structure is fixed** at §5 — 16 + 8 responses, the same tariffs, the same two
   choices.
4. **Anchor items.** Each year the setter carries **two items of a prior paper**
   (one هـ١ at tariff 2, one هـ٣ at tariff 6), re-worded but of identical demand.
   Cohort performance on the anchors is reported to the external examiner and is the
   evidence base for boundary movement under
   `ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §8.2(3).
5. **The مسألة خلافية rotates on a published cycle** so that no cohort meets the same
   pair twice and no teacher can drill one. **Both options in any cycle are from
   د٤ فقه الأسرة**, per §5 rule 2:
   | Cycle | Group (د) option 1 | Group (د) option 2 |
   |---|---|---|
   | **A** | اشتراط الولي في عقد النكاح | الطلاق الثلاث بلفظ واحد |
   | **B** | أقلّ الصداق وتأجيله | نفقة المطلقة البائن الحائل |
   | **C** | عدد الشهود وشرط العدالة في عقد النكاح | طلاق الغضبان |
   | **D** | حضانة الصغير بعد الفرقة ومدتها | الإشهاد على الرجعة |
   The cycle is published to teachers **as a list**, never as "this year's". The
   **د٣ هـ٤ marks (4)** are carried by a compulsory sub-part of group (ب) ب٣ — a
   minor dispute in المعاملات — so that المعاملات also meets disagreement every year
   without creating a second choice point.
6. **The paper is archived permanently** with its grid, mark scheme, mark
   distribution, anchor data and external examiner's report
   (`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` Part 13).

## 8. INDICATIVE GRADE BOUNDARIES

Published as a **starting point**, to be confirmed or moved by up to 3 marks by the
external examiner on the cohort evidence (`ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md` §8.2).

| Band | Mark | What a script at this boundary looks like on THIS paper |
|---|---|---|
| **امتياز** | **80** | Group (د) answered with positions **and** holders **and** a correct سبب الخلاف; the فرائض division correct including the final shares; أصول answers use the technical vocabulary accurately and within syllabus |
| **جدارة** | **70** | Group (د) has positions and holders but the سبب الخلاف is thin or asserted; فرائض correct in method with a slip in arithmetic; أصول secure on الأدلة الأربعة |
| **إجادة** | **60** | Recall secure; applied cases mostly right on familiar patterns; group (د) is narrative — one position given, no سبب; أصول names the الأدلة but the worked example is weak |
| **مقبول** | **50** | Recall present and usable; applied cases right where the pattern is close to a taught one; group (د) is a single madhhab's ruling restated with no awareness that it is disputed; أصول at or just above the 12/30 floor |
| **لم يبلغ المستوى** | **0–49** | Recall gapped; فرائض not attempted or attempted without method; أصول below the floor |

**The section floor bites independently of the boundary.** A candidate on 62 with
10/30 in أصول is **«ناجح بمعالجة في قسم الأصول»** — not simply إجادة.
