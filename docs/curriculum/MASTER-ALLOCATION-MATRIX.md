**Differentiation point.** NEVER DIFFERENTIATES — because it is never undifferentiated. حفظ القرآن is born مستقل at G1 and dies مستقل at G12. It does not emerge out of a parent subject, it is not a guest inside anyone else's block, and it cannot be مدمج at any grade for a structural reason: it is a HOST. التجويد العملي is مضمّن inside it at G1-G3; المراجعة lives inside it at every grade; الإقراء lives inside it from G9. A subject that hosts others cannot itself be integrated into a third. Asking when ḥifẓ differentiates is asking when the spine differentiates from the skeleton.

But there ARE two genuine internal hinges, and naming them is where the real engineering sits, because the submission names neither.

HINGE 1 — G4: التلقين السمعي → الحفظ من المصحف (نظر ثم حفظ). Why G4 and not G3: at G3 القراءة والتهجي is still at word level (الكلمات — the submission's own G3 entry). Memorizing from the muṣḥaf requires fluent decoding of connected, fully vowelled text including word-shape recognition and وقف placement — which the submission establishes only at G4 (الجمل القصيرة) and consolidates at G5. Move the hinge to G3 and the ring-fenced hour silently converts into reading remediation; worse, the child recites from oral memory while appearing to read, which masks a decoding deficit for years and is extremely hard to detect from the back of a halaqah. Why G4 and not G5: three costs. (i) A year of decoding fluency sits unused. (ii) Acquisition stays capped by teacher airtime — in a halaqah of 25, teacher-voice-bound acquisition gives roughly two minutes of new-text delivery per child, and the muṣḥaf is what breaks that ceiling. (iii) تحفة الأطفال starts at G4 (retained redline 3); pairing the first named tajwīd rules with the first independent reading of the ورد is what makes self-correction possible — delay one and you desynchronise both.

HINGE 2 — G10: التحصيل → الإتقان. The centre of gravity shifts from acquiring new text to guaranteeing the whole stock survives. Why G10 and not G9: G9 is the إعدادية exit and should be certified on the acquisition ladder it has been running for six years; re-basing the subject in its exit year corrupts the exit measure. Why not G11: at ~13-16 juz the review burden crosses the point where a weekly cycle can no longer touch every juz, and a student who hits that wall un-restructured begins losing text from the back faster than he gains at the front — and loses it invisibly, since only the front is being tested. Restructure before the wall, not after.

**Prerequisites.**
- السمع السليم والتمييز الصوتي (functional hearing and auditory discrimination) — assumed at G1, but it must be SCREENED at intake, not assumed. An undetected mild hearing loss is indistinguishable from 'a slow child' in a تلقين halaqah and will be misdiagnosed as such for years. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]
- النطق الصحيح للأصوات العربية غير الموجودة في اليوربا — ح/ه، ع/ء، ق/ك، ص/س، ط/ت، ض/د، ذ/ز/ظ، غ. Established G1-G2 inside the ring-fenced hour by التجويد العملي مضمّن, reinforced by القراءة والتهجي. This is a PREREQUISITE FOR VOLUME, not for beginning: a child may begin ḥifẓ before it is secure, but must not be permitted to accumulate juz before it is, because memorized mispronunciation fossilizes and later costs more to remediate than the original acquisition cost.
- التلقي بالتكرار والقدرة على الترديد المنغّم (imitative repetition with prosodic contour) — present naturally at G1; requires no teaching, only exploitation.
- فكّ الحروف وقراءة النص المشكول المتصل (decoding fully vowelled connected text) — established by القراءة والتهجي: word level at G3, short-sentence level at G4, simple-sentence level at G5. This is the hard gate on HINGE 1 and the single reason the muṣḥaf transition sits at G4.
- معرفة أحكام المد وأحكام النون الساكنة والتنوين والميم الساكنة بأسمائها — established G4-G5 via متن تحفة الأطفال والغلمان (retained redline 3), taught in its own slot outside the hour. Not a prerequisite for memorizing; a prerequisite for SELF-correcting, which is what makes G4+ ḥifẓ self-sustaining rather than teacher-throughput-bound.
- معرفة مخارج الحروف وصفاتها نظرياً — established G6-G7 via المقدمة الجزرية (retained redline 3). Prerequisite for إتقان-grade and إجازة-grade recitation, not for ordinary acquisition.
- معرفة الوقف والابتداء — imitatively from G2, formally from G6-G7 via الجزرية's باب معرفة الوقوف. Becomes load-bearing at G5 when البقرة's long āyāt begin.

**Differentiation point.** G4 — التجويد becomes a distinct named timetabled subject with متن تحفة الأطفال والغلمان. The reason it is G4 and not G3: تحفة's entire first half is rule-triggering on a VISUAL cue — a نون with a sukūn followed by one of a named set of letters, a tanwīn, a shadda. Until the child can reliably see and sort سكون / تنوين / شدة on the page, every rule is memorised noise. القراءة والتهجي reaches الكلمات only at G3 and المفردات والجمل at G4; the analytic category ساكن-versus-متحرك is only auditorily secure at end of G3 (which is why G3 carries the diagnostic screen). Starting at G3 would convert تحفة into a نشيد the child chants without applying, and a first experience of تجويد as meaningless chanting poisons the attitude for years. The reason it is G4 and not G5: articulatory and prosodic habit is still plastic at G4 and hardening fast. Every additional year of daily recitation with غنة-less إخفاء and inconsistent مد lengths adds motor habit that must later be un-learnt — and un-learning costs several times what learning costs. G4 is the last year where the habits are still soft and the first year where the eye can carry the rule. It is also the year with headroom: 1 period out of 19. Note the differentiation is of the SCIENCE only. The practice does not differentiate at G4 — it has been running مضمّن since G1 and continues مضمّن to G12.

**Prerequisites.**
- Reliable letter-sound correspondence and fluent reading of a fully pointed Arabic word — established by القراءة والتهجي, end of G3 (الكلمات stage). Without it تحفة cannot be applied to a page.
- Secure discrimination of ساكن / متحرك / مشدد / منوَّن as both auditory and visual categories — established end of G3 through the Qur'an hour plus القراءة والتهجي, and verified by the G3 diagnostic articulation screen. This is the hard gate on G4.
- A working oral repertoire of memorised Qur'an for the rules to operate on — established continuously from G1 by حفظ القرآن inside the 195-hour ring-fenced hour; sufficient stock by end of G3.
- Capacity to memorise رجز verse — established by G3 through النشيد والمحفوظات (منظومة التوحيد والإيمان at G3 in the submission). This is a real and often-missed dependency: تحفة is a 61-line rajaz poem, and G3's نشيد work is precisely what makes it tractable at G4.
- Arabic vocabulary sufficient for the matn's technical terms to be GLOSSED (not construed) — partial from اللغة العربية at G4-G5; needed at a higher level for the الصفات lexicon (الهمس، الجهر، الإطباق، الاستعلاء، الإذلاق، الإصمات، التفشي، الاستطالة) at G6, established by اللغة العربية G5-G6.
- Basic نحو — recognition of جملة اسمية/فعلية، الإسناد، المبتدأ والخبر، الفاعل، تمام المعنى — established by الآجرومية at G6 and ملحة الإعراب at G7. This is the sole prerequisite for الوقف والابتداء and the reason that content sits at G8, not G6-G7.
- Elementary صرف — أصلي vs زائد, and لام الفعل vs لام الاسم — established by البناء في الصرف at G6. A minor but real feed into lām rules and إدغام المتقاربين.
- Sufficient meaning-access to the text being recited to judge where a sense completes — established by التفسير from G6 onward; feeds the G8 وقف unit.
- Ability to hear, name and type another person's error — built G6-G8 by الجزرية plus the وقف unit; prerequisite for the G11 إقراء unit.

**Differentiation point.** NEVER DIFFERENTIATES into a مستقل subject — and that is correct, not a deficiency. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION] Decoding divorced from the text being decoded is dead drill; the entire pedagogical power of this subject is that the child decodes the very sūrah being memorised that week. Making it مستقل would (a) sever decoding from its purpose and (b) claim periods from a ceiling that cannot pay — at G1-G2 a standalone slot would consume roughly 3 of only 7 available periods.

It does, however, have an INTERNAL PIVOT at G4: the form stays مدمج but the curricular object changes from التهجي (building the decoding system) to القراءة (fluency plus Uthmanic orthographic convention).

Why G4 and not G3: تحفة الأطفال والغلمان becomes مستقل and independently assessed at G4 (retained redline 3). أحكام النون الساكنة والتنوين are unteachable to a child who cannot already SEE a نون ساكنة in the text and identify the following letter. G4 is therefore the first year decoding can be declared finished — which is precisely why the decoding gate sits at end of G3.

Why G4 and not G5: decoding does not need five years. Three years at ~18 min/day is roughly 175 hours of instruction, far more than a قاعدة requires; madrasa practice routinely completes it in 6-18 months. Holding decoding open to G5 produces the opposite failure from the one the submission fears — children plateau at word-by-word reading, never build phrase-level fluency, and then cannot sustain tajwid-governed تلاوة. Over-extending decoding is itself a pedagogical failure.

**Prerequisites.**
- Auditory discrimination of the Arabic phonemes absent from Yoruba and English (ح ع ق ص ض ط ظ غ خ). Established: G1 Term 1, orally, INSIDE this subject — it is its own first prerequisite, built rather than assumed, and must not be presupposed from the home or from prior slate-school attendance.
- Visual discrimination of dot-pattern minimal pairs (ب ت ث / ج ح خ / د ذ / ر ز / س ش / ص ض / ط ظ / ع غ / ف ق / ن ي). Established: G1 Term 1.
- Right-to-left directional tracking, taught in explicit contrast with the Latin-script literacy the child is acquiring concurrently in the morning national curriculum. Established: G1 Term 1. [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] Yoruba orthography's use of subdots and tone diacritics is a genuine ASSET here — the child already holds the concept that a mark above or below a letter changes its sound, which transfers directly to الحركات and التنوين and argues for early rather than deferred vowel work.
- Positional letter forms (مبتدئة / متوسطة / متطرفة / منفصلة). Established: G1 Term 3.
- Grapheme-vowel synthesis with الحركات الثلاث and السكون. Established: G1 Terms 2-3.
- التنوين, الشدة, المد الطبيعي. Established: G2.
- همزة الوصل/القطع, اللام الشمسية والقمرية at reading level, التاء المربوطة. Established: G2.
- The Uthmanic conventions that are not phonetic — الألف الفارقة، الألف الخنجرية، الحروف الزائدة، صلة هاء الضمير، علامات الوقف. Established: G3 (recognition) and G4 (systematic). These cannot be inferred by a child from a phonetic reading system and must be explicitly taught, or the child invents a wrong rule and defends it.
- A small stock of known words, so decoding can be cross-checked against meaning rather than produced blind. Supplied by اللغة العربية: الحروف at G1-G2, الكلمات at G3, المفردات والجمل at G4-G5 — the submission's sequence aligns correctly with this subject's needs.
- NEGATIVE PREREQUISITE, load-bearing: النحو is NOT a prerequisite at any point, because the muṣḥaf is fully vocalised. This is exactly why the subject can and must complete BEFORE النحو begins (مضمّن at G5, الآجرومية at G6) — and why unvocalised reading, which genuinely does require الصرف and الإعراب, cannot belong to this subject.

Every body of knowledge it might claim already belongs to a named neighbour — metre to العروض, figurative analysis to البلاغة, literary judgement and history to النقد والآداب, delivery and composition to الإنشاء والخطابة, the technical متون to التجويد/النحو/الصرف, the Qur'an to the ring-fenced hour. The ear must precede the scansion; invert that order and العروض becomes an abstract exercise in counting.

**Differentiation point.** G6 — the submission is CORRECT and is protected here, but for a reason it does not state. التفسير differentiates at G6 because three enabling competencies land in that single year and not before: (1) الآجرومية [RETAINED, redline 3] supplies the إعراب apparatus — مبتدأ وخبر، فاعل ومفعول به، الجار والمجرور، علامات الإعراب — which is the minimum vocabulary for saying WHY a verse means what it means rather than merely WHAT it means; (2) البناء في الصرف [RETAINED, redline 3] supplies الميزان الصرفي and الاشتقاق, so the pupil can connect Qur'anic word families (غفر / غفور / غفار / مغفرة) instead of learning each as an isolated gloss; (3) five years of مضمّن meaning-attachment mean the first tafsir course explains a corpus the pupil already carries in memory and already half-understands, so the cognitive load is the analysis, not the content.

NOT G5: at G5 النحو is only مضمّن and no إعراب terminology exists. A timetabled tafsir at G5 could only deliver translation-by-dictation — the pupil would memorize a Yoruba rendering as a second text alongside the Arabic. That is not a slow version of التفسير; it is the opposite of it, and it would inoculate against the G6 course by making it feel like revision of something already "known."

NOT G7: delaying to G7 would leave G6's grammar and morphology learned for a full year without the Qur'an as their object, which is their strongest motivating application and the one the pupils actually care about. It would also push الترجمة's start past G6, contradicting redline 8 [CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION], which fixes الترجمة's beginning at G6 riding on التفسير العملي.

**Role at G12.** At G12 التفسير is the integrating subject of the entire twelve-year curriculum — the single place where نحو، صرف، بلاغة، حديث، فقه، أصول الفقه، أصول التفسير، عقيدة and سيرة are all brought to bear on one verse, and where their absence is immediately visible. It is مستقل, 3 periods, independently assessed, and it carries the graduation capstone.

What the graduate can actually do because of it:
- Take an unseen passage of the Qur'an, parse it (إعراب), identify the غريب, and state its meaning in Arabic from the text rather than from memory.
- Read تفسير الجلالين and an abridged تفسير ابن كثير unaided, in Arabic, and follow the argument rather than the conclusion.
- State whether a passage is مكي or مدني and what follows from that; give a سبب نزول where one is established and say so when none is.
- Distinguish تفسير بالمأثور from تفسير بالرأي; recognize an unsourced narration in a tafsir and decline to repeat it as fact — the direct yield of the G9 الإسرائيليات work.
- Read a disagreement between two مفسرين, state what each holds, on what evidence, and whether the difference is اختلاف تنوع or اختلاف تضاد.
- Assemble a theme across the Qur'an (التفسير الموضوعي) rather than only reading one sura through.
- Prepare and deliver a تفسير session to a lay audience: in Arabic, then rendered accurately into English and Yoruba. This is the capstone assessment and it is also the actual social function the community will judge. It is assessed on accuracy of meaning, evidence cited, handling of difference, and absence of unsupported narration — not on fluency of delivery, which belongs to الخطابة.

What it is explicitly NOT: it does not certify ijāzah (that is the parallel opt-in track, redline 2); it does not make the graduate a مفسر; it does not close the subject. It produces someone equipped to continue in tafsir at a higher institution and competent to render the Qur'an's meaning responsibly in the meantime.

**Differentiation point.** G11 — مستقل, with مقدمة ابن تيمية as its matn.

WHY NOT EARLIER THAN G11 (the binding argument): أصول التفسير is a consumer discipline. It does not generate its own apparatus; it borrows from two other second-order disciplines and spends its own time on what is proper to it. Both suppliers land at G10.

(a) مصطلح الحديث differentiates at G10-G11 [CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION, accepted redline 1]. أصول التفسير's largest single chapter is التفسير بالمأثور. Taught before مصطلح الحديث, that chapter can only be taught devotionally — "the Prophet said," "Ibn Abbas said" — with no capacity to ask whether the report is established. That produces a learner who accepts any attributed tafsir report, which is worse than a learner who has no rules at all, because he now has confidence. The الإسرائيليات chapter is unteachable without it: the whole rule of تصديق/تكذيب/توقف presupposes the ability to weigh a transmitted report.

(b) أصول الفقه [COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED — my recommendation is G10] owns العام والخاص، المطلق والمقيد، المجمل والمبين، النسخ، دلالات الألفاظ. These are the SAME chapters, not similar ones. Placed at G10, أصول التفسير must either re-teach them from scratch (duplication the board has explicitly warned against) or teach them without their natural home. Placed at G11, it inherits them as owned tools and spends its two periods on what is genuinely its own: اختلاف التنوع والتضاد، الإسرائيليات، العبرة بعموم اللفظ، مناهج المفسرين، حكم الترجمة.

There is also a load argument, not merely a logical one: G10 already receives two new meta-disciplines with overlapping vocabulary. A third in the same year guarantees conflation.

WHY NOT LATER THAN G11: G12 must be the year the method is USED, not acquired. A discipline whose entire purpose is to govern a practice cannot be introduced in the terminal year and left unpractised — the graduate would leave having been examined on rules he never applied to an unfamiliar passage under supervision. One full year of supervised application is the minimum, and there is only one year after G11. Deferring acquisition to G12 would also leave the G6-G11 translation practice (redline 8) ungoverned for its entire run and then hand the learner its rules as he walks out.

A NOTE ON WHAT DIFFERENTIATES: G9 already gives the subject its first independent mark, but as a وحدة, not a differentiation — a bounded descriptive unit that ends. G11 is where it becomes a named discipline with a matn and a progression. The distinction matters: G9 supplies vocabulary, G11 supplies method.

**Role at G12.** FORM AT G12: مدمج with التفسير, one traceable period a week drawn from التفسير's existing block, independently assessed by capstone. The discipline does not survive G12 as a separate entity — and that is correct, not a demotion. أصول التفسير is a مقدمة discipline: acquired compactly once (G11), then permanently in service. Keeping it مستقل at G12 would force the invention of filler content.

WHAT G12 ACTUALLY DOES: sustained reading of real tafsir with the G11 apparatus in hand — ابن كثير as the مأثور exemplar against السعدي as the accessible contrast, with selected extracts from الطبري (to see a mufassir weigh reports and state a preference with a reason) and القرطبي (to see a fiqh ruling actually derived from an آية). Added as G12's own content: مناهج المفسرين as a classification applied to what is being read; the contemporary abuses named and refuted — التفسير العلمي المتكلف and the reading of modern ideology into the text; and a critique of the institution's designated English and Yoruba renderings against the Arabic.

WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO — stated as assessable performance on an UNSEEN passage of 10-20 verses, not as aspiration:
(a) State the meaning of the passage in Arabic and render it into English and Yoruba.
(b) For each meaning given, NAME ITS SOURCE CLASS — قرآن، سنة، قول صحابي، لغة العرب، اجتهاد — and be marked wrong for a meaning with no stated basis.
(c) Locate at least one parallel موضع elsewhere in the Qur'an bearing on the passage, from his own hifz.
(d) Report the sabab al-nuzul where one is established, and apply العبرة بعموم اللفظ لا بخصوص السبب correctly — i.e. not restrict a general ruling to its occasion.
(e) Where مفسرون differ, classify the difference as اختلاف تنوع or اختلاف تضاد, and say what follows in each case.
(f) Identify an إسرائيلية or a تفسير علمي متكلف when it appears in a commentary he is reading, and say on what ground he sets it aside.
(g) Take a published English or Yoruba translation of the passage and state SPECIFICALLY what the translation commits to that the Arabic leaves open.
(h) Say لا أدري, and say exactly where his competence stops and whom the question should go to.

Item (h) is the real terminal outcome of this subject and must carry marks. The purpose of teaching أصول التفسير in a school is not to produce mufassirūn at eighteen. It is to produce a graduate who knows what interpretation requires, can recognise when it has been done badly, and will not do it badly himself — including by knowing when not to do it at all. A graduate who leaves confident that he can interpret the Qur'an has been failed by this subject; a graduate who leaves able to say precisely why he cannot yet has been served by it.

**Differentiation point.** This subject differentiates in the opposite direction from every other subject on the register, and the board should not read its entry as if it behaved like the others. اللغة العربية is not a discipline that emerges out of a parent block — it IS the parent block. It is a distinct named entity from G1, and النحو، الصرف، الإملاء، الإنشاء والخطابة، البلاغة، العروض، النقد والآداب and الترجمة all differentiate OUT OF IT over twelve years. So the analytically meaningful event for this subject is not its birth but its point of maximal narrowing, and that is G6.

WHY G6: الآجرومية arrives at G6 and البناء في الصرف arrives at G6 (both retained by accepted redline 3). From the moment a matn-based النحو subject exists on the timetable, continuing to teach grammar inside اللغة العربية is straight duplication and, worse, it teaches two incompatible grammars — a functional one in the language lesson and a definitional one in the matn — to the same child in the same week. At G6 اللغة العربية sheds grammar entirely and is redefined as the comprehension-and-production subject whose job is to make the matns readable.

WHY NOT G5: because at G5 there is no matn yet, and the functional grammar taught مضمّن inside the language lesson at G5 is exactly what makes G6 survivable. الآجرومية opens with definitions — الكلام هو اللفظ المركب المفيد بالوضع، أقسامه ثلاثة: اسم وفعل وحرف. A student who has spent G5 identifying اسم، فعل، حرف، مبتدأ، خبر، فاعل in live sentences meets those definitions as names for things he already recognises. A student who has not, meets them as sounds. Moving the handoff to G5 removes precisely the year that makes the matn teachable.

WHY NOT G7: because by G7 ملحة الإعراب and لامية الأفعال are already running. If اللغة العربية still carried grammar through G6, the student would meet الآجرومية cold, spend G6 failing it, and then be handed a harder matn. The handoff must sit exactly on the G5→G6 boundary — one year of functional grammar, then out.

A second, smaller differentiation event, recorded for completeness: الإملاء is claimed by this subject from G3 as a مضمّن strand under accepted redline 7 and is never released, because it has no independent life as a timetable subject.

**Prerequisites.**
- Normal L1 (Yoruba) oral development and school readiness — assumed at entry, G1. اللغة العربية is the entry subject of Programme 2 and deliberately presupposes no Arabic.
- Letter-sound decoding of fully vocalised Arabic script — established by القراءة والتهجي, G1-G2, reinforced through G3. اللغة العربية must never ask a child to read a form القراءة والتهجي has not yet given him; the two syllabi are synchronised week by week, and this is a scheduling obligation on both subjects, not a wish.
- Accurate articulation of the Arabic phonemes absent from Yoruba and English — ع ح خ ص ض ط ظ غ ق ذ ث — established by practical التجويد مضمّن inside the ring-fenced Qur'an hour from G1. [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] This is a substantial free gift from the institution's own design that the submission does not name: in most Arabic-as-L2 settings the language teacher has to build this phonology himself and it consumes months. Here 195 hours a year of supervised recitation does it. اللغة العربية should assume it and not rebuild it.
- Arabic handwriting — letter formation and accurate copying. Claimed by this subject from G2 unless الخط exists as a separate register entry. Prerequisite for any written assessment from G3.
- Fluent oral reading of vocalised connected text at phrase level — established by القراءة والتهجي by end of G3. Prerequisite for the paragraph-level work at G4.
- A working productive vocabulary of roughly 1,000 words — established by اللغة العربية itself at G4. Prerequisite for the functional grammar of G5, because grammar taught on words the child does not know is grammar taught on nothing.
- Functional grammar: اسم/فعل/حرف، الجملة الاسمية والفعلية، المبتدأ والخبر، الفعل والفاعل، الضمائر، الإفراد والتثنية والجمع، التذكير والتأنيث، الماضي والمضارع والأمر — established by اللغة العربية itself at G5 (مضمّن). Prerequisite for الآجرومية and البناء في الصرف at G6.
- Root awareness (الجذر الثلاثي) and basic pattern recognition — established by الصرف at G6-G7 (البناء في الصرف, لامية الأفعال). Prerequisite for dictionary skills, which اللغة العربية teaches at G7.
- Reading of vocalised expository prose with inferential comprehension — established by اللغة العربية at G6. Prerequisite for the unvocalised transition at G8 and, more urgently, for every matn-based subject from G6 onward.
- Independent reading of unvocalised authentic prose — established by اللغة العربية at G8-G9. Prerequisite for the entire secondary stage: أصول الفقه، مصطلح الحديث (نخبة الفكر)، أصول التفسير، البلاغة، النقد والآداب all assume it from G10.

**Role at G12.** At G12 اللغة العربية has no timetabled form, and that is the mark of success rather than of neglect. Its function — carrying a Yoruba-speaking child from no Arabic to autonomous comprehension and production of unvocalised standard Arabic — is complete at the end of G9, maintained through G10 inside النقد والآداب and extended by the G11 modern-register unit. By G12 Arabic is not a subject; it is the medium in which أصول الفقه، البلاغة، مصطلح الحديث، أصول التفسير، النقد والآداب، الإنشاء والخطابة and the Islamic sciences are taught, read, discussed and examined. Retaining a general 'Arabic lesson' at G12 beside البلاغة، النقد والآداب and الإنشاء والخطابة would duplicate all three and take periods from disciplines that are still building.

The assessment does not disappear with the form. A comprehensive Arabic proficiency examination — reading, listening, speaking, writing — is sat as part of the G12 exit profile, and this subject owns the exit standard even though it owns no periods.

WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO BECAUSE OF THIS SUBJECT: read an unvocalised page of classical or modern Arabic prose at reading speed, opening a dictionary only for technical terms, and know how to open it — by root. Sit in an Arabic lecture or khutbah, follow it, and take notes in Arabic. Hold a sustained spoken conversation, answer an unprepared question, and be examined orally. Write several connected pages of controlled Arabic prose — a research paper, a formal letter, an essay — that an Arab reader would accept as written by someone educated rather than as translated. Read a contemporary Arabic newspaper or academic article, not only turāth. And present a certified proficiency result to an institution outside Nigeria.

That last item is the difference between a graduate who has studied Arabic texts and a graduate who has Arabic.

**Prerequisites.**

Supplied continuously and at no period cost by the ring-fenced حفظ hour; sufficient in volume from G6, exploited systematically from G7. This is the institution's structural advantage over a conventional school and the design should be built to spend it.

**Role at G12.** At G12 النحو stops being a body of content and becomes the graduate's working instrument, at reduced weight (2 periods) but retained identity. That unit exists so a graduate reading any شرح in any Islamic science can tell a GRAMMATICAL argument from a legal or theological one and not be lost in it. WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO BECAUSE OF THIS SUBJECT: (a) take an unseen, unvoweled printed page of classical prose — فقه, حديث, تفسير — vowel it correctly, and defend every ending they assigned; this is the certifying performance and it is assessed as such; (b) إعراب any verse of the Qur'an, including إعراب الجمل and whether a clause has a محل من الإعراب — cashing twelve years of ring-fenced حفظ into comprehension rather than storage; (c) read a نحوي's argument in a شرح and state what is at stake, without needing to adjudicate it; (d) find and fix a syntactic error in their own and another's Arabic writing, giving the reason; (e) hold ملحة الإعراب memorised in full and a defined core of الألفية, so that the reference travels with them and does not depend on a library; (f) lead a reading, teach a beginner matn, or enter a tertiary Arabic or Shariah programme with no remedial نحو. The negative statement matters as much: the G12 graduate is NOT a نحوي and the programme should not pretend otherwise. Original grammatical scholarship, full الألفية حفظ, and مغني اللبيب belong to the parallel إجازة track and to post-secondary study.

**Differentiation point.** There are two distinct events and the board should not conflate them. (1) الصرف becomes a DISTINCT NAMED ENTITY at G6 — its own name on the register, its own matn (متن البناء), its own mark — while still taught مدمج with النحو. It is G6 and not G5 because الميزان الصرفي is an algebraic operation: ف ع ل must be held as positional placeholders that stand for any three consonants, and a learner who cannot do that treats الوزن as letter-matching and collapses at the first مزيد (he cannot weigh اسْتَخْرَجَ). That capacity for symbolic placeholding arrives around age eleven, i.e. G6 here. It is not G7 because الآجرومية lands at G6 [redline 3] and its whole treatment of علامات الفعل، المعرب والمبني، نصب وجزم المضارع، الأفعال الخمسة presupposes that the learner can identify the morphological forms being inflected; delay الميزان to G7 and G6's نحو becomes recitation. (2) الصرف DIFFERENTIATES INTO مستقل at G8, and holds that standing for exactly two years. It is G8 because that is when الإعلال والإبدال والمعتل arrive, and that body of content demands a second and harder abstraction than the ف ع ل placeholder — accepting an underlying form that never surfaces (قَالَ is analysed as قَوَلَ). Attempt that before the صحيح paradigms are automatic and the underlying form is not a simplification, it is a competing item to memorise, and the student doubles his load and hypercorrects. The صحيح paradigms become automatic at the end of G7. It is not G9 because المعتل alone — مثال، أجوف، ناقص، لفيف by two kinds, each with its أمر، مجزوم، مبني للمجهول ومشتقات — is a full year of work, and مصطلح الحديث differentiates at G10–G11 [redline 1] with a terminology that is itself a morphological system (مُدلِّس vs مُدلَّس، مُرسِل vs مُرسَل، مَجروح vs جارح). Compress إعلال and معتل into G9 alone and the G10 redline placement inherits students who recognise forms but cannot produce them. It is also not مدمج at G8 because in every integrated block the discipline holding the examined text wins the minutes, and against الآجرومية's successor النحو would win every week.

**Prerequisites.**
- Automatic decoding of vocalised three- and four-letter words with no hesitation — established G3 by القراءة والتهجي (G1–G3), consolidated G4–G5. This is why G3 carries no صرف: the periods that would fund it are the periods that fund this.
- Accurate auditory and written discrimination of الحركات والسكون والشدة. الصرف is a vowel-melody system; a learner who cannot hear the difference between كَتَبَ / كُتِبَ / كَتَّبَ cannot do it at all. Established G2–G3 by القراءة والتهجي and, at no cost to this subject, by التجويد العملي مضمّن (G1–G5).
- A productive stock of roughly 300–500 Arabic words including at least 60–80 verbs. A pattern is a generalisation over a vocabulary; with twenty verbs there is no generalisation available, only labels to memorise. Established G4–G5 by اللغة العربية (المفردات والجمل القصيرة/البسيطة).
- Handwriting fluent enough to fill a conjugation table without the writing itself consuming the lesson. Established G4.
- The trichotomy اسم / فعل / حرف. Formally delivered by الآجرومية at G6, but it must be pre-taught inside اللغة العربية at G5 [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION] so that متن البناء at G6 is not waiting on its partner text to catch up.
- Capacity to hold a symbolic placeholder — ف ع ل standing for any three consonants, by position not by letter. This is the cognitive gate for الميزان الصرفي and it is why الميزان is at G6 and not G4–G5. Established G6.
- Tolerance for an underlying form that never surfaces (قَالَ analysed as قَوَلَ). A strictly harder abstraction than the placeholder, and the gate for الإعلال. Established G8 — which is why G8 is the مستقل point.
- Memorisation capacity sufficient for a versified matn. Built continuously and for free by the ring-fenced Qur'an hour (G1–G12) and by النشيد والمحفوظات (G3+). This is what makes لامية الأفعال deliverable at G7.

**Role at G12.** At G12 الصرف is no longer a subject being learned; it is a capability being exercised and certified, delivered through a bounded one-term وحدة (≈1 period/week) that revises the whole syllabus to external-examination and إجازة standard and applies it to the Qur'anic text. Concretely, the graduate can: take any Arabic word from a vocalised or unvocalised text and state its وزن، جذر، صيغة، and what the صيغة contributes to the meaning; conjugate any verb — صحيح or معتل, مجرد or مزيد — across الماضي والمضارع والأمر، معلوماً ومجهولاً، مثبتاً ومنفياً, unaided and for roots he has never met; find any word in a root-ordered dictionary including where إعلال or إبدال has buried the root (اتَّقَى ← و ق ي، قُلْ ← ق و ل، مِيزَان ← و ز ن، ازْدَجَرَ ← ز ج ر), which is the competency that makes him able to keep learning for the rest of his life without a teacher; read the technical vocabularies of مصطلح الحديث، أصول الفقه and البلاغة as transparent rather than opaque; explain a صرفي distinction in a Qur'anic verse — why غَفُور and not غَافِر, the مبالغة weight of غَفَّار, مَالِك against مَلِك as اسم فاعل against صفة مشبهة — and render that distinction accurately into English and Yoruba; produce correct derived forms in his own writing and speech in الإنشاء والخطابة; and carry متن البناء and لامية الأفعال in memory as a permanently available internal reference, which is what a matn is for and the reason the tradition versified this science in the first place.

**Differentiation point.** G10 — البلاغة becomes مستقل, with its own book, its own paper and its own progression.

Why G10 and not G9: علم المعاني is the core of the discipline and is wholly parasitic on consolidated نحو. المعاني asks why a مسند إليه is معرفة rather than نكرة, مقدّم rather than مؤخر, محذوف rather than مذكور; why القصر is by إنما rather than النفي والاستثناء; when to فصل and when to وصل. A student still acquiring نحو cannot answer those questions — he will re-do نحو badly inside the بلاغة slot and be unable to tell which subject he is failing. Under this curriculum نحو runs الآجرومية (G6) → ملحة الإعراب (G7) → consolidation and extension (G8-G9); it is held, not merely met, at the end of G9. G9 is also the year البلاغة is being independently assessed for the first time in مضمّن form — the diagnostic year. You differentiate after the diagnostic, not during it.

Why G10 and not G11: the three sciences plus شواهد work plus application cannot be done in one year at 2 periods — a G11 start yields a survey, and a survey of البلاغة is memorised definitions. G10-G11 gives two clean years for the apparatus and leaves G12 free for application. Also, G11-G12 التفسير and أصول الفقه need البلاغة already in hand rather than arriving alongside them.

A note on the G10 coincidence with مصطلح الحديث [CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION, redline 1]: this is not symmetry-copying. Both are second-order sciences — sciences ABOUT a first-order material (مصطلح about transmission, بلاغة about language) — and both require a consolidated first-order base plus the metalinguistic/formal reasoning that is secure around G10. The same criterion independently lands both in the same place. That is convergence, not imitation, and if the نحو ladder at G8-G9 turns out weaker than assumed, البلاغة moves to G11 regardless of where مصطلح sits. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]

**Prerequisites.**
- Fluent, stable Arabic decoding and reading aloud — established G3-G5 through القراءة والتهجي (separate book, independently assessed). Without automatic decoding, all attention goes to word-recognition and none to the comparison of alternatives.
- A working vocabulary large enough to hold BOTH the literal and the transferred sense of a word at once — built G3-G7 by the المفردات والجمل strand; adequate by G8, secure by G9. This is the specific L2 gate on الاستعارة, not a general 'more Arabic' wish.
- إعراب of the nominal and verbal sentence: المبتدأ والخبر، الفاعل، المفعول، المعرفة والنكرة — established G6 by الآجرومية. Without it the student cannot even see that a construction could have been otherwise, which is the precondition of every بلاغة question.
- The syntactic repertoire that علم المعاني actually operates on: التقديم والتأخير، الحذف، الحال، التمييز، أدوات النفي والاستثناء، الشرط، العطف — established through ملحة الإعراب at G7 and consolidated G8-G9. This is the direct gate on differentiation at G10.
- الاشتقاق، الأوزان، صيغ المبالغة — established G6-G7 through البناء في الصرف and لامية الأفعال. Needed for حسن التعليل، المبالغة، and much of البديع.
- A memorised stock of fine Arabic to serve as شواهد — accumulated continuously: حفظ القرآن G1-G12 (195h/yr, constitutionally ring-fenced, costing البلاغة nothing), الحديث from G3, and the النشيد والمحفوظات strand from G3 (ديوان الشافعي ولامية ابن الوردي at G6, تائية الإلبيري at G7). The two [UNVERIFIED] النشيد slots at G4 and G5 are excluded from this reservoir and nothing is substituted for them.
- Ability to restate an Arabic sentence in different Arabic (إعادة الصياغة) — REQUIRED BY END OF G9 and NOT explicitly guaranteed anywhere in the submission. This is the pivotal prerequisite: if the student cannot produce an alternative wording, he cannot compare wordings, and البلاغة at G10 will collapse into definition-memorisation. - Metalinguistic, formal-operational reasoning — the capacity to treat language as an object, to attribute intention to a speaker, and to judge fit-to-audience (مقتضى الحال). Typically emerging around G8, reliable by G10. [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] — this is a developmental prerequisite, not a curricular one, and it is the reason no amount of clever sequencing can pull البلاغة into the الابتدائية years.
- NOT a prerequisite, contrary to a common assumption: العروض. Knowledge of البحور helps read شواهد شعرية comfortably but is not needed for any of the three sciences. البلاغة must not be made to wait on العروض, and العروض must not be pulled early to serve it.

**Role at G12.** At G12 البلاغة stops being a body of knowledge to acquire and becomes an instrument used on whole texts, delivered مدمج with النقد والآداب and carrying its own reported grade. Its three G12 functions: applied analysis of complete literary texts (with النقد والآداب), a bounded وحدة on نظرية النظم from selected passages of دلائل الإعجاز, and a service function into التفسير and الترجمة — supplying the apparatus without annexing their hours.

What the graduate can actually do because of it:
- Take an unseen passage of Qur'an, hadith, or classical prose or verse and say, in Arabic, what the rhetorical choice is and why it was made: identify التقديم والتأخير، الحذف، القصر، الفصل والوصل، الإيجاز والإطناب، and state the مقتضى الحال that motivates each.
- Distinguish تشبيه from استعارة from كناية from مجاز مرسل, name their divisions, and produce شواهد from memory rather than from a handout.
- Recognise المحسنات البديعية and — the harder and more important skill — explain why they are subordinate to meaning, and identify where a text's ornament has outrun its sense.
- Argue, at an introductory but genuine level, that إعجاز القرآن lies in النظم (the relations between the words) rather than in strange vocabulary, and defend that position with a worked example. This is the single capability that most distinguishes this graduate from one who has merely finished تفسير.
- Carry an Arabic figure into English or Yoruba without literalism, and state precisely what is lost in the transfer — a directly employable skill in a Yoruba-speaking, English-official context.
- Preach, teach or address an audience with conscious attention to مقتضى الحال — matching the form of speech to the hearer. This is البلاغة's oldest practical payoff and its most useful one in the graduate's actual community.
- Sit an entry-level بلاغة placement examination for a تخصص in اللغة العربية or الشريعة, and, if he has taken the opt-in mastery track, present a memorised الجوهر المكنون as evidence of it.

**Differentiation point.** G8 — as a وحدة (one term), NOT as a مستقل subject, and never as a مستقل subject at any grade. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]

WHY NOT ONE YEAR EARLIER (G7): three concrete blockers, each dated. (a) الميزان الصرفي must be fully secure before الميزان العروضي is introduced, or they fuse — the well-known student failure is looking for فاء/عين/لام الكلمة inside فاعلن, or writing فاعِل for فاعلن. لامية الأفعال completes at the end of G7; before that the morphological balance is still being built and the two balances would be met simultaneously. (b) الكتابة العروضية is impossible without a secure written-versus-pronounced distinction (تنوين as نون ساكنة، الشدة as two letters، dropping همزة الوصل في الدرج، letters written but not sounded). That distinction is what الجزرية installs, and الجزرية runs G6-G7. A student who scans before it closes will get almost every line wrong and will conclude the science is arbitrary — the hardest attitude to reverse. (c) G7 is, on the submission's own face, the most loaded year in the whole ladder (الجزرية + ملحة الإعراب + لامية الأفعال + التفسير + العقيدة + الفقه + الحديث/السيرة inside 19 periods). Any period taken there is taken from عروض's own prerequisites: self-defeating.

WHY NOT ONE YEAR LATER (G9 or later): (a) The payoff of عروض in THIS institution is not literary appreciation, it is error-detection in a متن-based pedagogy. The student's heaviest منظومة load lies ahead of him in G9-G12 and on the إجازة track. Every year the tool is delayed, more mis-memorised hemistichs fossilise, and fossilised حفظ is extremely expensive to correct later. G8 is the last year that still sits upstream of most of the memorisation it protects. (b) G9 is the الإعدادية exit year and the plausible opening of البلاغة; introducing عروض there guarantees terminological interference between زحاف/علة and بديع. (c) Anything pushed into G10-G12 competes with أصول الفقه، مصطلح الحديث، البلاغة، المنطق، أصول التفسير. In that competition a small tool-science with no examination stake is always the first thing cut — meaning "G10 introduction" in practice means "never taught". Placing the foundation at G8 and only the completion rotation at G10 protects the discipline from its own budget vulnerability.

**Prerequisites.**
- Fully vowelled reading and writing — the student can supply and read الشكل on an unpointed line. Established by G5 (القراءة والتهجي G1-G5 + الإملاء as the longitudinal Arabic skill, per accepted redline 7).
- Phonetic analysis of the written line: التنوين = نون ساكنة; الشدة = ساكن + متحرك; همزة الوصل drops في الدرج; letters written but not pronounced; the behaviour of المد. This is the single most load-bearing prerequisite for الكتابة العروضية and it is not an Arabic-language competency in this curriculum — it is a التجويد competency. Established by تحفة الأطفال (G4-G5, أحكام النون الساكنة والتنوين والمدود) and completed by الجزرية (G6-G7, المخارج والصفات، الوقف والابتداء). Closes end of G7.
- الميزان الصرفي — فاء/عين/لام الكلمة، الزائد، المحذوف، أوزان المجرد والمزيد. Needed BOTH as a transferable habit of thought (a word has a shape independent of its letters) AND as a thing to be explicitly distinguished from the prosodic balance. Established by البناء في الصرف (G6) and لامية الأفعال (G7). Closes end of G7.
- النحو sufficient to read a بيت correctly aloud — i.e. not to mis-vowel a word ending and thereby mis-scan it. الآجرومية (G6) is enough for the G8 unit; ملحة الإعراب (G7) makes it comfortable. Note that ملحة الإعراب is itself an أرجوزة, so the نحو text doubles as scanning material.
- A substantial memorised poetic repertoire — realistically several hundred أبيات — so that التقطيع is checked against an ear rather than performed as blind symbol-manipulation. Established cumulatively by النشيد والمحفوظات (G3-G7) plus the أراجيز carried in other subjects (تحفة الأطفال، الجزرية، ملحة الإعراب). Sufficient by end of G7.
- Sustained accurate chanting/الإنشاد — the ability to deliver a line at an even pace without swallowing or padding syllables. Without it the student cannot hear his own scansion. Established from G3 onward through النشيد.
- Symbol-pattern fluency: mapping sounds to a two-value notation and counting positions reliably. Trivially satisfied by the morning national curriculum; listed only so it is not silently assumed away.

**Differentiation point.** G11 — and specifically G11, not G10 and not G12. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]

WHY NOT G10: النقد is not a body of content, it is the exercise of judgement using three instruments — الإعراب (what the sentence says), الوزن (how the line is built), and البلاغة (why this wording and not another). At G10 the third instrument is still being manufactured: on my assumed placement البلاغة's three branches are not complete until the end of G10. Criticism attempted with incomplete البلاغة is impressionism with vocabulary — the student says "this is more eloquent" and cannot finish the sentence. Worse, it inoculates: having "done نقد" at G10, the student meets the real discipline at G11 believing it is revision.

WHY NOT G12: two reasons. First, the discipline has a theoretical half and an applied half, and one year cannot carry the move from "here are the critics' criteria" to "here is my own argued judgement of an unseen text." Second, a subject existing only in the terminal year is certified on a single term's evidence, which is not a mastery threshold worth sealing. G11 gives the theory a year to settle before it is used under examination conditions.

WHY IT DIFFERENTIATES AT ALL rather than remaining مدمج forever: at G11 it acquires content that has no other owner. عمود الشعر, اللفظ والمعنى, الطبع والصنعة, السرقات — these are not البلاغة (a tool-kit), not العروض (mechanics), and not اللغة العربية (competence). Left مدمج, they get taught last and cut first. [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]

**Prerequisites.**
- Fluent decoding of vowelled and unvowelled Arabic — established by القراءة والتهجي, secure by G5 (independently assessed there from G1).
- Sentence-level comprehension and vocabulary beyond concrete nouns — اللغة العربية, G6-G7.
- إعراب of a line of verse, including تقديم وتأخير, حذف, and فاعل مؤخر — requires ملحة الإعراب, established G7, consolidated G8. This is the hardest gate: الآجرومية alone (G6) is NOT sufficient, because poetry's ordinary word order is exactly what الآجرومية's examples avoid.
- الصرف: recognition of أوزان and المشتقات (اسم الفاعل، اسم المفعول، الصفة المشبهة، صيغ المبالغة) so a poet's morphological choices are visible AS choices — لامية الأفعال, G7.
- A رصيد سماعي — a stock of verse held in memory and in the ear. Supplied by النشيد والمحفوظات, G3-G7 (منظومة التوحيد، ديوان الشافعي، لامية ابن الوردي، تائية الإلبيري). You cannot judge poetry you have never internalised; this is built over five years and cannot be compressed.
- Dictionary skill: locating a root and reading a lexical entry — needed by G8, currently unassigned in the register; recommend اللغة العربية take it at G7. [COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]
- العروض: التقطيع and recognition of the بحور of the texts actually studied — needed operationally by G10. Assumed established as a bounded course at G8-G9; my G11 differentiation depends on it.
- البلاغة: المعاني، البيان، البديع complete — needed by G11. Assumed complete by end of G10. If the board places البلاغة later, this subject's differentiation must move with it.
- الإنشاء: ability to write a connected paragraph of argued Arabic — needed from G9 (60-100 words) scaling to G12 (400-600 words). Assumed established from G9.
- الإملاء: orthographic accuracy sufficient for written criticism — a longitudinal اللغة العربية skill [accepted redline 7], sufficient by G9.
- A historical frame (الجاهلية، صدر الإسلام، الأموي، العباسي، الأندلسي) — supplied by السيرة والتاريخ الإسلامي from G6-G7 onward; this subject pegs to it and must never re-teach it.

**Role at G12.** At G12 this subject is the capstone of Programme 2 — the year the student stops receiving Arabic and starts judging it. Its final role is threefold: (a) convert the tools of البلاغة and العروض into argued judgement; (b) turn نظرية النظم outward onto the Qur'an, feeding أصول التفسير with substantiated rather than asserted claims about الإعجاز البياني; (c) model disciplined disagreement, through الموازنة and الوساطة, as a transferable intellectual habit.

WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO BECAUSE OF IT:
- Take an unfamiliar classical قصيدة and, with a dictionary, produce a correct شرح, determine its بحر by تقطيع, identify its غرض, place it in its period, and name and evaluate its صور.
- Write 400-600 words of criticism in Arabic arguing from a شاهد rather than from taste, and defend it orally under questioning.
- State the major critical controversies — عمود الشعر، اللفظ والمعنى، الطبع والصنعة، السرقات، القديم والحديث — and say which side they take and on what grounds.
- Bring شواهد شعرية to bear when reading تفسير and أصول الفقه instead of stepping over them.
- Write composition consciously modelled on an identified classical style, which is what makes G12 الإنشاء والخطابة more than template-filling.
- Hear the difference between Arabic that is correct and Arabic that is eloquent — the one outcome no examination fully captures and every listener notices.

**Prerequisites.**

[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]
- الوقف awareness — that ة is realised as ه in pause — needed before ة / ه / ت can be separated. Available from the practical التجويد strand and from القراءة والتهجي by G3–G4.

(3) Runway: G10 carries الفرق and G11 carries الشبهات والمدارس السنية. Those years need a systematic frame already built, not built alongside them. Not G7: the 19-period ceiling at G7 is already absorbing ملحة الإعراب and لامية الأفعال simultaneously — the heaviest language year in the ladder — and an additional standalone block there is both a budget failure and a cognitive one. **Differentiation point.** G5 — الفقه becomes مستقل at G5. This is an override; the submission never lets it differentiate at all through G7.

WHY G5 AND NOT G4: at G4 التوحيد is still a productive partner. Splitting a working pair for nothing is vandalism. Separately, G4 Arabic (المفردات والجمل القصيرة) cannot yet sustain a standalone Arabic فقه syllabus, and G4 is already carrying the launch of تحفة الأطفال as a new مستقل — two new independent subjects in one year is a load-balancing error, not a curriculum.

WHY G5 AND NOT G6: four reasons, each sufficient. (1) G5 is the exact year التوحيد leaves to pair with العقيدة under accepted redline 5, so الفقه is structurally orphaned at G5 whatever the board does; the submission's response is to hand it السيرة, which is the wrong answer to the right problem. (2) Age ~10 is the sharī'ah's own enforcement threshold; the obligations become personally binding on the learner in the same year. (3) The بلوغ-timing constraint is decisive: أحكام الحيض والنفاس والاغتسال must be delivered BEFORE بلوغ, and for girls menarche can arrive at G5–G6. (4) A matn must land on a subject that already exists. الأخضري arrives at G6; if G6 is also the year the subject first acquires independent identity, the matn will be memorised without the conceptual grid it compresses, which is exactly the failure mode redline 1 identified for نخبة الفكر.

IT IS PERIOD-NEUTRAL. I am not asking for extra periods. الفقه as half of "الفقه والسيرة" costs the same as الفقه مستقل once السيرة is returned to الحديث — which is where the submission itself had it at G4, and which is the disciplinarily correct pairing (both are riwāya-based أخبار). The board is being asked for a re-pairing, not a budget increase.

WHY THE PAIRING WITH السيرة IS WRONG ON ITS OWN TERMS: السيرة is narrative-historical; الفقه is normative-taxonomic. They share no cognitive operation, no technical vocabulary, and no assessment logic. Nothing a student learns in one transfers to the other. It is an administrative leftover-slot pairing, not a disciplinary one, and it breaks a good pair (الحديث والسيرة, correctly made at G4) to make a bad one.

- Parsing a simple Arabic sentence and recognising مبتدأ/خبر and فعل/فاعل — established G6 via الآجرومية, extended G7 via ملحة الإعراب. Required to read the conditional clauses (إذا ... فـ ...) that carry most of a fiqh matn's content.
- Recognition of أوزان المزيد — established G6 (البناء في الصرف) and G7 (لامية الأفعال). The فقه lexicon is disproportionately مزيد-derived (تيمّم، اغتسال، استبراء، انتقاض، استقبال، إمامة، إحرام); without صرف these are opaque strings to be memorised rather than words to be understood.
- Fractions, and later ratio and percentage — NOT established in this curriculum at all. This is a cross-curricular dependency on the MORNING national stream: fractions needed by ~G6 for زكاة, ratio/percentage by ~G8 for الفرائض. SHRS must verify this against the national scheme of work; it cannot be assumed.
- Familiarity with أحاديث الأحكام as a corpus — begun G4 via الحديث والسيرة, deepened G7–G9 via الحديث. الفقه consumes this; it must not annex it.
- Competence to judge whether a حديث is صحيح or ضعيف — established G10–G11 via مصطلح الحديث under accepted redline 1. This is the gate on الفقه's entire evidential stage; the redline that moved نخبة الفكر out of G6 is precisely what makes فقه's maturation at G10 possible.
- The categories of أصول الفقه (أمر/نهي، عام/خاص، مطلق/مقيد، ناسخ/منسوخ) — required from G10. Not owned by me; a hard dependency on whoever is placing أصول الفقه.

**Role at G12.** At G12 الفقه is the subject in which the graduate's competence becomes publicly usable. It is no longer about the student's own obligations — those were settled years earlier — but about the student's capacity to serve and, critically, to know where that capacity ends.

Concretely, the G12 graduate can: perform and lead the full range of عبادات correctly including the عوارض that defeat most people (الجمع والقصر في السفر, صلاة المريض, صلاة الخوف at description level, سجود السهو in its cases); perform غسل الميت and lead صلاة الجنازة start to finish, which is the single most frequently demanded service a young graduate faces in a Nigerian community and the one most often botched; calculate زكاة المال and زكاة الفطر on real figures and divide a simple ميراث; state a ruling on a common question together with its آية or حديث, name the Maliki position, and identify the main disagreement and its سبب; recognise a ربوي or غرر-bearing structure inside an ordinary Nigerian financial product; distinguish a sharī'ah ruling from a Yoruba custom in عقيقة, تسمية, وليمة and تعزية without dismissing the custom where it is merely permissible; and explain any of the above in Arabic, English and Yoruba — the Yoruba register being the one that actually reaches the congregation.

And the competence that makes the rest safe: the trained reflex of لا أعلم، اسأل أهل العلم. The graduate can distinguish نقل from اجتهاد, can cite a position to its source rather than to his own authority, and knows that a secondary-school certificate in فقه licenses him to teach and to answer the ordinary, not to issue fatwa on the contested. A school that produces confident young men who will rule on anything has produced a liability. This is the one capstone outcome I would refuse to trade for any amount of additional content.

**Role at G12.** At G12 أصول الفقه is the discipline that converts twelve years of accumulated content into judgement, and it is the graduating discipline of Programme 3. Concretely, the graduate can: read a فقه text that cites evidence and follow the استدلال rather than only the conclusion; take a مسألة on which qualified people differ and locate WHERE they differ — whether the أمر carries وجوب or ندب, whether a عام has been مخصص, whether a فعل نبوي is تشريع or عادة, whether the خبر is accepted — instead of counting which side has more shouting; transmit a ruling together with its source and its madhhab attribution rather than as a bare verdict; recognise the boundary of his own authority, know which questions require a مفتٍ, and say لا أدري as a trained response rather than an embarrassment; explain to a Yoruba- or English-speaking lay audience why Muslims differ, without collapsing into relativism on one side or takfir on the other; and enter a university course in أصول at or above entry level, having completed الورقات with شرح and memorised نظم الورقات. Defensive — G6 is already the heaviest year in the whole ladder (الآجرومية، البناء في الصرف، الجزرية، الأخضري، التفسير العملي، الترجمة all begin or run there). Because of it the graduate can: locate a hadith in the principal printed collections from its wording; read an isnād and name its structural condition from what is visible (متصل، منقطع، معلق، مرسل، معضل، مدلَّس) without claiming more than the chain shows; read a compiler's or a critic's printed حكم and state exactly what it commits him to and what it does not; write a short تخريج مصغّر — attribution, chain shape, reported gradings, and a written note on the limits of his own conclusion; distinguish a claim he can verify from one requiring رجال expertise he does not have; recognise the marks of a circulating fabricated فضيلة and decline to transmit it; and follow the Sunnah chapter of أصول الفقه and the مأثور material of أصول التفسير in their own vocabulary. That last competency is the point and is easy to undervalue: trained epistemic restraint — knowing precisely where his own authority to grade ends — is as much the output of this discipline at G12 as any classification he can perform.

**Differentiation point.** There are TWO events and the Board must not merge them, because they answer different questions.

EVENT 1 — FIRST DISTINCT NAMED ENTITY: G7, as a وحدة of one term, co-taught with الفقه, titled «وحدة البلوغ والتكليف». This is the year the subject first exists under its own name with its own mark.

WHY G7 AND NOT G6: two independent reasons converge, and either alone would be sufficient. (i) Developmental — the unit's core is المسؤولية الفردية, and that requires the learner to be able to hold his own conduct as an object and judge it, which is a capacity that consolidates around age 12-13 and is not reliably present at 11. [PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE] Delivered at G6 it becomes recitation of rules about a state the child is not in. (ii) Curricular — G6 is the single most loaded grade in this entire ladder: الآجرومية، البناء في الصرف، الجزرية، الأخضري، التفسير العملي والترجمة all arrive in one year, and by the النحو specialist's own arithmetic G6 sits at exactly 19 of 19. There is no room and there is no attention. A unit inserted at G6 would be taught badly by an exhausted teacher to an overloaded cohort.

WHY G7 AND NOT G8: بلوغ. The unit is PREPARATORY — its function is to give the learner أحكام الطهارة والتكليف BEFORE they bind him, not after. G7 is roughly age 12-13 in this cohort; a material portion of the girls will reach بلوغ during or before that year, and some boys during it. Delivered at G8 the unit arrives after the fact for a substantial minority. The specific failure that follows is not abstract: an early-maturing girl who does not know أحكام الحيض والغسل, and is too embarrassed to ask a male teacher or a mother who assumes the school taught it, prays when she should not or abandons prayer when she should not, conceals it, and does so for months. G7 is the last year in which the unit is still preparatory for nearly everyone. That is the whole argument, and it is a date, not a preference.

EVENT 2 — FIRST AND ONLY مستقل YEAR: G12. Not a second differentiation of the same entity but a different entity with a different function — أدب الفتوى والاختلاف والدعوة. WHY G12 AND NOT G11: the capstone consumes أصول الفقه's vocabulary of اختلاف (مستقل only from G11), مصطلح الحديث's صحيح/ضعيف discrimination (G10-G11), and الترجمة's rendering capacity into English and Yoruba (مستقل at G11). All three close at the end of G11. Taught at G11 it would be taught to a student who cannot yet name the level of a disagreement, which is precisely the competence the unit is about. WHY NOT LATER: there is no later. G12 is the last year the institution has custody of this young man before his community starts asking him questions.

WHAT NEVER HAPPENS: التربية الإسلامية never becomes a weekly examined subject at G1-G6, and this is a positive finding, not a budget concession. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION] Converting habituation into a timetabled paper is the documented failure mode of the discipline: pupils score full marks on الأمانة and cheat in the examination that follows. Formation at that age is produced by نظام، قدوة ومصاحبة, and those are institutional instruments, not periods.

**Role at G12.** At G12 the subject holds its only مستقل year — one period per week, «أدب الفتوى والاختلاف والدعوة» — and its function is terminal and specific: to govern the use of everything else the graduate is leaving with.

WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO BECAUSE OF IT:
- State an opponent's position in its strongest form, in Arabic, before advancing his own — and name the level of the disagreement using أصول vocabulary: قطعي or ظني, خلاف معتبر or قول شاذ, a difference of تحقيق مناط or of دليل.
- Say «لا أدري» as a deliberate scholarly act, and name the person or body he refers the questioner to. This is a trained behaviour with a script, not a disposition, and it is rehearsed in the seminar.
- Recognise the boundary of his own أهلية: he can transmit a settled ruling with its evidence; he does not issue فتوى in نوازل, in انتقال between madhhabs, or in matters touching marriage dissolution, inheritance shares in contested estates, or takfīr. He knows the list because he has been given the list.
- Deliver religious counsel to his own community in Yoruba and in English — drawing on الترجمة (G11) — without takfīr language and without implying that Yoruba social custom is as such suspect. He can distinguish, out loud and calmly, between a custom that conflicts with الشرع, one that is merely عرف, and one he simply dislikes.
- Verify an adab hadith before preaching from it, because he holds مصطلح (G10-G11) and has been made to apply it to this literature specifically, where fabricated material is thickest.
- Teach a younger pupil — he has been doing it under supervision since G11 — and knows the أدب of the teacher's side of that relationship from تذكرة السامع والمتكلم.
- Sustain a personal regime he set himself: a ميثاق التخرج carrying ورد قرآني, أذكار, a service commitment and a tutoring log, documented in a portfolio.

ASSESSMENT AT G12, precisely: independently assessed, with the mark composed of the portfolio, performance in the مناظرة/تحقيق seminar, and one written paper on أدب الخلاف. Conduct itself is REPORTED and never converted into a numeric grade. The distinction is load-bearing: the moment character becomes a scorable component of a leaving certificate, it becomes something to perform for the certificate, and the subject destroys in one year what it spent eleven building.

WHAT G12 IS NOT: it is not a revision of أخلاق. It is the year the school takes responsibility for what it has armed the graduate with.

**Differentiation point.** G5 — السيرة becomes a distinct named entity (مستقل) at G5, and a second, differently-purposed entity (فقه السيرة, دوراني) at G11. I am overriding the submission (which never differentiates it at all through G7) AND the earlier specialist draft (which put differentiation at G7). The G7 event is real but it is a MEDIUM CHANGE — the subject goes Arabic-medium and text-based — not a differentiation. Conflating the two is precisely what produced the submission's مدمج-with-التاريخ at G7.

WHY G5 AND NOT G4: at G4 the الحديث+السيرة pairing is still doing genuine two-way work — the hadith acquires a speaker, a setting and an occasion; the sirah acquires a reason to be attended to closely. A nine-year-old's chronology is still the two-box timeline of G3 being consolidated. Differentiating at G4 would break a functioning pair to gain nothing.

WHY G5 AND NOT G6 OR G7 — the decisive argument, and it is disciplinary, not administrative: G5 is the year BOTH possible hosts leave, and they leave for the same underlying reason. #21 الحديث becomes مستقل at G5 because its corpus crosses ~25 items and is reorganised THEMATICALLY (الإيمان، الطهارة والصلاة، الأخلاق، المعاملات) — its own specialist writes that this is "an organising logic السيرة cannot supply, since a life is chronological and a corpus is topical." #19 الفقه becomes مستقل at G5 around an explicit normative grid (شروط، أركان، واجبات، سنن، مبطلات) and rejects السيرة as a partner sharing "no cognitive operation, no technical vocabulary, and no assessment logic." Both are right. The moment at which narrative organisation diverges irreconcilably from topical organisation and from normative organisation IS the moment السيرة must stand on its own. To hold differentiation back to G7 would leave the discipline homeless for two full years — parked in a block whose organising principle actively destroys its method — and a curriculum that does that has not designed the subject, it has mislaid it.

WHY THIS IS AFFORDABLE AT G5 AND NOT MERELY DESIRABLE: differentiation here costs ONE period, because مستقل does not entail an Arabic matn. السيرة is the only discipline in Programme 3 that can carry a full independent identity in the language of instruction — its medium is narrative, not a technical register. The reading-capacity argument (correct in itself) gates the Arabic TEXT at G7; it does not gate independence. The G5 timetable has roughly six free periods on any honest reconstruction. G6, by contrast, has one, and G7 is over the ceiling — so deferring differentiation to the loaded years would also have been the more expensive choice.

WHY THE SECOND DIFFERENTIATION IS G11 AND NOT G12: فقه السيرة requires moral complexity (available by G10–G11), a completed narrative (G8), source awareness (G9) and مصطلح's apparatus (G10). G12 is taken by the capstone; placing the analytic pass there means it is done badly or abandoned. [NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]

Note the distinction: it is NAMED at G6 (in the label "السيرة والتاريخ الإسلامي") but is not yet a distinct entity. Differentiation means having no host left and having its own reason to exist.

WHY NOT G7 (one year earlier): two things are still true at G7 that are false at G8. (a) السيرة still has live subject matter — الفتنة is the direct continuation of the Companions' story, so تاريخ still has a legitimate and pedagogically useful host. (b) The strand that justifies independence — نشأة العلوم الإسلامية — has nothing to attach to at G7: the learner has one year of formal النحو (ملحة الإعراب), no فقه مذهبي, and no مصطلح. Telling a G7 learner about البصريين والكوفيين is telling him about a dispute over a subject he barely has. At G8 he has enough of each science for its genealogy to land.

WHY NOT G9 (one year later): (a) السيرة's subject matter ends at 11 AH; keeping the "السيرة والتاريخ" label past G7 is a fiction under which سيرة revision reliably eats تاريخ's time, because revision of known material is always easier to teach than new chronology. (b) G9 is the terminal year of الإعدادية and carries the West African strand, which cannot be compressed. Differentiating at G9 would force الأموي, العباسي, نشأة العلوم, الفرق, الأندلس and غرب إفريقيا into a single year — guaranteeing either superficiality or the loss of the African strand, which is the one part that cannot be lost. (c) G8 differentiation buys a genuine two-year مستقل run within الإعدادية rather than a one-year token.

**Role at G12.** At G12 the subject is a bounded وحدة, not a full-year course — deliberately de-escalated so that the terminal sciences (أصول الفقه، أصول التفسير، البلاغة، إكمال مصطلح الحديث) can have G12. Its role is synthesis and theorisation, not further narrative.

The graduate can, concretely:
1. Place any major figure, text or institution of the Islamic sciences on a dated hijri timeline and state what preceded it and what produced it — including every matn he himself studied.
2. Read a historical report and ask who transmitted it, through whom, and by what standard it was preserved — and articulate precisely why the evidentiary standards of التاريخ differ from those of الحديث, rather than either accepting chronicles credulously or dismissing the whole corpus.
3. Narrate the entry and development of Islam in West Africa and in Yorubaland, name its scholars and institutions, and situate his own community within the global arc rather than outside it.
4. Give a restrained, doctrinally grounded account of الفتنة that neither adjudicates between Companions nor evades the fact that it happened — and explain why the restraint is a position, not an absence of one.
5. Apply Ibn Khaldūn's categories — عصبية، أطوار الدولة، the relation of عمران to the flourishing of the sciences — to analyse the rise and decline of a polity, including a modern one.
6. Produce and orally defend a referenced historical essay in Arabic, using primary and secondary sources and weighing conflicting accounts.

Capability 6 is the one that carries the school's international ambitions: it is a transferable research competence recognisable to any external examiner, produced in Arabic.

**Differentiation point.** G12, as مستقل, 2 periods/week. This is the latest differentiation point of any subject on the register and it needs a defence rather than an apology.

WHY NOT G11. At G11 أصول الفقه is in its FIRST مستقل year — التعارض والترجيح، الإجماع، الأخبار are being formalised that very year [#20]. الدراسات الحديثة has no method of its own; it borrows أصول's. Differentiating at G11 means running a subject whose entire toolkit is being manufactured in the room next door, in the same twelve weeks. The predictable output is confident misapplication — students deploying مصلحة مرسلة and عرف with a two-month-old grip on their controls. Separately and independently: G11 is the second-heaviest grade in the ladder and the periods are not there. Either reason alone is sufficient; together they are decisive.

WHY NOT G10. G10 is the peak load year (مصطلح الحديث differentiates, أصول الفقه differentiates as مدمج, البلاغة differentiates, العقيدة and المنطق are both live). More importantly, at G10 أصول is only مدمج — the student has the vocabulary but not the system. What G10 CAN carry, and does carry in this design, is the method unit in a rotation slot that already exists and is otherwise idle. That is a genuine presence, not a placeholder; but it is not differentiation.

WHY NOT G13. There is no G13. G12 is the last year the institution has custody of the student. That is not a residual argument — it is the positive one.

THE POSITIVE CASE FOR G12 SPECIFICALLY. This subject is a capstone by nature, not by convenience. It is the only entity on the 26-subject register whose object requires simultaneous, live use of أصول الفقه, الفقه, مصطلح الحديث, العقيدة, المنطق and الترجمة on a single question. Every other subject examines itself: تفسير examines تفسير, فقه examines فقه. Nothing else in twelve years asks the student to demonstrate that the twelve years converge. A capstone placed in the middle of a wall is not a capstone; it is a weak course. All six of its feeder disciplines reach مستقل status at G10-G11 and no earlier, so G12 is the first year — and the only year — in which the convergence is even possible.

And the exit argument is concrete, not rhetorical: the G12 student is weeks away from a Nigerian university or workplace where, in month one, he will meet interest-bearing student finance, mixed residence, insurance, workplace compromise and secular law — and will be asked about all of them by peers because he is «the one who went to the Islamic school». Differentiating at the point of departure means the method is fresh, the supervision is present, and the first unprecedented question he faces alone is his second, not his first. **Role at G12.** FINAL ROLE: the integration instrument. مستقل, 2 periods/week, independently assessed — the only standing timetable claim the subject makes in twelve years, deliberately spent in the year the student leaves.

It is the sole place on the 26-subject register where the graduate must demonstrate that twelve years of separately-taught sciences converge on one question. Every other G12 entity examines its own domain; أصول الفقه's own G12 year runs تخريج الفروع على الأصول — established differences reconstructed BACKWARD to their أصولي locus. This subject runs the identical machinery FORWARD, onto questions that have no فرع to reconstruct. That directional difference is the whole justification for its separate existence and the board should hold the two apart on exactly that line.

WHAT THE GRADUATE CAN ACTUALLY DO, stated as testable acts rather than as attributes:
1. Take an unfamiliar question and state it precisely — تحرير محل السؤال — separating the actual question from the questioner's framing of it.
2. Describe accurately how the thing in question actually works before ruling on it, and recognise when he does not know how it works and must ask someone who does (a banker, a physician, an engineer). فقه الواقع as a procedural step, not a slogan.
3. Produce a written تكييف naming at least one classical analogue and the ʿilla being claimed, and say what would defeat that analogy.
4. Take any evidence cited at him and ask whether it is ثابت — grading, source, and the honest limit of what he personally can conclude — using the مصطلح apparatus from G10-G11.
5. Locate and read at least two contemporary scholarly treatments in Arabic, identify the actual point of disagreement rather than the rhetorical one, and distinguish what is قطعي and settled from what is legitimately contested.
6. Render all of the above accurately to a lay questioner in English and in Yoruba, without flattening a contested question into a single confident answer. His questioners will not ask in Arabic, and this is where الترجمة is finally cashed.
7. Write a 1,500-2,500 word Arabic بحث on a single نازلة with correct sourcing — double-marked with الإنشاء والخطابة on construction and gated by الإملاء's published orthographic standard.
8. Decline. Identify which questions exceed him, say «لا أدري» without loss of face, and route the questioner to someone competent. This is examined, not hoped for. It is the competency most likely to matter and the one most likely to be skipped if it is not on the paper.
