## 2. A MISSING DOCUMENT THAT IS THE DELIVERABLE ITSELF

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 §3.2, closing line:

> توزيع **الحصص التسع عشرة** على هذه المواد **صفًّا صفًّا** مثبت في **سجل

   §4.2–§4.4.
3. **A prior adjudication is on record**: الشرعيات العليا were re-weighted from
   **twelve حصص to ten** to create the chairs of النحو and الصرف in الثانوية.
   A precedent exists for exactly the kind of reallocation G8–G12 now needs,
   and it was made «معلنةً لا صامتة» — declared, not silent.

## 5. PREMATURE DIFFERENTIATION

`[RECOVERED]` §3.2 registers **التربية الإسلامية (ر–٦)** with the reason
«**الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن يكون علومًا مسماة**» — religion as love and habit
before it becomes *named sciences*. The range ر–٦ is the mechanism: for the
first six years there is one subject, not several.

The matrix names, at **G1**: التوحيد، الفقه، التفسير، السيرة النبوية،
التربية الإسلامية — **five named shar'ī sciences**. By **G3**, six.

This contradicts §3.2 directly, and it contradicts the standing direction «do
not invent fragmentation». `[BLACK]` — it is the single decision from which
most of §4's drift follows.

## 15. WHAT THIS REDLINE DOES NOT ESTABLISH

It does not establish that ACD-007 §3.2 governs. §3.2 is `[RECOVERED]`; the
26-subject universe is `[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` and later in time. Where
they conflict, **the Council may lawfully prefer its own architecture** — the
finding is that it has never been told it was departing.

Every departure in §§4–11 may be defensible. **None is minuted.** That is the
system-level defect: the matrix was built as if §3.2 did not exist.
