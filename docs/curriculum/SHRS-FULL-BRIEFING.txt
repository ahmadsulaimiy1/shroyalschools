READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS AND HOW TO USE IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a complete, self-contained briefing on the curriculum of Sultan Hanafi
Royal Schools, a supplementary Islamic and Arabic school operating in the
Yoruba-speaking region of Nigeria (Ilorin / Ibadan). It is written to be handed
to another AI assistant, or to a human who has never seen this work before, so
that they can continue it without having to rediscover anything.

The curriculum is a single coherent twelve-year system. It is NOT twelve
separate year plans stitched together. Every decision below interacts with the
others, which is why the decisions are numbered and locked.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT THING TO UNDERSTAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project has one recurring failure mode, and it destroyed months of work
before it was diagnosed. The failure is this:

    Each document was rebuilt from whatever happened to be in front of the
    person rebuilding it. Every time that happened, decisions that had been
    settled earlier but did not appear in THAT PARTICULAR FILE were silently
    dropped. The Director General then had to restate them from memory.

These were never disagreements. They were losses. The same rulings were made
three and four times over.

The remedy is a single file, docs/curriculum/00-LOCKED-DECISIONS.md, called
"the register". It holds 35 numbered rulings, L-01 through L-35. Its governing
rule, in the original Arabic, is:

    لا يُغيَّر بند مقفل إلا بقرار صريح جديد من المدير العام.
    لا يُغيَّر بالنسيان، ولا بإعادة البناء، ولا باجتهاد لجنة.
    وما ليس في هذا السجل فليس بقرار.

    "A locked item is changed only by an explicit new ruling from the Director
    General. Not by forgetting. Not by rebuilding a document. Not by a
    committee's own judgement. And what is not in this register is not a
    decision."

If you work on this curriculum, THREE RULES BIND YOU:

  1. A locked item changes only by an explicit new ruling from the Director
     General. A board may refer a locked item back for reconsideration; it may
     not overrule one.

  2. If a change you are about to make would break a locked item, STOP. Do not
     make the change and explain afterwards. Raise it first.

  3. A new ruling is written into the register FIRST, in the change log, with
     its reason - and only then implemented in the other documents.

WORKING LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subject names, text titles and instructional forms are written IN ARABIC. This
was an explicit instruction from the Director General. English may gloss a rule;
it may never replace a name. "العروض وعلم القافية" is not "prosody". "البحث
والخطابة" is not "research and oratory". The reason given was direct: English
cannot do justice to Arabic. Keep the Arabic names throughout.

Throughout this briefing, Arabic names appear as they must be used. English
follows only as explanation.

PART 1 - THE CONSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE
================================================================================

1.1  TWELVE CLASSES, NO MORE AND NO FEWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The school runs exactly twelve classes. There is no kindergarten (روضة) and no
stage before class one. An earlier version of the plan assumed a kindergarten
existed and built the Qur'an memorisation target on thirteen funded years; when
the kindergarten was abolished, the target was not recalculated. That error is
described in Part 9.

1.2  FOUR SECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    القسم التمهيدي   - classes 1-2   (Primary 1-2)
        Theme: التهيئة وفك الحرف - preparation and unlocking the letter
        Exit gate: بوابة القراءة (the reading gate) in class 1

    القسم الابتدائي  - classes 3-6   (Primary 3-6)
        Theme: القراءة فالطلاقة فالجسر إلى عربية العلم
               reading, then fluency, then the bridge to the Arabic of
               scholarship
        Exit gates: بوابة الطلاقة (3) · الشهادة الابتدائية (5) · بوابة الجسر (6)

    القسم الإعدادي   - classes 7-9   (JSS 1-3)
        Theme: العلوم الشرعية بأسمائها والنحو على متونه
               the sharʿī sciences under their own names, and grammar on its
               classical matns
        Exit gate: الشهادة الإعدادية (9), with an external examiner

    القسم الثانوي    - classes 10-12 (SS 1-3)
        Theme: علوم الآلة والمتون الكبرى ثم سنة التتويج
               the instrumental sciences and the major matns, then the
               capstone year
        Exit gates: الثانوية القرآنية (11) · شهادة التخرج (12)

1.3  THREE PROGRAMMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    البرنامج الأول   ·  القرآن وعلومه          (the Qur'an and its sciences)
    البرنامج الثاني  ·  اللغة وعلومها          (language and its sciences)
    البرنامج الثالث  ·  الدراسات الإسلامية      (Islamic studies)

NOTE CAREFULLY: Programme Two is اللغة وعلومها. It is NOT "اللغة العربية
وعلومها". This was corrected explicitly and has been wrong in drafts before.

The binding rule:

    كل مادة في كل صف تقع تحت واحد من البرامج الثلاثة، ولا تقع خارجها.
    "Every subject, in every class, falls under one of the three programmes -
    and none falls outside them."

There is one presentational exception at class 12: WAEC and التحرير الامتحاني
are grouped under a heading called خدمة سنة التتويج (capstone-year service).
They remain accountable to the three-programme rule; the heading is an
administrative grouping for the certificate year, not a fourth programme.

PART 2 - TIME: THE OPERATING WEEK AND THE ANNUAL LEDGER
================================================================================

2.1  THE WEEK AS ACTUALLY OPERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Monday    14:00 - 18:00
    Tuesday   14:00 - 18:00
    Wednesday 14:00 - 18:00
    Thursday  NO SCHOOL (لا دوام)
    Friday    NO SCHOOL (لا دوام)
    Saturday  09:00 - 15:00
    Sunday    09:00 - 15:00

This is a supplementary school. Children attend other schooling on Thursday and
Friday. The week is short and every minute is contested - which is why the
arithmetic below is treated as a constitutional matter and not an
administrative one.

2.2  THE UNITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    حصة (lesson slot)     = 40 minutes
    فصل (term)            = 13 weeks
    سنة (year)            = 39 weeks, in three terms
    Weekly slots, classes 1-6   = 13
    Weekly slots, classes 7-12  = 19

2.3  THE PROTECTED QUR'ANIC HOUR - الساعة القرآنية
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A protected daily hour for memorisation and revision sits OUTSIDE the 13 or 19
weekly slots entirely. It is 290 minutes a week in classes 1-6 and 340 minutes
a week in classes 7-12.

    الساعة القرآنية محمية - لا تُقتطع لأي مادة في أي صف
    "The Qur'anic hour is protected - it is not drawn upon for any subject in
    any class."

No subject may take minutes from it. This has been violated in drafts by
quietly hosting subjects inside it; see Part 9, error 12.

2.4  THE ANNUAL LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Classes 1-6:   13 slots x 39 weeks x 40 min = 338h subject time
                   + 188h30m Qur'anic hour
                   = 526h30m per year

    Classes 7-12:  19 slots x 39 weeks x 40 min = 494h subject time
                   + 221h Qur'anic hour
                   = 715h per year

    THE WHOLE TWELVE-YEAR JOURNEY:  7,449 hours

A rule of method applies here and must be respected: never round silently, and
always separate ANALYTICAL DEMAND (how much time a subject needs) from
OPERATIONAL ALLOCATION (how much time it is actually given). A timetable period
is an operational decision; it is not the unit of curriculum design.

An earlier ledger claimed 754 hours per year and a 19h20m week. That was wrong:
100 minutes had been booked as مراجعة (revision) when 60 of those minutes were
actually wuḍūʾ and dismissal. The corrected figures - 18h20m per week and 715
hours per year - are the ones above.

PART 3 - THE VOCABULARY OF INSTRUCTIONAL FORM
================================================================================

This vocabulary is closed. These six forms and no others may be used to describe
how a subject is delivered. Inventing a seventh form is a breach of L-08.

    مستقل   INDEPENDENT
            Holds its own timetable slot. Has its own book. Has its own exam
            paper. Enters the average. Appears in Section A of the transcript.

    مدمج    MERGED
            Shares a slot with ONE named partner subject. Has no book of its
            own - it lives inside the partner's book. It gets a NAMED SECTION on
            the partner's paper with a 40% floor, so it cannot be quietly
            dropped. Enters the average, aggregated.

    مضمّن   EMBEDDED
            Taught inside a NAMED HOST subject. No slot, no book, no separate
            paper. Assessed inside the host. Still appears in Section A through
            its host.

    وحدة    UNIT
            A named, separately examined block that never holds a slot of its
            own. Consumes its HOST's minutes. Appears in Section B of the
            transcript as an achievement rating. Does NOT enter the average.

    مسار    STRAND
            A thread inside a book (used for the four strands inside كتاب
            الدراسات الإسلامية in the lower classes). No slot, no book, no
            mark. Appears only as a descriptive appendix - متقن / نامٍ / مبتدئ -
            with NO NUMBER attached.

    دوراني  ROTATING
            A slot that cycles between courses. Reserved vocabulary; used
            sparingly.

    شرط     CONDITION
            Not an instructional form but an assessment category. A شرط NEVER
            CARRIES A MARK. It appears in Section C of the transcript as met or
            not met. خدمة التتويج and the WAEC sitting requirement are شروط.

THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE (L-07):  لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة
    "No name without a minute."
    No subject may appear on any list unless it has either timetabled lessons
    or a NAMED HOST. A subject with neither is a fiction. This rule exists
    because a subject (WAEC) once gated the leaving certificate while having
    zero timetabled minutes anywhere in the school, and a "38/38 subjects
    accounted for" check still came out balanced because the claimant had never
    been entered into the ledger at all.

ASSESSMENT FOLLOWS FORM MECHANICALLY. The instructional form determines the
assessment consequence with no discretion:

    form      paper?                    book?           average?   transcript
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    مستقل     yes                       yes             yes        Section A
    مدمج      named section, 40% floor   partner's book  yes        Section A
    مضمّن     no                        no              via host   Section A
    وحدة      achievement rating         no              no         Section B
    مسار      no                        no              no         descriptive
    شرط       NEVER CARRIES A MARK       -               no         Section C

PART 4 - THE THIRTY-NINE LOCKED DECISIONS, IN FULL
================================================================================

Each is given in the original Arabic exactly as it stands in the register,
followed by an explanation of what it means and why it exists. These are the
constitution. Do not paraphrase them away.

CHAPTER ONE - PRINCIPLES

L-01  ١٢ صفًّا — لا أكثر ولا أقل. لا روضة ولا مرحلة قبلها
      Twelve classes, no more and no fewer. No kindergarten and no stage before
      it. Any plan that assumes thirteen or more funded years is invalid.

L-02  ٤ أقسام: التمهيدي (١–٢) · الابتدائي (٣–٦) · الإعدادي (٧–٩) · الثانوي (١٠–١٢)
      Four sections, with those exact class ranges. This was forgotten once in
      a rebuild and had to be restated angrily.

L-03  ٣ برامج: القرآن وعلومه · اللغة وعلومها · الدراسات الإسلامية
      Three programmes. Note again: اللغة وعلومها, not اللغة العربية وعلومها.

L-04  كل مادة تحت واحد من البرامج الثلاثة، ولا واحدة خارجها
      Every subject under one of the three programmes; none outside.

L-05  في التمهيدي والابتدائي كل شيء مدمج أو مضمّن. لا يُثقَل الطفل بالكتب
      In التمهيدي and الابتدائي (classes 1-6) EVERYTHING is merged or embedded.
      The child is not burdened with books. This is why classes 1-6 carry only
      three books despite meeting twelve or thirteen named sciences.

L-06  الحد الأدنى ثلاثة كتب — كتاب لكل برنامج — والمصحف. والمصحف أصلٌ لا كتابَ تعليم
      Minimum THREE books - one per programme - plus the muṣḥaf. The muṣḥaf is
      a source, not a teaching book, and is never counted as Programme One's
      book. This was originally "two books", which left Programme One with no
      book at all in the lower classes. The Director General caught it: if
      there are three programmes, there must be at least three books.

L-07  لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة — لا مادة بلا حصص ولا مضيف مسمّى
      No name without a minute. See Part 3.

L-08  الصيغ وحدها: مستقل · مدمج · مضمّن · وحدة · دوراني · مسار
      Those forms and no others.

L-09  الحمل يرتفع مع الصف
      The load rises with the class. Note: this governs PROGRAMME LOAD overall.
      It does NOT mean an individual subject can never be demoted. An earlier
      draft invented a "monotonic status" rule - once a subject rose it could
      never fall - and this was explicitly withdrawn. The Director General said
      plainly that it is not compulsory to teach every subject every term.

L-10  صف التخرج يحمل الأكثر — ولا يُخفَّض بقرار لجنة
      The graduating class carries the MOST. A board once reduced class 12's
      load on its own judgement and had to be reversed. Class 12 currently
      carries 27 sciences and 14 books - more than any other class.

L-11  لا يلزم أن تُدرَّس كل مادة كل فصل — مقررات فصلية كالجامعة
      Not every subject must be taught every term. Term courses, like a
      university. This is a central design idea; see Part 6.

L-12  يجوز أن تنقطع المادة ثم تعود — بشرط أن تعود أعلى، لا تكرارًا
      A subject may pause and return - provided it returns HIGHER, not as
      repetition.

L-13  مواد الأساس لا تنقطع: النحو · اللغة العربية · الفقه · العقيدة · الحديث · التفسير
      The cornerstones never pause. These six run as independent full-year
      subjects continuously from class 7 to class 12.

L-14  البلاغة: ستة فصول كاملة
      Balāghah gets six full terms. It currently has NINE, so this floor is
      comfortably exceeded.

L-15  العروض وعلم القافية: لا يُدرَّس في فصل واحد
      Prosody and rhyme are NOT taught in a single term. The Director General
      was explicit: three or four terms, not one.

L-16  النقد والآداب: مادة لها وزنها — لا وحدة صغيرة
      Criticism and literature is a subject with real weight, not a small unit.

L-17  الصرف: حاضر في كل صف متقدم
      Morphology is present in EVERY advanced class - 7 through 12, without
      exception.

L-18  الخطابة والتواصل أولوية — ومعها الترجمة إلى اليوربا
      Oratory and communication are a priority, and with them translation into
      YORUBA. Yoruba is the mother tongue of the students. The graduate must be
      able to render his research and his khuṭbah back into Yoruba. This has
      been silently dropped from drafts before.

L-19  الفرائض: باب مُصان بورقة وسطر مستقلين
      Inheritance law is a ring-fenced chapter with its own paper and its own
      line on the certificate.

L-20  الساعة القرآنية محمية — لا تُقتطع لأي مادة في أي صف
      The Qur'anic hour is protected in all twelve classes.

L-21  مذهب أهل السنة، بلا التزام مذهب واحد
      Ahl al-Sunnah, with NO exclusive commitment to a single school of law.

L-22  يجوز أن يختلف الإطار من باب إلى باب
      The declared frame may differ from topic to topic. See Part 8.

L-23  الرواية: حفص عن عاصم · المصحف: مطبعة الملك فهد
      The riwāyah is Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim; the muṣḥaf is the King Fahd printing.

L-24  الأسبوع: الإثنين–الأربعاء ٢–٦ مساءً · الخميس والجمعة لا دوام · السبت والأحد ٩–٣
      The operating week as in Part 2.

L-25  الحصة ٤٠ د · الفصل ١٣ أسبوعًا · السنة ٣٩ أسبوعًا في ثلاثة فصول
      The units as in Part 2.

L-26  لا يُختلق دليل مفقود
      A missing document is never invented. If evidence is absent, say it is
      absent.

L-27  لا يُخلط المستردّ بقرار المجلس الحالي
      Recovered material is never mixed with a current ruling. Each is labelled.

L-28  لا يُقدَّم الاستنتاج على أنه نصٌّ مستردّ
      An inference is never presented as recovered text.

L-29  لا تُستنبط العقيدة ولا المذهب من قوائم الكتب
      Neither creed nor school of law is inferred from a booklist. If a draft
      proposes classical texts, that does not establish the school's
      orientation. Only an explicit ruling does.

L-30  لكل مادة في كل برنامج نصٌّ مقرَّر أو مادة مصدر. ولا مادة بلا مصدر
      Every subject in every programme has a prescribed text or a named source
      material. No subject without a source. When this check was first run it
      FAILED on thirteen subjects - including التربية الإسلامية itself, the
      first book a child ever holds. Chapter Four of the register is the
      remedy.

L-31  عدّ الكتب بقاعدة واحدة معلنة
      Books are counted by one declared rule, not by hand:
        - كتاب القرآن وعلومه counts as 1 in every class, all twelve years
        - one book for every independent FULL-YEAR subject
        - all term courses share ONE volume: كراسة المقررات الفصلية
        - مدمج and مضمّن carry NO book
        - التجويد carries no separate book; its matn is bound inside
          كتاب القرآن وعلومه
        - the muṣḥaf is a source and is never counted
      This rule exists because the book counts had drifted between documents -
      the same class showing 9 books in one file and 11 in another - purely
      because each had been tallied separately by hand.

L-32  التجويد مادة مستقلة من الرابع إلى الحادي عشر — حصةٌ ومتنٌ وورقة.
      لا يُضمَّن في الساعة القرآنية في الصفوف المتقدمة
      Tajwīd is an INDEPENDENT subject from class 4 to class 11: its own weekly
      lesson, its own matn, its own paper. It is NOT folded into the Qur'anic
      hour in the advanced classes. The hour is for ḥifẓ and murājaʿah. See
      Part 7.

L-33  الرسم العثماني وفرش رواية حفص بابان داخل منهج التجويد في التاسع —
      لا مادتان ولا مقرران. والرواية وصفُ المصحف لا مادةَ تُدرَّس
      ʿUthmānī orthography and the farsh of Ḥafṣ are CHAPTERS inside the class-9
      tajwīd syllabus - not subjects and not courses. The riwāyah is a
      description of the muṣḥaf we read from (L-23), not a subject on a
      timetable.

L-34  لا يُقدَّم علمٌ على آلته. ولا تُفتَح البلاغة قبل تمام النحو الأساس —
      فأولُ موضعها العاشر، بعد ختم قطر الندى في التاسع
      NO SCIENCE BEFORE ITS INSTRUMENT. Balāghah is not opened before
      foundational naḥw is complete - its first place is class 10, after Qaṭr
      al-Nadā closes in class 9. See Part 5.

L-35  ترتيب البلاغة: المعاني ← البيان ← البديع. لأن المعاني نحوٌ مطبَّق، والبيان قائم عليه
      The order within balāghah is maʿānī, then bayān, then badīʿ - because
      maʿānī is applied grammar and bayān rests upon it. This is the order of
      the Talkhīṣ and the Miftāḥ. Our draft had inverted the first two.

L-36  لا تفسير قبل الصف الخامس. أولُ موضع التفسير الخامس، ولا يُلقَّن في الأول إلى الرابع
      NO TAFSĪR BEFORE CLASS FIVE. Tafsīr had been taught from class one,
      embedded in the Qurʾānic hour, the meaning given alongside each sabaq.
      But in classes one to four the child is unlocking the letter and working
      toward fluency - بوابة القراءة at one, بوابة الطلاقة at three. Giving him
      the meaning before he can read the words is a science before its
      instrument, the same fault L-34 names. The chapter's own rule already
      said tafsīr follows what is memorised; it should follow it from the point
      the child reads. First place: CLASS FIVE, alongside الشهادة الابتدائية.
      It held no slot in classes 1-4, so the timetable was unaffected.

L-37  العربية آلةُ العلوم كلِّها. ففي الإعدادي لا ينزل برنامج اللغة عن الدراسات
      الإسلامية. وفي الثانوي على ألّا تنزل اللغة عن الثلث
      ARABIC IS THE INSTRUMENT OF EVERY SHARʿĪ SCIENCE. The language had been
      collapsing at the bridge: 61.5% of the week in class 6, 31.6% in class 7,
      while Islamic studies took 52.6% - its highest share anywhere in the
      school. Giving the sciences more time than the instrument they depend on
      is the fault of L-34 at the level of the programme. In Ideadi, the section
      that BUILDS the instrument, the language programme does not fall below
      Islamic studies. In Thanawi the instrument is USED on the major matns, so
      the sharʿī share may rise - against a floor of one third.
      (This rule was first written to cover Thanawi too; measurement showed
      classes 11 and 12 failing it, and the RULE was narrowed rather than the
      curriculum bent to it.)

L-38  اللغة العربية ليست النحو. الإنشاء والتعبير مادةُ اللغة الحية في الابتدائي
      والإعدادي، ثم تنضج بحثًا وخطابةً في الثانوي
      THE LANGUAGE IS NOT ITS GRAMMAR. In class 8 the instrumental sciences held
      15 lesson-terms against 6 for the living language. And the communication
      subject was absent from classes 7, 8 and 9 entirely, with الترجمة absent
      before class 10 - while L-18 makes both a priority. The living language
      now runs unbroken from class 4 to class 12, with الترجمة إلى اليوربا
      embedded in it from class 7.
          LIVING LANGUAGE   اللغة العربية (مطالعة · محادثة · استيعاب · نصوص) ·
                            الإنشاء والتعبير / البحث والخطابة · النشيد
                            والمحفوظات · النقد والآداب · الترجمة
          INSTRUMENTS       النحو · الصرف · العروض وعلم القافية ·
                            فقه اللغة والمعاجم

L-39  لا يُقحَم اسمُ مرحلةٍ في غيرها. البحث عملُ الثانوي لا الإعدادي
      A STAGE'S NAME IS NOT IMPORTED INTO ANOTHER STAGE. البحث is the work of
      Thanawi. A boy of thirteen composes and expresses; he does not referee
      research. The ladder:
          classes 4-6    الإنشاء والتعبير  embedded in اللغة العربية
          class 7        الإنشاء والتعبير  التعبير الشفوي والكتابي · الوصف والسرد
          class 8        الإنشاء والتعبير  الإنشاء الموجَّه · الرسالة والتقرير
          class 9        الإنشاء والتعبير  الإنشاء الحر والإلقاء · الخطبة القصيرة
          classes 10-12  البحث والخطابة   البحث المحكّم · الخطبة · المناظرة
      The instrumental sciences at Ideadi are النحو والصرف, and they are not the
      living language and do not stand in for it.

PART 5 - TARTĪB AL-ʿULŪM: NO SCIENCE BEFORE ITS INSTRUMENT (L-34, L-35)
================================================================================

This is a doctrine, not a scheduling convenience. Stated in the register:

    العلمُ الذي يقوم على غيره لا يُفتَح قبل تمام ما يقوم عليه.
    وهذا ليس ترتيبًا تنظيميًّا يُتسامح فيه، بل شرطُ فهمٍ: من لقي «التقديم والتأخير»
    مصطلحًا بلاغيًّا قبل أن يعرفه إعرابًا، حفظ الاسم ولم يفهم المسمَّى.

    "A science that stands on another is not opened before the one it stands on
    is complete. This is not an organisational nicety to be relaxed; it is a
    condition of understanding. Whoever meets 'fronting and postponement' as a
    rhetorical term before knowing it as iʿrāb has memorised the name and not
    understood the thing named."

PART 7 - TAJWĪD ACROSS THE TWELVE CLASSES (L-32, L-33)
================================================================================

This was one of the four errors the Director General personally corrected. The
principle:

    الساعة القرآنية للحفظ والمراجعة. والتجويد علمٌ له حصته ومتنه وورقته.
    "The Qur'anic hour is for memorisation and revision. Tajwīd is a science
    with its own lesson, its own matn and its own paper."

Folding the science into the memorisation hour in the advanced classes left a
discipline that has a matn and no lesson. The Qur'anic sciences board had
already ruled that tajwīd should be independent - and that ruling was mislaid
during a rebuild.

    CLASS      FORM          SLOTS   MATN AND SYLLABUS
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1-3        مضمّن         -       No matn. Correction of makhārij and ṣifāt
                                     by ear and by talqīn only.
    4          مستقل         1       تحفة الأطفال والغلمان
    5          مستقل         1       تحفة الأطفال, completed
    6          مستقل         1       المقدمة الجزرية - introduction
    7          مستقل         1       الجزرية - المخارج والصفات
    8          مستقل         1       الجزرية - الأحكام
    9          مستقل         1       الجزرية completed · الوقف والابتدا ·
                                     فرش رواية حفص · الرسم العثماني
    10         مستقل         1       الإتقان - application across the whole
                                     muṣḥaf
    11         مستقل         1       الإقراء والتصحيح - preparation for the
                                     الثانوية القرآنية committee
    12         مضمّن         -       No matn. The science completed at class 11;
                                     here it is maintenance and checking only.

    INDEPENDENT IN EIGHT CLASSES (4-11) = 208 HOURS.

The two bolded items at class 9 - فرش رواية حفص and الرسم العثماني - are
CHAPTERS OF THIS SYLLABUS. They are not courses and must never be re-entered as
subjects.

Tajwīd's matn is bound INSIDE كتاب القرآن وعلومه, which is why making it
independent did not break the three-book floor in classes 4-6.

FUNDING: the weekly slots for tajwīd were taken one at a time from اللغة
العربية (classes 4,5,6), التربية الإسلامية (7,8), النحو (9), البحث والخطابة

PART 8 - THE TERM-COURSE ARCHITECTURE (L-11, L-12)
================================================================================

This is one of the defining features of the design and it came directly from the
Director General:

    "It is not necessary that you compress the subject into the advanced
    classes... some may be learned maybe even for just a term, like a course.
    Knowledge of inheritance to just be learned maybe for a term, first term of
    SS 2... like university courses. Sometimes some may drop and return."

MECHANISM: a خانة فصلية (term-slot) is ONE weekly slot that carries THREE
DIFFERENT COURSES across the three terms of a year. The slot is counted ONCE in
the weekly ledger, not three times. (An early version counted it once per
course, which produced a class 9 carrying 23 slots against a ceiling of 19.)

All term courses in a class share ONE bound volume, كراسة المقررات الفصلية,
counted as a single book under L-31.

THE TERM SLOTS AS THEY NOW STAND:

    CLASS 9  - خانة فصلية [2 slots/week]
        term 1  النقد والآداب     chosen texts, tasted, no rhetorical
                                  terminology - preparing for balāghah
        term 2  العروض وعلم القافية   the famous metres by EAR - ear training
        term 3  مصطلح الحديث      البيقونية, memorised

    CLASS 10 - خانة فصلية [1 slot/week]
        term 1  التاريخ والسيرة   الإسلام في نيجيريا - الشيخ آدم الإلوري
        term 2  النقد والآداب     pre-Islamic and Islamic texts
        term 3  الصرف             لامية الأفعال completed, applied taṣrīf

    CLASS 11 - خانة مكثفة [2 slots/week]  (an INTENSIVE slot)
        terms 1+2  الفرائض        متن الرحبية · 52 lessons · its own paper and
                                  its own certificate line (L-19)
        term 3     أصول التفسير   مقدمة ابن تيمية

    CLASS 12 - خانة فصلية (أ) [1 slot/week]
        term 1  الفرائض التطبيقية  estates · the Sharīʿah Court of Appeal ·
                                   the Marriage Act · إدي-إيجي and أوري-أوجوري
        term 2  الدراسات الحديثة   النوازل and the fiqh of coexistence
        term 3  علم الكلام         studied ABOUT, not studied FOR

    CLASS 12 - خانة فصلية (ب) [1 slot/week]
        term 1  النقد والآداب      Andalusian and African literature
        term 2  العروض وعلم القافية  علم القافية - the closing unit
        term 3  المنطق             التصورات والتصديقات

WHY THIS MATTERS: the term-course architecture is what removed the pressure
that had earlier produced indiscriminate MERGING of subjects. Before it existed,
the only way to fit a subject into a full timetable was to merge it into another
subject, which destroyed its identity. Term courses let a subject appear
properly, with its own paper, for a defined stretch.

PART 9 - MADHHAB POLICY AND THE NON-EXCLUSIVITY TEST
================================================================================

THE RULING (L-21, L-22): the school is أهل السنة with NO exclusive commitment to
one school of law, and the declared frame MAY DIFFER FROM TOPIC TO TOPIC.

A core text is a DECLARED TEACHING FRAME (إطار تعليمي مُعلَن), never an
affiliation (انتساب). And no single school frames every science. The ladder as
actually built runs:

    فروع الفقه        مالكي declared (المقدمة الأخضرية, الرسالة, بداية المجتهد)
    أصول الفقه        شافعي declared (الورقات, نظم الورقات)
    الفرائض           شافعي declared (متن الرحبية)
    مصطلح الحديث      شافعي declared (نخبة الفكر, نزهة النظر)
    أول متون الحديث   حنبلي (عمدة الأحكام, جامع العلوم والحكم)
    أول متون العقيدة  حنبلي (لمعة الاعتقاد)
    عمود العقيدة      حنفي المؤلف (العقيدة الطحاوية - the widest thing the
                      people of the Sunnah agreed upon)
    البدعة            مالكي deliberately (الاعتصام للشاطبي)
    الصوت الحنفي      الطحاوي, شرح معاني الآثار
    النظير الإقليمي   عثمان بن فودي, إحياء السنة - West African

CRITICAL RULE (L-29): DO NOT INFER THE SCHOOL'S CREED OR MADHHAB FROM THE
BOOKLIST. The texts above are proposed by drafting work. They do not establish
orientation. Only an explicit ruling does.

مِعيار عدم الحصر - THE NON-EXCLUSIVITY CONFORMITY TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A mechanical test, so that "no exclusive madhhab" is auditable rather than
aspirational. All five must hold:

    1. Every course DECLARES its frame explicitly in its documents.
    2. A BOUND REGISTER of the reasons for disagreement (سجل مدارك الخلاف) is
       maintained.
    3. All four schools appear TWICE OR MORE in the distribution, and NO SINGLE
       SCHOOL exceeds 50%.
    4. A comparative-disagreement question is worth 16% OF EVERY FIQH PAPER
       from class 10 onward, AND an answer given in a single madhhab may not
       score more than HALF the marks on it.
    5. The external reviewer is NOT from the frame school.

TEXTS EXPLICITLY REJECTED AS MATNS (these decisions are locked):
    متن البناء في الصرف    rejected - outside the recovered scope of ṣarf (7-11)
                           and its attribution is not verified
    ملحة الإعراب           NOT taught as a matn - it is memorisation repertoire
    مختصر خليل             rejected as a matn - for familiarity only
    كشف الشبهات            for the TEACHER only, at every class - never a
                           student text
    نواقض الإسلام          taught AFTER the موانع (impediments), never before
    كتاب التوحيد           a limited unit, NOT a pillar
    جوهرة التوحيد          read as a DOCUMENT, not taught as doctrine

PART 10 - ASSESSMENT, THE SCALE, THE TRANSCRIPT AND THE GATES

10.1  THE DOCTRINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instructional form determines assessment consequence MECHANICALLY. There is no
discretion. The table is in Part 3. The one rule that must never bend:

    الشرط لا يحمل درجة أبدًا  -  "A condition never carries a mark."

10.2  THE THREE-SECTION TRANSCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    SECTION A   Graded subjects. Enter the average. مستقل، مدمج، مضمّن.
    SECTION B   Units (وحدات). Achievement ratings. Do NOT enter the average.
    SECTION C   Conditions (شروط). Met / not met. NO MARKS EVER.

    Plus, for classes 1-2 only: a DESCRIPTIVE APPENDIX reading متقن / نامٍ /
    مبتدئ, with NO NUMBER. A small child gets one mark in Section A and a
    description - not a spread of numbers.

10.3  THE SCALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    امتياز    80 and above    (excellence)
    جدارة     70              (merit)
    إجادة     60              (proficiency)
    مقبول     50              (pass)
    الشهادة الإعدادية requires 55 or above.

10.4  THE GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    الثانوية القرآنية      class 11   TWELVE JUZʾ MEMORISED TO A CERTIFIED
                                      STANDARD (متقنة), with FIFTEEN JUZʾ as

                                      (المناقشة) + PROOF OF SITTING WAEC

10.5  THE QUR'AN TARGET - WHY TWELVE AND NOT FIFTEEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is important and must not be quietly reverted. The original target was
FIFTEEN JUZʾ. That target was calculated on THIRTEEN funded years, including a
kindergarten (روضة). Two things then changed and the target was never
recalculated:

    - the kindergarten was abolished (L-01), removing funded years
    - the gate moved to class 11, removing another

The honest recalculation gives TWELVE JUZʾ MEMORISED TO A CERTIFIED STANDARD as
the certified requirement, with FIFTEEN retained as مرتبة الامتياز. A school
that certifies fifteen on twelve years of funding is certifying something it
did not fund. There is also an optional مسار الحفاظ (ḥuffāẓ track) from class 4
for those capable of more, sealed by a full khatmah before an EXTERNAL
committee of shaykhs.

PART 12 - ERRORS ALREADY MADE AND CORRECTED - DO NOT REPEAT THESE
================================================================================

This section exists so that the next person does not spend weeks rediscovering
what has already been settled. Every item below actually happened.

12.1  STRUCTURAL AND ARITHMETIC ERRORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. A whole recovered document (SHRS-ACD-2026-007, Part 4, sections 4.1-4.4)
    containing a complete timetable and ledger was MISSED, and version 1 of the
    ledger was built on invented scenarios instead. Always read the recovered
    corpus completely before building.

 2. A "monotonic status" rule was INVENTED - once a subject rose in status it
    could never be demoted. This was never ruled and was withdrawn. It is not
    compulsory to teach every subject every term.

 3. All eleven sharʿī sciences were made STRANDS at classes 1-6, including the
    instrumental sciences (أصول الفقه، مصطلح الحديث، المنطق). A child of six
    does not have a strand of uṣūl al-fiqh. Corrected to four legitimate
    strands: التوحيد، الفقه، الحديث، السيرة.

 4. A shared term-slot was counted ONCE PER COURSE instead of once per slot,
    producing a class 9 carrying 23 slots against a ceiling of 19.

 5. The MUṢḤAF was counted as Programme One's book, which left the lower
    classes with books for only two of three programmes. The muṣḥaf is a
    source. كتاب القرآن وعلومه was created to be Programme One's actual book.

 6. WAEC had ZERO TIMETABLED MINUTES at class 12 while gating the leaving
    certificate - and a "38/38 subjects accounted for" check still balanced,
    because a mandatory claimant had never been entered into the ledger at all.
    This is the origin of L-07.

 7. A timetable was built on allocation matrix v2 while the matrix in force was
    v3 - two internally verified documents specifying different weeks.

 8. Asymmetric مدمج notation (السيرة I / التاريخ ●) was recording a HOST-GUEST
    relation, not a merge - so a ruling to merge sīrah with history had never
    actually been implemented despite appearing to be.

 9. FOUR SUBJECTS WERE SILENTLY DROPPED during an unrelated balāghah rebuild:
    أصول التفسير · التاريخ والسيرة · الترجمة · مصطلح الحديث. The Director
    General noticed. This is the single most characteristic failure of this
    project.

10. The L-30 check FAILED on its first run: thirteen subjects had no source at
    all, including التربية الإسلامية - the first book a child ever holds.

11. The three-programme structure was forgotten, and then the four sections
    were forgotten, in successive rebuilds.

12. The Qur'anic sciences board's ruling that تجويد be مستقل at classes 4-11
    was OVERRIDDEN in a rebuild, folding the science back into the memorisation
    hour. This is now L-32.

13. الرسم العثماني was carried as an independent course and رواية حفص as
    another. Neither is a course. This is now L-33.

14. البلاغة was opened in class 9 term 1 while قطر الندى was still unfinished
    that same year - teaching a science before its instrument. And within
    balāghah the order was بيان then معاني, which is inverted. These are now
    L-34 and L-35.

15. التفسير was taught from class one, embedded in the Qurʾānic hour, to
    children still unlocking the letter. Meaning before reading is a science
    before its instrument. It now begins at class five. This is now L-36.

16. The language collapsed at the bridge — 61.5% of the week in class 6, 31.6%
    in class 7, Islamic studies at 52.6%. البحث والخطابة, the priority subject,
    was absent from classes 7-9 entirely and الترجمة from every class before 10.
    This is now L-37 and L-38.

17. البحث والخطابة was put into Ideadi as the fix for the above — which was the
    same fault one layer over: a subject before its stage. A boy of thirteen
    composes; he does not referee research. And the same error had been sitting
    unnoticed at classes 4-6, where the label read البحث والخطابة while the
    content beneath it already read «الإنشاء والتعبير» — right content, imported
    name. This is now L-39.

18. Book counts drifted between documents - the same class showing 9 in one
    file and 11 in another - purely because each file was tallied by hand. This
    is now L-31.

19. A Primary 6 regression during the tajwīd fix pushed that class to FOUR
    books by splitting قواعد اللغة الوظيفية out as its own entry. It folds back
    into the language book carrying بوابة الجسر.

20. The annual ledger claimed 754 hours where 100 minutes had been booked as
    مراجعة when 60 of those minutes were wuḍūʾ and dismissal. Corrected to 715.

21. The Qur'an target of fifteen juzʾ was calculated on thirteen funded years
    including a kindergarten that was later abolished, and was never
    recalculated.

PART 13 - HOW THE LOCK IS ENFORCED MECHANICALLY
================================================================================

A register that only a reader checks is how every previous decision was lost.
So the register is now enforced by a script.

    RUN:  cd docs/curriculum && python3 verify.py

It checks the allocation data against the register and EXITS NON-ZERO if any
locked item is broken. A non-zero exit means the document is not ready and is
not published.

THIRTY OF THE THIRTY-NINE ITEMS ARE CHECKED MECHANICALLY:

    L-01/02   twelve classes in four sections
    L-03/04   every subject under one of the three programmes
    L-05      lower classes: everything merged or embedded
    L-06/31   three books minimum, one per programme, classes 1-6
    L-07      no name without a lesson or a named host
    L-09      the load rises with the class
    L-10      the graduating class carries the most
    L-11      every term slot carries exactly three terms
    L-13      the cornerstones never pause
    L-14      balāghah carries six full terms or more
    L-15      prosody is never taught in a single term
    L-16      criticism and literature keeps its weight
    L-17      ṣarf is present in every advanced class
    L-18      translation into Yoruba is carried, not dropped
    L-19      farāʾiḍ is ring-fenced
    L-20      the Qur'anic hour untouched
    L-24/25   every class balances its weekly lessons
    L-30      every subject has a named source
    L-32      tajwīd independent in classes 4-11
    L-33      rasm ʿUthmānī / riwāyat Ḥafṣ never counted as courses
    L-34      no science opened before its instrument
    L-35      balāghah runs maʿānī -> bayān -> badīʿ
    L-36      no tafsīr before class five
    L-37      Arabic >= Islamic studies at Ideadi; >= one third at Thanawi
    L-38      the living language unbroken 4-12, الترجمة 7-12
    L-39      الإنشاء والتعبير 4-9, البحث والخطابة 10-12, never swapped

NINE ITEMS CANNOT BE CHECKED BY A SCRIPT, and the script says so explicitly on

    L-08  the closed vocabulary of instructional forms
    L-12  a subject returning HIGHER rather than repeating
    L-21  ahl al-sunnah without exclusive madhhab
    L-22  the frame may differ from topic to topic
    L-23  the riwāyah and the muṣḥaf
    L-26  a missing document is never invented
    L-27  recovered material never mixed with a current ruling
    L-28  an inference never presented as recovered text

The script closes with:

    والتسعةُ الباقية تُقرأ ولا تُفحَص — فلا يُقال «تمّ التحقق» حتى تُقرأ.
    "The remaining nine are read, not checked - so it is not said that
    verification is complete until they have been read."

A GREEN RUN IS NOT A VERIFICATION UNTIL A PERSON HAS READ THOSE NINE.

Primary 6 fourth book, الترجمة silently dropped, tajwīd folded back into the
hour, الرسم العثماني re-added as a course, and balāghah opened at class 9. In
each case the relevant checks fail and the script exits 1. A check that cannot
fail is not a lock.
