SULTAN HANAFI ROYAL SCHOOLS (SHRS / GACAIS) — CURRICULUM BRIEFING (SHORT FORM)
================================================================================
A supplementary Islamic and Arabic school in the Yoruba-speaking region of
Nigeria (Ilorin / Ibadan). One coherent twelve-year system, not twelve year
plans stitched together. Everything below is SETTLED and must not be redesigned.
Write all subject names and text titles IN ARABIC.

THE ONE THING TO UNDERSTAND FIRST
Every document in this project was at some point rebuilt from whatever was in
front of the rebuilder, and each time, decisions settled earlier but absent from
THAT file were silently dropped. Four subjects vanished this way once. Do not
rebuild from context. The rule is:
    "لا يُغيَّر بند مقفل إلا بقرار صريح جديد من المدير العام. وما ليس في هذا السجل فليس بقرار."
    A locked item changes only by explicit new ruling. What is not in the
    register is not a decision.

STRUCTURE
  12 classes exactly. No kindergarten, no stage before class 1.
  4 sections: التمهيدي (1-2) · الابتدائي (3-6) · الإعدادي (7-9) · الثانوي (10-12)
  3 programmes, and EVERY subject falls under one of them:
      البرنامج الأول   القرآن وعلومه
      البرنامج الثاني  اللغة وعلومها      <- NOT "اللغة العربية وعلومها"
      البرنامج الثالث  الدراسات الإسلامية

TIME
  Mon-Wed 14:00-18:00 · Thu-Fri no school · Sat-Sun 09:00-15:00
  حصة = 40 min · فصل = 13 weeks · year = 39 weeks in 3 terms
  Weekly slots: 13 in classes 1-6, 19 in classes 7-12
  الساعة القرآنية — a protected daily hour OUTSIDE those slots (290 min/wk lower,
  340 upper). NEVER drawn upon by any subject, in any class.
  Year: 526h30m (classes 1-6) / 715h (7-12). Whole journey 7,449 hours.

INSTRUCTIONAL FORMS — closed vocabulary, invent no seventh
  مستقل  independent: own slot, own book, own paper, enters the average
  مدمج   merged with ONE named partner: no book, a NAMED SECTION on the
         partner's paper with a 40% floor
  مضمّن  embedded in a NAMED HOST: no slot, no book, assessed inside the host
  وحدة   examined block with no slot; consumes its host's minutes; Section B,
         does NOT enter the average
  مسار   a strand inside a book: no mark, descriptive only (متقن/نامٍ/مبتدئ)
  شرط    a condition: NEVER CARRIES A MARK. Section C, met or not met.
  GOVERNING RULE: لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة — no subject on any list without lessons or a
  named host. (WAEC once gated the certificate with zero timetabled minutes and
  a "38/38 accounted for" check still balanced.)

THE DESIGN IDEAS THAT MATTER MOST
  1. Lower classes (1-6): EVERYTHING merged or embedded. The child is not
     burdened with books. Minimum THREE books — one per programme — plus the
     muṣḥaf. The muṣḥaf is a source, never counted as Programme One's book;
     كتاب القرآن وعلومه is Programme One's actual book.
  2. TERM COURSES, like a university. Not every subject every term. One weekly
     slot (خانة فصلية) carries three different courses across the year, and is
     counted ONCE in the ledger. A subject may pause and return — provided it
     returns HIGHER, not as repetition.
  3. The load RISES with the class, and THE GRADUATING CLASS CARRIES THE MOST
     (27 sciences, 14 books). A board once reduced it and was reversed.
  4. CORNERSTONES NEVER PAUSE — النحو · اللغة العربية · الفقه · العقيدة ·
     الحديث · التفسير run continuously from class 7 to 12.
  5. NO SCIENCE BEFORE ITS INSTRUMENT (لا يُقدَّم علمٌ على آلته). البلاغة does not
     open before foundational naḥw is complete — first place is CLASS 10, after
     قطر الندى closes in class 9. And within it the order is المعاني (10) →
     البيان (11) → البديع (12), the order of the Talkhīṣ and the Miftāḥ, because
     maʿānī is applied grammar and bayān rests on it. What precedes balāghah in
     class 9 is النقد والآداب — texts tasted, WITH NO RHETORICAL TERMINOLOGY.
     Taste first; class 10 then names what the student had been finding.
  6. NO TAFSĪR BEFORE CLASS FIVE. It had been taught from class one inside the
     Qur'anic hour; a child still unlocking the letter was being given the
     meaning before he could read the words — a science before its instrument.
     It now begins at class 5, alongside الشهادة الابتدائية, and runs to 12.
     التفسير يتبع المحفوظ — tafsīr follows what is memorised (classes 5-9);
     from class 10 it parts from it, because the graduate must read tafsīr of
     what he has not memorised.
  7. التجويد IS AN INDEPENDENT SUBJECT, classes 4-11 — its own lesson, matn and
     paper. It is NOT folded into the Qur'anic hour in the advanced classes; the
     hour is for ḥifẓ and murājaʿah. تحفة الأطفال (4-5) → الجزرية (6-9) →
     الإتقان (10) → الإقراء والتصحيح (11). 208 hours. Embedded without a matn in
     classes 1-3 and, as maintenance only, in class 12.
  8. الرسم العثماني and فرش رواية حفص are CHAPTERS inside the class-9 tajwīd
     syllabus — NOT courses, NOT subjects. رواية حفص عن عاصم is a description of
     the muṣḥaf we read from, not a subject taught. علوم القرآن is likewise not
     a standalone course.
  9. الخطابة والتواصل are a priority, and with them الترجمة إلى اليوربا — the
     graduate renders his research and khuṭbah back into Yoruba.
 10. البلاغة 9 terms (floor 6) · العروض never a single term · النقد والآداب a
     subject with weight, not a small unit · الصرف in EVERY advanced class ·
     الفرائض ring-fenced with its own paper and certificate line.

MADHHAB
أهل السنة with NO exclusive commitment to one school, and the declared frame MAY
DIFFER FROM TOPIC TO TOPIC. A core text is a DECLARED TEACHING FRAME (إطار
تعليمي مُعلَن), never an affiliation. As built: مالكي in furūʿ · شافعي in uṣūl,
farāʾiḍ and muṣṭalaḥ · حنبلي in the first ḥadīth and creed matns · حنفي in the
creedal spine (الطحاوية) · مالكي deliberately for bidʿah (الاعتصام) · a West
African regional peer (عثمان بن فودي).
  مِعيار عدم الحصر — the conformity test, all five must hold: every course
  declares its frame · a bound register of the reasons for disagreement · all
  four schools appear twice or more with none above 50% · a comparative question
  worth 16% of every fiqh paper from class 10, with a single-madhhab answer
  capped at half marks · an external reviewer NOT of the frame school.
  CRITICAL: never infer the school's creed or madhhab from a booklist.
  Riwāyah: حفص عن عاصم. Muṣḥaf: مطبعة الملك فهد.

ASSESSMENT AND GATES
  Scale: امتياز 80 · جدارة 70 · إجادة 60 · مقبول 50. الشهادة الإعدادية needs 55+.
  Transcript: Section A graded · Section B units (no average) · Section C
  conditions (NO MARKS EVER).
  Gates: بوابة القراءة (1) · الطلاقة (3) · الشهادة الابتدائية (5) · بوابة الجسر
  (6) · الشهادة الإعدادية (9, external examiner) · الثانوية القرآنية (11) ·
  شهادة التخرج (12: ten competencies + viva + proof of sitting WAEC).
  QUR'AN TARGET: TWELVE JUZʾ certified (متقنة), with FIFTEEN as مرتبة الامتياز.
  The original fifteen was calculated on thirteen funded years including a
  kindergarten that was later abolished, and was never recalculated. A school
  that certifies fifteen on twelve years is certifying what it did not fund.

RULES OF EVIDENCE — these bind you
  A missing document is never invented. Recovered material is never mixed with a
  current ruling. An inference is never presented as recovered text. Creed and
  madhhab are never inferred from booklists. Every subject has a named source
  (when this was first checked, THIRTEEN subjects had none — including التربية
  الإسلامية, the first book a child holds). School-authored material is adopted
  exactly as a matn; what is not yet written is A DEBT OWED BY THE SCHOOL, never
  a gap in the curriculum.

IF YOU ARE ASKED TO WORK ON THIS
  Do not redesign what is above. Do not rebuild a document from whatever is in
  front of you. If a change would break one of these, STOP AND ASK first. A
  mathematically balanced timetable is not automatically a curriculum solution —
  ask what a child of that age actually meets, what book is in his hand, and
  which science he needs before this one.
