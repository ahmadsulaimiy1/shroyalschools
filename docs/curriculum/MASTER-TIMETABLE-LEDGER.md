## SHRS G1–G12 · rebuilt from the constitutional operating week

**Supersedes:** every period, minute and hour figure used in
`REDLINE-arithmetic-audit.md` and in the 26 subject lifecycles.

**Status of this document:** it establishes how much instructional time
*exists*. It does not allocate that time to any subject. Allocation is a
separate act, and this ledger is its only permitted source.

## 0. Why the prior arithmetic is void

Every figure produced before this document rested on three assumptions that
were never checked against a clock:

| Assumption used | Status | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Wed evening = 240 usable minutes | **FALSE** — 240 is gross clock time | 30 min/day of protected time (ʿAṣr 20 + assembly 10) was spent twice |
| The week contains 5 × full instructional days at full length | **FALSE** — the five days are unequal (210 / 210 / 210 / 360 / 360 gross-to-usable) | "lessons per week" was treated as day-independent |
| 39 instructional weeks × 5 days = 195 instructional days | **FALSE** — exam weeks and mid-term breaks are inside those 39 weeks | the year was overstated by 24 instructional days |

No prior over/under figure survives. They are re-derived in §7 below.

### 1.1 Two unresolved collisions in the week as stated

These are raised, not silently resolved. Neither is an objection to the
directive; both are facts the directive does not yet address.

**(a) Jumuʿah.** Friday runs 09:00–15:00. Jumuʿah falls inside that window.
A 20-minute Ẓuhr slot cannot absorb khuṭbah, ṣalāh, and movement to and from
the muṣallā. A realistic Friday mid-day block is 75–90 minutes.
`[COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]` — is Friday an instructional day with a
Jumuʿah block, a half day ending before Jumuʿah, or is the Friday session
itself to be reconsidered?

**(b) Conflict with the recovered calendar.** ACD-007 Part 4 (pp.19–21)
records `[RECOVERED]`:

> الإثنين والثلاثاء والأربعاء ٢:٠٠–٦:٠٠ مساءً · **الخميس والجمعة: لا دوام** ·
> **السبت والأحد ٩:٠٠ صباحًا–٣:٠٠ ظهرًا**

The recovered week places the two day-sessions on **Saturday and Sunday** and
declares Thursday and Friday **non-attendance days**. The current direction
places them on **Thursday and Friday**. The recovered design is internally
coherent — it lets pupils attend conventional schooling Monday–Friday and
brings them to SHRS in the evenings and at the weekend. Moving the day-sessions
to Thursday–Friday removes that compatibility and creates collision (a).

This ledger is built on the **current direction**, as Layer B governs. The
discrepancy is recorded, not merged. `[COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]`

## 4. Layer 5: term totals

### 4.1 The year is 171 instructional days, not 195 and not 360

`[CALCULATED FROM RECOVERED DATA]` — ACD-010's 195 hrs/year for the Qur'an
hour is only arithmetically possible as 60 min × 195 days, i.e. 39 weeks ×
5 days. That treats all 39 weeks as fully instructional.

Real structure of a 13-week term:

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Calendar weeks in term | 13 |
| Examination period | −1 full week |
| Mid-term break | −3 instructional days |
| **Instructional days in term** | **57** |
| **Instructional days in year** (×3) | **171** |

**Shortfall against the recovered figure: 24 instructional days per year.**

### 4.2 The placement of the mid-term break is a curriculum decision

The five days are unequal, so *which three days* the break removes changes the
minutes removed:

| Break placed on | Minutes withdrawn per term | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Mon / Tue / Wed | 3 × 210 = **630** | 1,890 |
| Thu / Fri + one evening | 300 + 300 + 210 = **810** | 2,430 |
| **Difference** | **180** | **540** |

540 minutes a year is nine hours — two and a half whole evening sessions,
lost or kept on the placement of a break. A decision normally left to an
administrator is worth that much instruction.

**Recommended: break placed Monday–Wednesday.** It is the cheaper option in
instructional minutes *and* the better rest, because it interrupts the evening
sequence rather than the long-form day-sessions where matn work is done.

## 6. The ceiling — and the redline on "19 lessons"

`[RECOVERED]` ceilings: **7 lessons/week at G1–G2**, **19 lessons/week at
G3–G12**, lesson = 40 minutes, covering all of Programme 2 + Programme 3.

The contested pool expressed in 40-minute Standard Lesson Units (SLU — an
accounting unit for comparison only, **not** a timetable period):

| Scenario | Weekly contested pool | ÷ 40 | vs the 19 ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1,050 | **26.25** | +7.25 |
| B | 990 | **24.75** | +5.75 |
| **C** | **930** | **23.25** | **+4.25** |
| D | 870 | **21.75** | +2.75 |

**The 19-lesson ceiling cannot be derived from the clock in any scenario.**
The narrowest reading of the constitutional week contains 21.75 lessons; the
widest contains 26.25. Nothing in the recovered corpus shows a derivation for 19.

Two readings are possible and they are not the same thing:

- **19 is a policy cap** — on pupil attention, teacher load, or preparation
  burden — deliberately set below what the clock allows. If so it stands, and
  the surplus of 2.75–7.25 lessons/week is deliberate slack, not free space.
- **19 was an estimate of the clock** — in which case it was wrong, and the
  real ceiling is the clock ceiling.

`[COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]` This is the single highest-value open question
in the ledger. Every over/under figure in §7 depends on which reading holds.

### 6.1 G1–G2: the ceiling accounts for less than half the week

At G1–G2 the recovered ceiling is 7 lessons = 280 minutes, plus the Qur'an
hour 300 = **580 minutes** of a **1,230-minute** week.

**650 minutes a week — 53% of the constitutional week — is unaccounted for at
G1–G2.** Either:

- the G1–G2 week is genuinely shorter than the G3–G12 week, and the ledger
  must carry a separate usable-time line for it; or
- the constitutional week applies unmodified to six-year-olds, in which case
  a G1 pupil sits 1,230 instructional minutes — 20.5 hours — per week, and
  650 of them have no subject attached.

`[COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]` The ledger cannot allocate G1–G2 time until this
is answered. **This, not the Qur'an hour, is the real G1–G2 problem**, and it
was invisible until the week was costed.

## 8. Candidate operational allocation

**Analytical demand and operational allocation are different things.** §3–§6
may carry decimals; a timetable may not. Period lengths below are chosen from
the permitted set (20/25/30/35/40/45/50/60/75) by pedagogical justification,
and each grid sums to its usable total exactly. Nothing is rounded.

### 8.1 Evening day (Mon / Tue / Wed) — 240 gross, 210 usable

| From | To | Block | Min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14:00 | 14:10 | الطابور — assembly | 10 *(protected)* |
| 14:10 | 15:10 | **ساعة القرآن** | 60 |
| 15:10 | 16:00 | Period 1 | 50 |
| 16:00 | 16:20 | **صلاة العصر** | 20 *(protected)* |
| 16:20 | 17:10 | Period 2 | 50 |
| 17:10 | 18:00 | Period 3 | 50 |
| | | **Gross** | **240** |
| | | **Usable** | **60 + 150 = 210** ✓ |

**Why 50 minutes.** These pupils have already been schooled all day. 60 is
past the attention limit of a late-afternoon child; 40 is too short to seat a
matn segment, its explanation and a drill in one sitting. 50 seats all three
and leaves the day at exactly three subject slots — a number a child can hold.

**Why ʿAṣr at 16:00.** It is the natural mid-point and it *is* the break; no
separate break is scheduled. ʿAṣr drifts seasonally in Ilorin (≈15:45–16:20),
so **the slot is the anchor, not the clock time** — Period 1 ends at ʿAṣr,
whenever ʿAṣr is, and Period 2 begins after it. On the longest days Period 3
absorbs the drift. `[NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]`

### 8.2 Day session (Thu / Fri) — 360 gross, 300 usable (Scenario C)

| From | To | Block | Min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | 09:10 | الطابور — assembly | 10 *(protected)* |
| 09:10 | 10:10 | **ساعة القرآن** | 60 |
| 10:10 | 10:50 | Period 1 | 40 |
| 10:50 | 11:30 | Period 2 | 40 |
| 11:30 | 12:00 | راحة — rest & meal | 30 *(protected)* |
| 12:00 | 12:40 | Period 3 | 40 |
| 12:40 | 13:00 | **صلاة الظهر** | 20 *(protected)* |
| 13:00 | 13:40 | Period 4 | 40 |
| 13:40 | 14:20 | Period 5 | 40 |
| 14:20 | 15:00 | Period 6 | 40 |
| | | **Gross** | **360** |
| | | **Usable** | **60 + 240 = 300** ✓ |

**Why 40 minutes here and 50 in the evening.** Six slots at 50 would need 300
minutes of subject time, which does not exist. 40 is the recovered lesson
length and matches the corpus's own accounting. The morning child sustains six
short slots better than the evening child sustains three long ones — the
shorter period is the *concession to the number of slots*, not to attention.

**Friday.** This grid assigns Friday a 20-minute Ẓuhr. If collision 1.1(a) is
ruled the other way, Friday loses a further 60–70 minutes and moves to
Scenario D. **The Friday grid above is provisional on that ruling.**
