## 1. The method in one line

> **A subject's instructional form decides how it is assessed. Nothing is decided
> subject by subject, and nothing is decided twice.**

Three rules generate everything below:

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| **1** | Every subject sits in **exactly one** of the three programmes, at every grade. |
| **2** | Every subject holds **exactly one instructional form** at each grade — ● I E R U s —. |
| **3** | The form determines the assessment consequence **mechanically**. No separate assessment decision is ever taken. |

---

## 2. Form → consequence

| Form | Own paper? | Own mark line? | Enters the average? | Reported in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **● مستقل** | yes | yes | **yes** | **A** |
| **● مستقل** *(unpapered — continuous only)* | no | yes | **yes** | **A** |
| **I مدمج** | **shared**, named sections | one **joint** line + sub-marks | **yes** (combined) | **A** |
| **E‑ع مضمّن مُعلَّم** | no — a named section inside the host | sub-mark under the host | no (inside host) | **A** |
| **E‑س مضمّن مستتر** | no | no | no (inside host) | **A** (host only) |
| **U وحدة** | no — a terminal task | no | **no** | **B** |
| **U وحدة** *(declared a paper domain)* | inside the host paper | no | inside host | **A** |
| **R دوراني** | **never** | no | **no** | **B** |
| **s strand** | **never** | **never** | **no** | **annexe to A** |
| **شرط condition** | **never a contributory mark** | no | **no** | **C** |

**Two guards built into the rule:**
- **A merged pair must read `I` on both rows, or the merge is void.** An asymmetric pair (`I / ●`) is a *host and guest*, not a merge — the exact defect found at G9, now caught by the policy instead of by a reviewer.
- **A section floor of 40%** applies inside any merged or E‑ع paper, so a candidate cannot pass by carrying one science and abandoning the other.

## 10. Conditions — and why they carry no mark

A **threshold** is operationally distinct from a **mark**:

| | Mark | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Averaged? | yes | **never** |
| Compensable? | yes, within limits | **never** |
| Is surplus worth anything? | yes | **no** |

**خدمة التتويج is verified, not graded.** The recovered text says «شرط استلام
شهادة التخرج **لا زينة فيها**». A mark creates a pass mark; a pass mark creates
the possibility of "passing" at 52% while the condition — an *executed,
impact-documented* project — was never met. **A marked service project is the
«زينة» the constitution forbids.**
