### 1 · P2-01-h — differentiation of the sharʿī sciences before G6

**Question.** Does SHRS name the sharʿī sciences as separate disciplines before
G6, or hold them integrated?

**Recommended: H3 — graduated naming.** Sciences exist as named strands *inside*
التربية الإسلامية at G3–G6, becoming subjects at grade 7.

> **APPROVE / REJECT:** *The sharʿī sciences are carried as internally named,
> internally sequenced strands within a single التربية الإسلامية subject from
> G1 to G6, reported as one mark, and become separate timetable subjects at
> grade 7. ACD-007 §3.2's principle «الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن يكون علومًا
> مسماة» is retained and governs what the child is told he is studying.*

*If instead H2 (early naming) is approved, the Council must additionally minute
(i) the withdrawal of that principle and (ii) the discarding of Part 1's
prescribed remedy for the grade-7 jump.*

### 2 · P2-01-a — قواعد اللغة الوظيفية

**Question.** Restore it, embed it as a strand, or retire it?

**Recommended: A1 — restore as a distinct subject at grades 4–6.**

> **APPROVE / REJECT:** *قواعد اللغة الوظيفية is restored as a distinct subject
> at grades 4–6, carrying the principle «القاعدة تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في
> المتن», standing behind بوابة الجسر at grade 6, and النحو's entry returns
> toward grade 7.*

*Coupling: under H2 this recommendation weakens and A3 (retire, explicitly)
becomes coherent. Do not vote **a** before **h**.*

### 3 · P2-01-d — خدمة التتويج

**Question.** Subject, certification requirement, or both?

**Recommended: D3 — dual.** A supervised G12 programme that is simultaneously
the certificate condition.

> **APPROVE / REJECT (i):** *خدمة التتويج is a supervised G12 programme with
> named instructional supervision, and is simultaneously competency 10 and a
> condition of شهادة التخرج الملكية per ACD-007 Part 7. It is not a markable
> subject whose pass substitutes for the condition.*

> **APPROVE / REJECT (ii):** *البحث is reinstated in the identity of subject 14,
> which reverts to البحث والخطابة at G10–G12 and owns the refereed 1,500-word
> paper — an outcome distinct from the service project.*

---

### 4 · P2-01-i — the G12 architecture

**Question.** Reduced terminal year, full teaching year, or certification year?

**Recommended: C — instruction culminates at G11; G12 certifies.** It is the
only option that survives all six tests: Part 1's «الثاني عشر خفيف», §4.4's 546
hours, the 19-حصة ceiling, the grade-11 Qur'anic gate, the two-gate certificate
structure, and WAEC as a certificate condition.

> **APPROVE / REJECT:** *Instructional curriculum culminates at grade 11, closing
> at الثانوية القرآنية. Grade 12 is a declared capstone year — terminal
> certification, WAEC, the refereed research paper and oratory, the service
> project, Qur'anic maintenance of the secured fifteen ajzāʾ, and مناقشة التخرج
> before مجلس المناقشة — and is to be published as such, not as a year in which
> teaching stopped.*

> **CONSEQUENTIAL DIRECTION (required with A or C):** *Grade 11 is to be costed
> before the matrix is rebuilt.* Both options move load onto grade 11, which
> already carries 19 direct claimants — the heaviest in the ladder.

### 5 · P2-01-k — NEW ITEM TABLED

**Question.** The theological and madhhab architecture.

ACD-007 states a creed **posture** — «وسطية أهل السنة بأدلتها بلا غلو ولا
تمييع» — and names **no school of creed and no madhhab**; مذهب، مالكي and مالك
do not occur in the document. A creed booklist was directed in an earlier
sitting; the **fiqh madhhab has never been ruled.**

**No recommendation is offered.** This is a doctrinal decision for the Council
and must not be inferred from the classical texts appearing in drafts.

> **TABLED FOR RULING:** *The institution's madhhab in fiqh, and the relationship
> between the stated creed posture and the creed booklist already directed.*
