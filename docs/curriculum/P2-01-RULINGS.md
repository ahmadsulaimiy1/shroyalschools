# 1 · P2-01-h — EARLY DIFFERENTIATION OF THE SHARʿĪ SCIENCES

> **Which remedy should SHRS adopt for the already-diagnosed G7 transition
> problem?**
>
> `[COUNCIL REFRAME]` The question is no longer whether named subjects begin
> early or at G7 in the abstract. ACD-007 Part 1 diagnosed the transition defect
> and prescribed a remedy. The three architectures below are **three remedies to
> one recorded problem**, and are to be evaluated against the recovered evidence
> rather than against general educational preference.

## 1.1 Evidence

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 §3.2, سجل المواد:

| Subject | Range | Stated reason |
|---|---|---|
| التربية الإسلامية | **ر–٦** | «**الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن يكون علومًا مسماة**» |
| العقيدة | ٧–١١ | «وسطية أهل السنة بأدلتها بلا غلو ولا تمييع» |
| الفقه وأصوله | ٧–١١ | «العبادة الصحيحة أولًا، ثم آلة فهم الأحكام» |
| الحديث ومصطلحه | ٧–١١ | «ثاني الوحيين — حفظًا وفهمًا ثم آلة نقد علمي» |
| السيرة والتاريخ | ٧–١١ | «القدوة والذاكرة» |
| التفسير | ٧–١١ | «فهم الكتاب على منهج أهله لا على الهوى» |

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 Part 1, التدقيق الشامل — الانتقالات:

> الحكم: **انتقالان حرجان** · البينة: «جسر السادس محكم؛ **قفزة السابع (١١ مادة
> دفعة) غير ممهدة**» · موضع الإصلاح: «**تمهيد الفصل الأول من السابع**»

**This is the decisive line.** ACD-007 confirms that **eleven named subjects
enter at once at grade 7** — the sciences are demonstrably not named before it.
And ACD-007 **already identified the resulting jump as a defect**, then chose a
remedy: bridge it inside term 1 of grade 7. It did **not** choose to move
subjects downward.

`[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` The 26-subject universe names, at G1: التوحيد،
الفقه، التفسير، السيرة النبوية، التربية الإسلامية — five; by G3, six.

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` The two architectures are **two remedies to the same
diagnosed problem.** The 26-subject universe is not ignorant of the G7 jump; it
dissolves the jump by distributing entry across G1–G8. ACD-007 preserves the
jump and bridges it. Neither is naive.

`[COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]` Which remedy the institution adopts.

## 1.2 A separate axis that must not be folded into this ruling

`[RECOVERED]` The corpus states a creed **posture** — «وسطية أهل السنة بأدلتها
بلا غلو ولا تمييع» — and names **no school of creed and no madhhab**. A search
of ACD-007 returns **no occurrence** of مذهب، مالكي، or مالك.

`[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` Najdi creed books were selected in an earlier
sitting.

`[UNVERIFIED]` The fiqh madhhab of the institution. It is **nowhere in the
corpus.** It must not be read off the classical texts appearing in drafts —
those texts were proposed by subject work, not ruled by the Council, and
inferring doctrine from a draft booklist would let the drafting tail wag the
constitutional dog.

**This axis is orthogonal to P2-01-h.** Early differentiation can be adopted
under any madhhab; a madhhab can be declared under either differentiation
model. `[BLACK]` — separate ruling, listed as **P2-01-k**.

## 1.3 Options

| | **H1 — retain late naming, bridge at G7** | **H2 — dissolve the transition downward** | **H3 — graduated naming + explicit bridge** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | one التربية الإسلامية to G6; the eleven-subject transition **bridged within Term 1 of G7** exactly as Part 1 prescribes | named subjects move progressively downward through G1–G8 until no transition remains | التربية الإسلامية remains the **child-facing umbrella** G1–G6 while its **internal strands are developed and sequenced G3–G6**; formal differentiation at G7; **the Term-1 G7 bridge is also implemented** |
| Basis | `[RECOVERED]` §3.2 + the Part 1 remedy, applied as written | `[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` | `[NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]` built on both |
| The G7 jump | preserved and bridged | dissolved | **reduced *and* bridged** |
| Part 1's remedy | implemented | discarded | implemented |

`[COUNCIL REFRAME]` H3 as tabled by the Council is stronger than the form
first drafted: it **implements the Part 1 bridge as well as** reducing the jump.
The earlier draft left the bridge implicit, which would have discarded a
prescribed remedy while claiming to honour it. The correction is adopted into
the option and noted here rather than absorbed silently.

## 1.4 Consequences

| Dimension | H1 | H2 | H3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educational** | protects «الدين محبةً وعادةً» — religion met as practice before taxonomy | a 6-year-old meets five named sciences; taxonomy precedes affection | practice-first, with the teacher naming the strand while the child meets one subject |
| **Scholarly** | matches the classical madrasa order — عبادة and أدب before مصطلحات | earlier technical vocabulary; earlier متون entry | preserves the order, adds teacher-facing structure |
| **Curricular** | ~6 of the 26 collapse into one row for G1–G6 | matrix stands | matrix rows survive as strand records, not timetable rows |
| **Assessment** | one mark for religion to G6; simpler and less informative | six separate marks at G3 — granular, and granular about a six-year-old's creed | one reported mark, strand-level evidence retained |
| **Timetable** *(deferred)* | frees primary capacity | the G1 and G8 pressure already found | intermediate |
| **Staffing** | one primary religion teacher per class | five specialists, or one teacher presenting five subjects — which is differentiation in name only | one teacher, structured guide |
| **Certification** | G1–G6 reports carry one religion line | six lines from G3 | one line, strand annexe |
| **Downstream** | jump bridged as prescribed; Part 1's unfinished work completed | G7 jump gone; G8 pressure instead (Phase 2 §12: +6 claimants) | jump reduced **and** bridged; G8 unrelieved |

## 1.5 Contradictions — preserved, not resolved

1. §3.2's «ر–٦» against five named sciences at G1. **Direct.**
2. §3.2's stated reason «قبل أن يكون علومًا مسماة» is not a range but a
   *rationale*; H2 does not merely extend a range, it **withdraws a stated
   principle**. That must be minuted as a withdrawal, not a revision.
3. Part 1 diagnosed the G7 jump and prescribed bridging. H2 **discards a
   prescribed remedy without recording that it did so.**

## 1.6 Recommendation — `[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]`

**H3**, in the form tabled by the Council — graduated strands **plus** the Term-1
G7 bridge. It is the only option that keeps ACD-007's pedagogical principle
intact, implements the remedy Part 1 actually prescribed, and captures what H2
was reaching for. «الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن
يكون علومًا مسماة» constrains **what the child is told he is studying** — it
does not forbid the institution from knowing, sequencing and assessing strands
internally. H1 leaves a defect ACD-007 itself flagged; H2 pays for the fix with
the corpus's most explicit pedagogical commitment and with six assessment lines
on a six-year-old.

H3's cost is real and should not be hidden: **it is a new engineering decision,
found in neither document**, and it demands a teacher's guide that most primary
staff will not have.

> ### COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED — P2-01-h
> Adopt **H1**, **H2** or **H3** as the constitutional position on
> differentiation of the sharʿī sciences before G6. If H2, the Council must
> additionally minute the **withdrawal** of «الدين محبةً وعادةً قبل أن يكون
> علومًا مسماة» and the **discarding** of the Part 1 G7-bridging remedy.

# 2 · P2-01-a — قواعد اللغة الوظيفية

## 2.1 Evidence

`[RECOVERED]` §3.2: **قواعد اللغة الوظيفية (٤–٦)** · «**القاعدة تُلمس في النص
قبل أن تُسمى في المتن**» · outcome «وعي تركيبي عملي **يهيّئ لمتون الإعدادية**».

`[RECOVERED]` §2.1 places grade ٦ as **معهد اللغة**, «الجسر: من عربية الطفولة
إلى عربية العلم», gated by بوابة الجسر بلجنة ثلاثية.

`[RECOVERED]` Part 1, التدقيق الشامل — علوم العربية العليا: «النحو والصرف كانا
**مطويين في «العربية»**» → remedy «**كرسيان مستقلان بمتونهما**». A recovered
precedent for splitting a strand out of Arabic — **done deliberately and
minuted.**

**The circular argument is set aside as directed.** That النحو currently sits
at G5 is a *consequence* of this subject's absence, not evidence against its
necessity.

## 2.2 What the subject actually is

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` Read with §2.1, قواعد اللغة الوظيفية is not junior
النحو. It is **the instructional content of the bridge** — the three years in
which grammar is *encountered in text* so that متون can later be *entered as
matn*. Its outcome names its own successor: «يهيّئ لمتون الإعدادية». A subject
whose stated purpose is to prepare for متون cannot be discharged by a subject
that is already teaching متون.

## 2.3 Options

| | **A1 — restore as a distinct subject ٤–٦** | **A2 — named strand inside اللغة العربية** | **A3 — retire by explicit decision** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status | `[RECOVERED]` restored | `[NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]` | `[NEW ENGINEERING DECISION]` |
| Principle «تُلمس قبل أن تُسمى» | preserved | preserved if the strand is inductive | **withdrawn** |
| النحو entry | returns toward ٧ | returns toward ٧ | stays at G5–G6 |

## 2.4 Consequences

| Dimension | A1 | A2 | A3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educational** | inductive grammar has a guaranteed home | same content, no separate identity — risks being displaced by whatever else اللغة العربية hosts | grammar is named from G5; the recovered principle ends |
| **Scholarly** | matches the classical order: استعمال then تقعيد then متن | acceptable | defensible only if the Council holds that early formal grammar suits this cohort |
| **Curricular** | 27th subject; ٤–٦ only | no new row | no change |
| **Assessment** | its own gate feeding بوابة الجسر at ٦ | assessed within Arabic | النحو assessed from G5 |
| **Timetable** *(deferred)* | +1 claimant at G4–G6, offset by النحو leaving G5–G6 | neutral | current state |
| **Staffing** | a primary teacher can carry it — that is the point | same | G5 needs a النحو teacher |
| **Certification** | a line on the primary certificate | none | none |
| **Downstream** | **restores the prerequisite ACD-007 built for النحو**; بوابة الجسر becomes meaningful | partially restores it | بوابة الجسر at ٦ has no distinct subject behind it |

## 2.5 Contradictions

1. Its absence is unminuted — no document records a decision to remove it.
2. **Part 1's precedent cuts against A2**: when the institution last found
   grammar folded inside «العربية», its own judgement was that it should be
   **pulled out**, not left embedded. A2 re-folds what Part 1 unfolded.
3. A3 requires withdrawing «القاعدة تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن»,
   which is a pedagogical doctrine, not a scheduling note.

## 2.6 Recommendation — `[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]`

**A1.** The subject is load-bearing: it is the content of the bridge stage, its
outcome names متون الإعدادية as its successor, and بوابة الجسر at grade ٦ has
nothing else behind it. A2 is the option Part 1 explicitly diagnosed as a
defect. A3 is available but must be taken knowingly.

**A1 is coupled to P2-01-h.** Under H1 or H3 it is close to mandatory. Under
H2 — where naming early is the settled policy — A3 becomes coherent, because
H2 has already withdrawn the «تُلمس قبل أن تُسمى» family of reasoning.

> ### COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED — P2-01-a
> Adopt **A1**, **A2** or **A3**. If A3, minute the withdrawal of «القاعدة
> تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن» and state what stands behind بوابة
> الجسر at grade ٦.

# 3 · P2-01-d — خدمة التتويج

## 3.1 Evidence — this ruling is settled by recovered text, not inference

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 Part 7, الكفايات العشر — competency **10**:

> **الخدمة والأمانة** — «مشروع خدمة منفذ موثق الأثر وسجل سلوك بتقدير جدارة فأعلى»

> «هذه الكفايات ليست ديباجة: كل واحدة موصولة في كتب الكفايات بمصفوفة (أين تُبنى
> صفًّا صفًّا ← وأين تُقاس أداةً أداة) — **والعاشرة شرط استلام شهادة التخرج لا
> زينة فيها**.»

`[RECOVERED]` The certificate ladder:

| Certificate | When | Requirement | Awarded by |
|---|---|---|---|
| الشهادة الإعدادية | نهاية التاسع | المتوسط العام + بوابة القرآن ٨/١٠ | ممتحن خارجي |
| **الثانوية القرآنية** | **نهاية الحادي عشر** | **خمسة عشر جزءًا راسخة بتجويد مُجاز** | لجنة الامتحانات القرآنية |
| **شهادة التخرج الملكية** | **نهاية الثاني عشر** | **الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج + إتمام WAEC** | مجلس المناقشة برئاسة الإدارة |

**خدمة التتويج is competency 10, and competency 10 is a condition of receiving
the graduation certificate.** This is `[RECOVERED]`, not the professional
inference offered in the previous sitting. That earlier inclination is
withdrawn and replaced by the text.

## 3.2 The tension inside ACD-007 — preserved, not resolved

خدمة التتويج appears **both** in §3.2 سجل المواد — the *subject* register — and
in Part 7 as a certificate condition. ACD-007 is not inconsistent here so much
as **dual**: a supervised service project consumes instructional supervision
time (hence a register entry) while functioning as certification (hence Part 7).

**Both readings are preserved.** The Council decides which one governs
identity; the other becomes a note.

## 3.3 Options

| | **D1 — certification requirement only** | **D2 — instructional subject only** | **D3 — dual: a G12 programme that is also the certificate condition** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lives in | graduation architecture | the 26-subject universe | both, explicitly |
| Supervision time | must still be found, unnamed | named | named |
| Risk | supervision has no owner — the failure Phase 4 exists to catch | a graduation *condition* treated as a markable subject; a pupil could "pass" it and still fail the condition | requires the teaching/assessment identity doctrine the project has named but never applied |

## 3.4 The 1,500-word refereed paper

`[RECOVERED]` §3.2, البحث والخطابة (١٠–١٢): «**بحث محكم ١٥٠٠ كلمة** وخطبة عشر
دقائق ومناظرة بأدب الخلاف».

`[RECOVERED]` Part 1: الثاني عشر خفيف — remedy «برمجة خدمة التتويج **والتحرير
الامتحاني** (الجزء الثاني) خارج WAEC».

**The paper belongs to البحث والخطابة, whose range already includes ١٢.** It is
not homeless — it was orphaned when the matrix renamed that subject
**الإنشاء والخطابة** and dropped البحث from its identity. `[RECOVERED]`

**Recommended placement:** restore البحث as a named component of **subject 14**
at G10–G12, distinct from خدمة التتويج. **No 27th subject is created.** The
matrix's **الإنشاء والخطابة** is flagged as a **naming and content defect in an
existing row** — the subject was renamed and البحث dropped from its identity —
and is corrected in place at the matrix rebuild. The paper is the *research* outcome; the
service project is the *service* outcome. Part 7 requires both, separately.

## 3.5 Recommendation — `[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]`

**D3.** D1 loses the supervision time and reproduces the unhosted-demand
failure. D2 mis-types a certificate condition as a markable subject. D3 is the
only option consistent with both places ACD-007 puts it — and it is the first
concrete case where the teaching/assessment distinction must actually be
operationalised rather than merely asserted.

> ### COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED — P2-01-d

> **(ii)** Confirm that the **1,500-word refereed paper** is restored to

# 4 · P2-01-i — THE G12 ARCHITECTURE

## 4.1 Evidence — including the line that decides most of it

`[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 Part 1, التدقيق الشامل — **السنوات الخفيفة**:

> الحكم: «**الثاني عشر خفيف**» · البينة: «بين التدقيق العشري و WAEC **فراغ غير
> مبرمج**» · موضع الإصلاح: «**برمجة خدمة التتويج والتحرير الامتحاني (الجزء
> الثاني) خارج WAEC**»

**ACD-007 diagnoses G12 as a light year with an unprogrammed gap, and programmes
that gap with service and examination writing — not with subjects.** This is the
institution's own recorded judgement about its twelfth year.

`[RECOVERED]` §4.4: سنة التتويج = **546 hours** against 754 for grades ١–١١ — a
reduction of 208 hours, consistent with the Part 1 diagnosis.
`[RECOVERED]` §2.1: gates are **الثانوية القرآنية (١١)** then **شهادة التخرج (١٢)**.
`[RECOVERED]` §3.1: G12 is the one year the system duplicates morning subjects,
for intensive WAEC revision.
`[RECOVERED]` Part 7: شهادة التخرج = الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج + إتمام WAEC.
`[RECOVERED]` §3.2: only **البحث والخطابة (١٠–١٢)** and **خدمة التتويج (١٢)**
carry ١٢ in their ranges.
`[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` The matrix: 24 live at G12, 13 independent.

## 4.2 Testing Option C against the corpus, as directed

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| **Recovered corpus** | Part 1 («الثاني عشر خفيف») and §4.4 (546 h) both describe a reduced terminal year. C fits; B contradicts both. |
| **The 19-حصة ceiling** | §3.2 fixes the distribution of 19 حصص «صفًّا صفًّا». A G12 of 13 independents cannot sit inside 19 alongside Qur'an, WAEC, research and a service project. **C is the only option that does not require the ceiling to be broken or superseded.** |
| **The Qur'anic gate** | الثانوية القرآنية closes at **١١**. Under C, G12 Qur'an work is **maintenance of a secured 15 ajzāʾ**, not acquisition — which matches «التدقيق العشري» appearing in the G12 diagnosis. C fits precisely. |
| **Certification structure** | Two gates, two years: ١١ Qur'anic, ١٢ graduation. C uses both as written. A merges them loosely; B leaves ١٢'s gate competing with 13 subjects. |
| **National examination** | WAEC sits in G12 by §3.1 and is a **condition** of the certificate by Part 7. Under B, WAEC revision competes with 13 independents. Under C it is a programmed component. |
| **The 12-grade architecture** | **C's weakest point.** If instruction ends at ١١, the twelve-grade ladder is instructionally eleven. Externally this may read as an eleven-year curriculum with an administrative year appended. |

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` The last row is the one real objection to C, and it
is a presentational objection, not a structural one: a capstone year of
research, oratory, service, national examination and defence is a **normal and
respected final-year structure**, provided it is *declared* as such rather than
appearing to be a year in which teaching quietly stopped.

## 4.3 Consequences

| Dimension | **A — reduced terminal year** | **B — full teaching year** | **C — instruction culminates G11; G12 certifies** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educational** | closure and consolidation | a twelfth year of new matter on top of WAEC | mastery demonstrated rather than extended |
| **Scholarly** | متون complete at ١١ | متون extend by a year | متون complete at ١١, defended at ١٢ |
| **Curricular** | 13 G12 independents complete at G11 — **not deleted** | matrix stands | same as A, plus terminal assessment at ١٢ |
| **Assessment** | thin at G12 | heavy and competing | **G12 becomes the assessment year** — the clearest use of the ladder |
| **Timetable** *(deferred)* | 546 h fits | 546 h cannot hold it | 546 h fits |
| **Staffing** | G12 needs supervisors, not subject teachers | G12 needs ~13 subject teachers | supervisors, examiners, a WAEC tutor |
| **Certification** | both gates usable | ١٢'s gate competes with instruction | **both gates used exactly as recovered** |
| **Downstream** | Phase 2's five G12 demotions explained | they remain anomalies | explained; and G11 becomes the heaviest year — **a new pressure, and it must be costed, not assumed away** |

## 4.4 Contradictions

1. §3.2 lists خدمة التتويج under سجل المواد while Part 7 makes it a certificate
   condition (see §3.2 above). **Preserved.**
2. B contradicts §4.4's 546 hours and Part 1's «الثاني عشر خفيف» simultaneously.
   Adopting B requires minuting **both** as superseded.
3. A and C both leave the matrix's 13 G12 independents to be re-terminated at
   G11 — which **relocates the G12 problem to G11**, where Phase 2 already
   counted 19 direct claimants, the highest in the ladder. Neither option
   resolves that; it must be taken up at the matrix rebuild.

## 4.5 Recommendation — `[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]`

**C**, and not for elegance: it is the only option that survives all six tests
in §4.2. Part 1 and §4.4 independently describe a light twelfth year; the two
gates are dated ١١ and ١٢; WAEC and the ten competencies are certificate
conditions, not subjects; and C alone leaves the 19-حصة ceiling unbroken.

**The objection stands and must be answered in the adoption, not after it:**
C must be declared as a capstone year — research, oratory, service, national
examination, Qur'anic maintenance, defence — and not permitted to read as a
year in which teaching stopped. And **G11 must be costed before C is
implemented**, because C pushes load onto the grade that is already heaviest.

> ### COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED — P2-01-i
> Adopt **A**, **B** or **C** as the G12 architecture. If B, minute §4.4's 546
> hours and Part 1's «الثاني عشر خفيف» as superseded. If A or C, direct that
> G11 be costed before the matrix rebuild.
