### Withdrawn: the monotonic-status rule

A rule was applied in v2 that **no subject may be demoted once it has risen**.
**That rule is withdrawn.** It was an over-application of the rising-load
direction and it flattened every lifecycle into a straight line.

The rising-load direction governs **programme load per grade**, not the
trajectory of any individual subject. A subject may enter late, taper, drop
out entirely, or appear as a bounded unit in only one or two grades of the
whole twelve. Three subjects in this matrix do exactly that:

| Subject | Lives at | Span |

| **خدمة التتويج** | G12 only | **1 grade** |
