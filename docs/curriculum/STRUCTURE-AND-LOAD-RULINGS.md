## 1. The مراجعة correction, which re-prices everything

The recovered 100 weekly minutes decompose as Mon–Wed `مراجعة ووضوء 10` +
`مراجعة وانصراف 10` (= 60) and Sat–Sun `شريط المراجعة 20` (= 40).

**The 60 evening minutes are struck from the instructional ledger.** A ten-minute
block bracketed by ablution on one side and departure on the other cannot be
taught and cannot be assessed. It was never instruction.

**The 40 weekend minutes are assigned to حفظ القرآن الكريم** as
**مراجعة المنزل المسندة** — delivered by the Qur'an teacher, marked weekly on the
**سجل المنزل**, feeding بوابة القرآن. They now have an owner, a mark and a gate.

|---|---|---|
| Instructional week, G7–G11 | 19 h 20 m | **18 h 20 m** |

## 2. Q1 — the Qur'anic target: **twelve juz متقنة, fifteen as امتياز**

Unit cost from the recovered envelope: 2,535 h ÷ 15 juz = **169 h per juz**.

Three routes were refused with reasons: restoring the روضة puts a four-year-old
into the week Q2 exists to dismantle; moving the gate to G12 yields only 13.85
and destroys the two-gate structure; raising the hour to fund 15 costs one حصة
every evening — **the curriculum paying for the certificate.**

Instead the hour is funded properly inside the revised week:

| Band | Qur'an min/week | Years | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1–G6 | 3×60 + Sat 90 + مراجعة 20 = **290** | 6 | 1,131 h |
| G7–G11 | 5×60 + مراجعة 40 = **340** | 5 | 1,105 h |
| | | | **2,236 h = 88.2%** |

**2,236 h funds 13.23 juz against a 2,028 h requirement for twelve — 10.3%
headroom**, which absorbs absence, illness and slow starters. That is a **6.4%
real improvement** on the previous position, bought without adding a year and
without touching the 40-minute حصة.

> **شهادة الثانوية القرآنية** — «… قد أتقن **اثني عشر جزءًا من القرآن الكريم
> إتقانًا راسخًا، بتجويدٍ مُجازٍ بلجنة الامتحانات القرآنية**…»
>
> **سطر الامتياز** — «**مع مرتبة الامتياز في الحفظ** — خمسة عشر جزءًا فأكثر
> راسخة بالتجويد المُجاز.»

The recovered phrase «خمسة عشر جزءًا راسخة بتجويد مُجاز بلجنة» is **preserved
verbatim.** It moves from the gate line to the امتياز line — re-sited to where
it is funded, not deleted.

## 3. Q2 — the child's week: a meal, a buffer, and a free day

**The meal and the transfer buffer cost nothing.** Old non-teaching time was
`assembly 10 + مراجعة ووضوء 10 + مراجعة وانصراف 10 = 30`. New non-teaching time
is `استقبال وغداء 20 + وضوء 10 = 30`. **An exact swap.**

### Evening session, all grades, Mon–Wed 14:00–18:00

| | Block | Min |
|---|---|---|
| 14:00–14:20 | **استقبال وغداء خفيف** *(non-instructional)* | 20 |
| 14:20–15:20 | **الساعة القرآنية** | 60 |
| 15:20–16:00 | حصة ١ | 40 |
| 16:00–16:10 | الوضوء | 10 |
| 16:10–16:40 | **صلاة العصر** | 30 |
| 16:40–17:20 | حصة ٢ | 40 |
| 17:20–18:00 | حصة ٣ | 40 |
| | **Σ** | **240** ✓ |

**The buffer is bought by making the first 20 minutes non-instructional.** A
child released at 13:45 and arriving at 14:25 has missed nothing. This survives
traffic and an overrunning morning school, and — unlike a later start — it does
not push dismissal toward maghrib (earliest Nigerian maghrib ≈ 18:25). The
ten-minute طابور is abolished: ceremony three times a week, in a school that
already assembles at the weekend, is time the child cannot afford.

### **Sunday is deleted at G1–G6**, and the Qur'anic cost is recovered the same day

The Saturday hour extends 60 → **90 minutes** (الحلقة القرآنية الممتدة), funded
by dropping Saturday from 5 حصص to 4. **Qur'an at G1–G6 therefore falls by 50
minutes a week, not 340.**

| | Mon–Wed | Sat | Sun | **حصص/week** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1–G6** | 9 | 4 | — | **13** |
| **G7–G11** | 9 | 5 | 5 | **19** |
| **G12** | Wed only 3 | 5 | 5 | **13** |

### The six-year-old, before and after

|---|---|---|

The Board's own words: *48 hours is still high. It is what a supplementary

**G7–G12 keep Sunday.** A six-year-old at 54 hours is a safeguarding matter; a
fifteen-year-old eleven months from a Qur'anic gate is not. The recovered
19-حصة ledger survives intact where it matters.

## 4. Q3 — G12: the Board overrules the rising-load direction, at G12 only

**To the Director General, in the Board's own terms:** the ruling that load
rises with the grade is **upheld from G1 to G11** — it rises **4 → 12 taught
blocks, monotonically, without exception.** It is overruled **at the twelfth
grade alone**, because the institution's own record contradicts it in four
independent places:

1. **§4.4** prices سنة التتويج at **546 hours against 754.**
2. **Part 1** diagnoses «**الثاني عشر خفيف**» and prescribes «برمجة خدمة التتويج
   والتحرير الامتحاني خارج WAEC» — *programme the gap with service and
   examination writing, not with subjects.*
3. **§3.2** ranges stop at **١١**, with two exceptions carrying ١٢.
4. **خدمة التتويج exists as a G12-only subject.**

> The direction was given without these four in front of you. Applied to G12 it
> does not produce a heavier year; it produces **25 names of which 12 hold no
> minutes.** That is not rising load. It is a longer list.

**Adopted: instruction culminates at G11; G12 certifies.** Monday and Tuesday
evenings are **مُفرَّغة** — released and logged, not timetabled: WAEC revision,
service fieldwork, research supervision.

| # | G12 subject | حصص/week |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WAEC** — IRS · العربية · تقنية الامتحان | 3 |
| 2 | **خدمة التتويج** | 2 |

Plus **حفظ القرآن وتدقيقه** — 3 × 60 + 40 = 220 min («التدقيق العشري»).

```
520 taught + 180 Qur'an + 40 مراجعة = 740 min/wk × 39 = 481 h
```

**This reconstructs §4.4's figure to the hour**, and programmes the «فراغ غير

**Nothing is deleted.** Seventeen subjects **terminate at G11**, marked

**G11 was costed before adoption, as required:** 12 taught blocks in 19 حصص plus

## 5. Q4 — WAEC: three slots, and the condition is downgraded

**3 حصص/week at G12 = 78 hours a year** — the single sanctioned exception to
«لا تكرر المنظومة مواد الصباح».

| Slot | Paper | Why here and not the morning school |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Islamic Studies** | SHRS's own curriculum *is* the syllabus, and exceeds it |
| 2 | **Arabic Language** | no conventional school in the catchment teaches it |
| 3 | **تقنية الامتحان** | rubric, timing, command words, past-paper drill **in English** — transferable across all nine papers |

**Mathematics, English Language and the sciences are not taught at SHRS at any
grade, including G12.** That boundary is written into the parent contract in
terms, so no family believes SHRS prepares the full WAEC.

> ~~الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج + **إتمام WAEC**~~
> **الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج + إثبات الجلوس لامتحان WAEC**

**Proof of sitting, not of passing.** The school may not make its own award
hostage to an examination it neither teaches in full nor enters candidates for —
that hands a third party a veto over a SHRS certificate and is unenforceable
against a pupil whose conventional school fails to enter him. The result is
recorded on the transcript; it is not a gate.

**Sunset:** SHRS registers as a WAEC centre within **three admission cycles**,
or the condition is struck from the certificate entirely.

## 6. Q5 — the fortnightly cycle is abolished · **لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة**

| Band | Contact floor |
|---|---|
| G1–G3 | **3/week** |
| G4–G9 | **2/week** |
| G10–G12 | **1/week** |

**Every grade returns to a weekly cycle.** The fortnight was introduced to make
25 subjects fit; it produced subjects at 19 lessons a year and rotational-slot
subjects at **3.8 lessons a year**. *A subject a pupil meets 3.8 times in a year
is not taught; it is mentioned.* **No subject in this school is taught less than
once a week.** With the block counts below a weekly cycle fits without touching
the 40-minute حصة — the problem the fortnight solved no longer exists, because
the problem was the subject count.

**مضمّن is abolished as a load-bearing code.** Every E cell resolves one of two
ways and there is no third: a **وحدة** — named, separately examined, inside a
host that *does* hold minutes, reported as an indented component line — or
**deleted**. R and the دورة slot are abolished outright. `s` survives only inside
التربية الإسلامية at G1–G6.

**No subject appears on a timetable, report card, certificate or headline count
unless it holds timetabled minutes in that grade.**

| Absorbed as a وحدة | Into | Grades |
|---|---|---|
| التوحيد | العقيدة | G7–G12 |
| السيرة + التاريخ → **التاريخ والسيرة** | one block — RR-01 finally executed | G6–G11 |

| فقه اللغة والمعاجم | الصرف | G8 |
| **التجويد** | **الساعة القرآنية**, where it was already funded — holding a حصة too was double-counting | all |

**Subject universe: 28 → 24 named subjects.** Two new (التحرير الامتحاني, WAEC).
