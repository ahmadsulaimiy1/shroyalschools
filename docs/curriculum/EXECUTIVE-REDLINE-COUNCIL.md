## 1. Mandate

No curriculum allocation, timetable solution, subject cut, merge, deferral or
progression change may be adopted **merely because it makes one ledger
balance**.

> **A mathematical solution is not automatically a curriculum solution.**

The objective is an architecture that survives hostile scholarly, pedagogical,
operational, mathematical and editorial examination — not the largest volume of
curriculum text.

---

## 3. Procedure

> PROPOSAL → INDEPENDENT REDLINE → CONTRADICTORY POSITIONS → EVIDENCE →
> OPTIONS → CONSEQUENCES → EXECUTIVE COUNCIL DECISION → RESIDUAL RISKS → STATUS

**Preservation rule.** Where a contradiction is found, both sides are preserved
and the contradiction named. Nothing is silently reconciled, averaged, rounded,
merged, deferred or deleted.

**Ignorance rule.** The Council states explicitly when it does not know. An
unresolved question is preferable to a coherent-looking answer assembled from
unsupported assumptions.

**Production bar.** No detailed textbook production proceeds while a
constitutional architecture issue stands at RED or BLACK.

| `[UNVERIFIED]` | Asserted somewhere, source not yet located |
| `[RED — DOCUMENT RECONCILIATION REQUIRED]` | Two sourced figures conflict |
| `[BLACK — EXECUTIVE COUNCIL DECISION REQUIRED]` | Architecture cannot proceed until ruled |

**A RED is only issued where two *sourced* figures conflict.** A conflict
between a sourced figure and a working error is not a RED; it is a correction,
and it is recorded as one. Manufacturing a RED over a resolved item would
itself breach the preservation rule, by preserving a side that no document holds.

## 6. Whole-period rule

Analytical calculation may produce 37.5, 38, 42.5 or 47 minutes. **These are
diagnostic demand figures.** A calculated 38-minute demand is never converted
into a 38-minute timetable period.

Every allocation is presented as:

> calculated demand → candidate operational period → variance → pedagogical
> justification

Operational periods use deliberate whole-minute durations — 20, 25, 30, 35, 40,
45, 50, 60, 75, or another explicitly justified whole-minute value.

**Standing note.** SHRS-ACD-2026-007 §§4.2–4.3 already fixes the operational
حصة at **40 minutes** across all five days, with the Qur'an hour at 60 and
مراجعة in blocks of 10 and 20. Any departure from 40 is a change to a
functioning recovered structure and carries the burden of justification; it is
not an open engineering choice.

## 7. Timetable governance

`[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` — the constitutional operating week:

| | |
|---|---|
| Monday–Wednesday | 14:00–18:00 |
| Thursday–Friday | no school |
| Saturday–Sunday | 09:00–15:00 |

**Three standing directions, and their evidential status as at Session 01:**

**(a) "Do not invent a 19-period ceiling unless an authoritative policy
document establishes one."** — An authoritative policy document establishes
one. ACD-007 §4.4 states «١٩ حصة» verbatim, and §§4.2–4.3 produce it by direct
count (3 حصص × 3 evenings + 5 × 2 day-sessions = 19). The figure was not
invented; it was **recovered late**, after this project had already spent a
cycle reasoning without it. Whether ACD-007 §4.4 is *authoritative* — as
against superseded by the Council's own later architecture — is `[BLACK]`,
referred as **CR-01**. The direction is honoured in substance: nothing is
assumed; the document is produced.

**(b) "Do not assume Thursday/Friday deductions that have not been formally
adopted."** — There are no Thursday or Friday deductions to assume. Both
documents and the direction itself make Thursday and Friday non-attendance
days. The live question is the **Saturday–Sunday** deduction structure, which
ACD-007 §4.3 specifies in full (طابور 15 · فسحة 15 · ظهر وغداء 45 · انصراف 5).
No scenario is required, because none is missing.

**(c) "Monday–Wednesday contains 20 minutes ʿAṣr and 10 minutes assembly."** —
ACD-007 §4.2 states **30** minutes for ʿAṣr, preceded by a separate **10** for
المراجعة الخاطفة والوضوء, and §4.3 states **15** for الطابور on day-sessions.
This is a genuine conflict between a sourced figure and a current direction.
`[RED]`, referred as **CR-02**. Neither figure is altered. Worth 1 hour 10
minutes a week.
