# P2-01 — SUBJECT-UNIVERSE RECONCILIATION
## ACD-007 §3.2 register (18) × the Council's 26-subject universe

Neither architecture receives priority for being cleaner. The register is
`[RECOVERED]`; the 26 are `[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION]` and later in time. The
task is to determine, for each discrepancy, whether it is a **superseded
decision** or an **accidental omission** — and to say which by evidence, not by
semantic similarity.

---

## 0. Two corrections to the Phase 2 redline

**(a) الخط.** Phase 2 listed الخط as a registered subject absent from the
matrix. The register names a **compound**: «**الخط والإملاء**». It is one
registered subject, of which the matrix retained one half. The finding stands
but its shape is different — this is a split with an unminuted partial
retirement, not a missing subject.

**(b) G12.** Phase 2 stated the register gives G12 *one* subject. It gives
**two**: خدمة التتويج (١٢) **and** البحث والخطابة (**١٠–١٢**), which is the only
register subject whose range explicitly includes ١٢. §6 below is rewritten on
the correct reading, and the divergence remains large.

## 1. The four subjects that triggered this gate

### 1.1 قواعد اللغة الوظيفية — ACD-007 ٤–٦

`[RECOVERED]` purpose: «**القاعدة تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن**» —
the rule is *touched in the text* before it is *named in the matn*. Outcome:
«وعي تركيبي عملي يهيّئ لمتون الإعدادية».

The matrix has no row. The nearest thing is **النحو as مضمّن at G5** — but
النحو names the rule, which is precisely what this subject exists to postpone.

**The subsumption is not a relabelling; it reverses the stated pedagogy.**
It is also the mechanism by which النحو was pulled two grades below its
register range: with no functional-grammar subject to occupy ٤–٦, النحو had to
descend to fill the gap.

**Verdict: `UNRESOLVED`.** Two readings, both defensible, neither minuted:

| | Reading A — accidental omission | Reading B — superseded |
|---|---|---|
| Claim | the subject was lost when the universe was rebuilt | the Council chose to name grammar from G5 |
| Consequence | restore as a distinct ٤–٦ subject; النحو returns toward ٧ | ratify; record that «تُلمس قبل أن تُسمى» is withdrawn |
| Cost | +1 subject at G4–G6 | the النحو range change becomes a decision, not a drift |

### 1.2 الخط — half of «الخط والإملاء»

Register outcome: «**يد نسخية مضبوطة** وهجاء صحيح في التحرير الحر» — a
disciplined copying hand *and* correct spelling. The matrix carries الإملاء
(spelling) at G2–G12 and **nothing owns the hand**.

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` — in a system whose pupils copy متون by hand and
whose Qur'anic work is plate-bound, الخط is not ornamental; it is the motor
skill the copying depends on.

**Verdict: `SPLIT — one half unminuted-RETIRED`.** Council must either restore
الخط (as its own subject or as a named strand inside الإملاء with its own
outcome), or minute its retirement and delete «يد نسخية مضبوطة» from the
graduation outcomes.

### 1.3 النمائية والنوادي — throughout

Register purpose: «الطفل يتعلم **باللعب والمشروع** ما لا يتعلمه بالمقعد».

**This one has recovered evidence of a home.** `[RECOVERED]` ACD-007 §4.3, the
day-session grid: الحصة الخامسة is annotated «مادة الجدول — **وللعمليات
والأنشطة نصيبها هنا**» — operations and activities take their share here.

**Verdict: `SUBSUMED` — into the Saturday/Sunday الحصة الخامسة**, not retired.
It is a **co-curricular block, not a matrix subject**, which is why it has no
row and why its absence is not an error. Council should confirm this reading
and record النمائية والنوادي in the architecture as a named block rather than
as a subject.

### 1.4 خدمة التتويج — ١٢

Register: «الخريج **يرد للأمة بعض ما أخذ — قبل أن يستلم شهادته**». Outcome:
«**مشروع خدمة منفذ ومناقشة تخرج أمام المجلس**» — an executed service project
and a graduation defence before the Council.

**Verdict: `UNRESOLVED` — and it is a graduation requirement, not a subject
that can quietly lapse.** It is the condition on receiving the certificate.
See §6.

## 4. The pattern, stated plainly

Of the fourteen register subjects that survive into the matrix:

- **six were split into twelve** — العقيدة، الفقه وأصوله، الحديث ومصطلحه،
  السيرة والتاريخ، التفسير، القرآن والتجويد
- **eight of those twelve descend three or more grades below their register range**
- **eleven of the fourteen extend by exactly one grade, into G12**

`[PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE]` These are not fourteen independent decisions. They
are one decision — *name the sciences early and separately* — applied
consistently, plus one consequence — *everything therefore needs a G12* —
applied consistently. **That is an architecture, not a drift.** It may be the
right architecture. It has simply never been stated as one, which is why it
reads as error.

The Council's real question is therefore narrow:

> **Does the system name the shar'ī sciences separately from G1–G4, or does it
> hold them undifferentiated to G6 as «التربية الإسلامية (ر–٦)» directs?**

Answer that and roughly twenty of the discrepancies above resolve together.
`[BLACK]`

(the grade-11 gate, `[RECOVERED]` §2.1) as the **joint** terminal gate of both.

## 6. G12 — MAJOR ARCHITECTURAL DIVERGENCE

### 6.1 What the recovered documents say G12 is

| Source | Statement |
|---|---|
| §2.1 | Stage «الثانوية والتتويج» ١٠·١١·١٢ — «علوم الآلة والمتون الكبرى **ثم سنة التتويج**». Exit gates: **الثانوية القرآنية (١١)** فـ**شهادة التخرج (١٢)** |
| §3.1 | «لا تكرر المنظومة مواد الصباح — **إلا في سنة التتويج** حيث تقدم **مراجعات WAEC المكثفة** خدمةً امتحانية» |
| §3.2 | **خدمة التتويج (١٢)**: «مشروع خدمة منفذ **ومناقشة تخرج أمام المجلس**» |
| §3.2 | **البحث والخطابة (١٠–١٢)**: «بحث محكم ١٥٠٠ كلمة وخطبة عشر دقائق ومناظرة» |
| §4.4 | **سنة التتويج = 546 hours** — against 754 for grades ١–١١ |

Read together: **G12 is a reduced-load terminal year** — service project, WAEC
examination service, research and oratory, graduation defence — sitting behind
a Qur'anic gate taken at **grade 11**, not 12. Twelve of the register's
subjects end at ١١ *by design*, because grade 11 is where the ladder's
scholarly gates close.

### 6.2 What the matrix says G12 is

**24 live subjects, 13 independent** — a full teaching year, heavier in
independent subjects than any grade except G11.

### 6.3 The divergence is not reconcilable by adjustment

| | Register G12 | Matrix G12 |
|---|---|---|
| Character | terminal / service / certification | instructional |
| Subjects | 2 + WAEC + project + defence | 24 |
| Load | **546 h — reduced by 208 h** | implies ≥ the standard year |
| Qur'an gate | at **grade 11** | حفظ still ● at G12 |
| Graduation condition | an executed service project | **not present** |

**Neither structure is adopted.** Three options, with consequences:

**Option A — ratify the register's G12.** Subjects terminate at ١١; G12 is
service, research, WAEC and defence at a reduced 546 hours.
*Consequence:* the matrix's 13 G12 independents dissolve — but **this is not a
subject cut**, because those subjects complete at G11 rather than being
deleted. It also explains, without cutting anything, the "five subjects demoted
at G12" regression found in Phase 2 §10: those authors were half-detecting this
structure. *Risk:* an eleven-year scholarly ladder ending a year before the
certificate may read as short to an external examiner.

**Option B — ratify the matrix's G12.** G12 is a full teaching year.
*Consequence:* the 546-hour figure, خدمة التتويج, the WAEC service and the
graduation defence must all fit inside it, or §4.4 and §3.2 are superseded and
must be minuted as such. *Risk:* on §4.4's own numbers this is arithmetically
impossible — but **that is a Phase 9 finding and is recorded here only as a
consequence, not used to decide.**

**Option C — instruction ends at G11; certification runs at G12.** Subjects
terminate at ١١ for teaching, and G12 carries their **terminal examination and
certification** alongside service, research and WAEC.
*Consequence:* this is the only option that uses both recovered gates as
written — الثانوية القرآنية at ١١, شهادة التخرج at ١٢ — and it makes G12
assessment-bearing without being instruction-bearing.
*Risk:* requires a clean doctrine separating **teaching identity** from
**assessment identity**, which the project has named but never applied.
