**Binding delivery rules:**
- **كل سبق يُلقَّن بمعناه الإجمالي** — التفسير at G1–G5 is a *manner of delivery

- **The Qur'an hour at G1–G3 is phonically sequenced against القراءة والتهجي** —

  interference, not reinforcement.
- **التجويد draws from the hour and holds no حصة** — practice مضمّن at all

  G9 وحدات. The pupil meets مواضع السكت inside جزء عم, and reads الصلوة in the
  muṣḥaf while being marked wrong for writing it.

## 6. Programme 3 — texts and the doctrine of frames

**The madhhab is that of أهل السنة with no exclusive school.** A single-school
core text is a **declared teaching frame (إطار تعليمي مُعلَن)**, never an
affiliation. The frame is Mālikī in فروع العبادات on three administrative
grounds — **عرف، توفر المعلم، صلاحية المتن** — **none of which is a claim that
Mālik is more correct, and that sentence is printed in the scheme of work.**

**The ladder deliberately runs four schools:** Mālikī in فروع · Shāfiʿī in
أصول (الورقات), الفرائض (الرحبية), مصطلح (نخبة الفكر) · Ḥanbalī in عمدة الأحكام
and لمعة الاعتقاد · Ḥanafī in الطحاوية and القواعد.

### Comparative method — four stages, beginning at G8

| طور | الصفوف | The pupil | May not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **التمكين** | ٦–٧ | one determinate practice; every ruling with its دليل from the first lesson | — |
| **التنبيه** | ٨ | meets the *fact* of خلاف — 4 masāʾil/yr: القبض والسدل · القنوت · الجهر بالبسملة · رفع اليدين · الصلاة خلف المخالف | be asked which is correct |
| **المقارنة** | ٩–١٠ | holds a second and third book **in his own hands**; learns سبب الخلاف | adjudicate — he reports the ʿulamāʾ's ترجيح |
| **الاستدلال** | ١١–١٢ | theorises خلاف (ابن رشد); may weigh under supervision, naming whose ترجيح it is | issue فتوى; select on ease |

> He watches the Tijānī, the Izāla-influenced, the NASFAT congregation and the
> old Ilorin masjid pray differently in the same street. **An unnamed difference
> is experienced as a deviation** — which is what produces the takfīr reflex at
> sixteen.

**Two prohibited outcomes:** **التعصب** (one position, the rest error) = الغلو ·
**تتبع الرخص** (four positions, pick the lightest) = التمييع.

### الفقه

الأخضرية G6–G7 (frame, with a **دليل sheet facing every باب**) · **متن أبي شجاع
G8** (Shāfiʿī — the pivot: a second arrangement of content he already owns) ·
الرسالة G8–G9 · **عمدة الفقه G9** (Ḥanbalī) · **مختصر القدوري G10** (Ḥanafī) ·
**المرشد المعين G10** (ابن عاشر — read as a *document*, its three strands named
aloud) · **إحياء السنة G11** (عثمان بن فودي) · **بداية المجتهد G11–G12** (the
comparative core, re-sited to arrive *after* he holds four books) · مختصر خليل —
**ruled out as a taught text**, recognition only.

### العقيدة

**الطحاوية is the spine** (G9 + G12) — a Ḥanafī author's creed accepted by
Ashʿarīs, Māturīdīs, Atharīs and Salafīs alike.

G7 **«متن الأدلة»** — the creedal نصوص themselves, memorised. *There is no
school-neutral creedal منظومة in existence; to memorise any at G7 is to adopt a
kalām school at twelve by default.* · الأصول الثلاثة G7 (cited by its author's
name aloud, never the only text in a year) · لمعة الاعتقاد G8 (**declared
Ḥanbalī/Athari aloud**) · **G10: أم البراهين *and* العقيدة الواسطية — same year,
same term, same teacher, both declared.** That pairing is the constitution made
operational · نواقض الإسلام G10 inside «التكفير: شروطه وموانعه», **موانع first**
· الاعتصام G11 (**البدعة taught from a Mālikī, deliberately**) · **كتاب التوحيد
— a bounded G11 unit, not the core**, after كف الحكم على المعيّن · **كشف الشبهات
— teacher reference only, no grade.**

**علم الكلام, G12, documentarily:** الإبانة · مقالات الإسلاميين · كتاب التوحيد
(الماتريدي — *the title clash is taught deliberately*) · إلجام العوام · تحريم
النظر في كتب الكلام **at equal length** · جوهرة التوحيد read as a document.
Assessment rule printed on the paper: *state each position in the terms its own
holders use, and name three things they agree on.* **The pupil is never asked to
adjudicate, and a paper that asks him to is a defect against the examiner.**

### الفرائض

A **ring-fenced باب with its own certificate line** — not a وحدة, because a
وحدة sharing a mark line is the thing cut when the term runs short, *and this
science has already been compressed to a passing note once.*

**متن الرحبية** memorised at G11. **Mathematics must certify LCM, equivalent
fractions and common-denominator addition in both notations before the unit
runs** — الرحبية does not fail for fiqh reasons; it fails at the notation
boundary.

**G12 applied block:** real anonymised estates worked to a distribution sheet ·
the State Sharia Court of Appeal · **the Marriage Act problem** — statutory
monogamous marriage and the administration route that can displace the Qurʾānic
shares · **Yorùbá ìdí-ìgì and orí-ọjọ́rí** set against the Qurʾānic shares.

> **The classical Mālikī and Shāfiʿī mashhūr assigns the residue to بيت المال.
> There is no بيت المال in Nigeria. The Board must minute the applied rule on
> ذوو الأرحام والرد — this single question decides real distributions every
> week.**

> **Legal materials are verified and kept current by a retained Nigerian
> practitioner, not by a scholar working from memory. A curriculum that teaches
> the wrong section number teaches a family to lose an estate.**

## 7. Programme 2 — sequencing

**القراءة والتهجي** G1–G3, مستقل, terminating at بوابة الطلاقة — *its exit
criterion is its completion condition.* 7 contacts at G1, distributed one per day
plus a second at the weekend, each حصة split **20 فك وتركيب + 20 قراءة محررة**.

**قواعد اللغة الوظيفية** G4–G6 — «القاعدة تُلمس في النص قبل أن تُسمى في المتن» —
standing behind **بوابة الجسر**, which has nothing else behind it. In **term 3 of
G6** it crosses its own threshold and begins naming in matn register what the
child has been touching since G4, so الآجرومية arrives as **names for things he
already recognises.**

**النحو** G7–G12 · **الآجرومية G7 (term 1 = تمهيد) · قطر الندى G8–G9 · الألفية
G10–G12.** Six years, six grades, the recovered sequence exactly — **ملحة
الإعراب and متن البناء are struck**, the first re-sited to النشيد والمحفوظات as
repertoire.

**اللغة العربية** مستقل G1–G12. *A pupil can إعراب a page of ابن عقيل and be
unable to sustain five minutes of spoken Arabic.* In Ibadan nothing outside this
timetable supplies the four skills. At G10–G12 it is **redefined, not reduced**:
المطالعة الموسعة of unvocalised prose, الاستماع, المحادثة والمناظرة, التحرير.

**الإنشاء** is a named composition strand inside اللغة العربية from G4 — a pupil
cannot arrive at G10 and produce a refereed 1,500-word paper having never been
taught to build a paragraph.

**العروض وعلم القافية** enters **G9, by ear**, inside النشيد. Yoruba is tonal and
English stress-timed; neither supplies a perceptual category for Arabic vowel
quantity, so a G5 pupil would count, and count wrong.

**الترجمة** G10–G12, **العربية → اليوربا primary.** Nobody else teaches the child
to render a تفسير or a khutbah into his own congregation's tongue — that is
unowned content, not duplication of the morning school.

## 9. Standing prohibitions

1. **لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة** — no subject on any list without timetabled minutes.
2. **No subject is taught less than once a week.** The fortnightly cycle is
   abolished; a subject met 3.8 times a year is not taught, it is mentioned.
3. **A certificate condition never carries a mark.** خدمة التتويج is pass/fail,
   non-compensable — «شرط استلام شهادة التخرج لا زينة فيها».
4. **No attribution is invented to fill a column.** Where no classical matn
   exists for a grade — as for الحديث at G1–G4 — the school compiles, and says so.
5. **Nigerian statutory citations are verified by counsel before any pupil sees
   them.** Substance may be certified by scholars; section numbers may not.
