> · **التجويد** كان مضمّنًا في الساعة القرآنية في الصفوف المتقدمة — وهو الآن **مستقل ٤–١١** (L-32)

## Cornerstones run throughout · lesser sciences run as term courses · nothing is compressed

---

## 1. Why this is better than merging — the arithmetic

| | Lessons/year | What the pupil experiences |
|---|---|---|
| A subject at **١ حصة/أسبوع، ٣٩ أسبوعًا** | **39** | met once a week; forgets between contacts; the teacher spends the first ten minutes of every lesson rebuilding context |
| The same subject at **٣ حصص/أسبوع، فصل واحد** | **39** | met three times a week for thirteen weeks; **held, finished, and examined while it is still warm** |

**Identical total. Opposite outcome.** And the decisive gain:

> **خانة فصلية — one weekly slot carries three term courses in a year.**
> Three sciences are each taught properly in the space where three sciences
> were each taught badly.

This is what removes the pressure that produced merging. **Nothing is
compressed into anything else.** الفرائض is not folded into الفقه; it holds
**٣٩ حصة in one term** — more contact than it would ever have received as a
weekly line, and it ends with its own paper.

## 2. Three classes of subject

| | الصنف | Shape | Which |
|---|---|---|---|
| **أ** | **مواد الأساس** — cornerstones | **full year, every year of their span**, 2–4 حصص | حفظ القرآن · اللغة العربية · النحو · الفقه · العقيدة · الحديث النبوي · التفسير · التربية الإسلامية *(primary)* · القراءة والتهجي *(G1–G3)* · قواعد اللغة الوظيفية *(G4–G6)* · أصول الفقه *(G10–G11)* |
| **ب** | **مقررات فصلية** — term courses | **one full term**, 2–3 حصص/أسبوع = **٢٦–٣٩ حصة** | الفرائض · المنطق · العروض وعلم القافية · الترجمة · النقد والآداب · البلاغة · مصطلح الحديث · الصرف *(from G10)* · التاريخ والسيرة *(from G10)* · الدراسات الحديثة |
| **ج** | **دورات مكثفة** — intensives | **a bounded block inside a host**, ≤ half a term | علوم القرآن · أصول رواية حفص · الرسم العثماني · فقه اللغة والمعاجم · علم الكلام · التحرير الامتحاني |

**A cornerstone never becomes a term course.** النحو، اللغة العربية، الفقه،
العقيدة، الحديث، التفسير run without interruption from their entry grade to
their exit grade. That is what makes them cornerstones.

**Fourteen sciences at SS 1 and SS 2 in ten blocks — none of them merged into
another, and none of them at one lesson a week.** The weakest provision
anywhere in the upper school is now **٢٦ حصة in a single term**, which is a
real course. Previously the weakest was 19 lessons scattered across a year, and
before that 3.8.

## 6. Rules that keep it honest

| | |
|---|---|
| **١** | **A cornerstone never becomes a term course.** النحو، اللغة العربية، الفقه، العقيدة، الحديث، التفسير run unbroken across their whole span. |
| **٢** | **A term course occupies a whole term, never a fragment.** ٢٦ حصة is the floor — below that it is a دورة مكثفة inside a host, and is reported in Section B. |
| **٣** | **A term course ends with its own assessment in the term it is taught**, while it is warm. It is not deferred to a June paper. |
| **٤** | **A term slot carries at most three courses a year** — one per term. A slot asked to carry four is a slot carrying fragments. |
| **٥** | **الفرائض holds the intensive slot at SS 2 ف١ by rule**, at ٣ حصص — because its arithmetic (العول، الرد، المناسخة) collapses if spread thin, and because it is the competence this graduate will be asked for first. |
| **٦** | **لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة** stands. A science with no term and no host is not on the register. |
