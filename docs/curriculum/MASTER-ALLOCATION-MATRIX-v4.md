## What the boards changed, and why

### القرآن وعلومه
- **التفسير's host returns to the Qur'an hour** (G1–G5). v3 had moved it to
  التربية الإسلامية — a Programme 3 subject — so a child would memorise for five
  years text whose meaning he was never told. The fix costs **zero minutes**: it is
  a manner-of-delivery rule inside سبق — *كل سبق يُلقَّن بمعناه الإجمالي*.
- **أصول التفسير deleted G1–G7.** An آلة whose object is تفسير cannot precede
  تفسير. Taught early it produces a pupil who accepts any «قال ابن عباس» *with
  confidence*. Entry G8 مضمّن, discipline proper at G11.
- **التجويد: مضمّن G1–G3 · مستقل G4–G11 · مضمّن G12.** v3's oscillation was the
  footprint of an edit, not a decision. A وحدة cannot carry the G11 certificate
  whose recovered wording is «خمسة عشر جزءًا راسخة **بتجويد مُجاز بلجنة**».

### اللغة العربية وعلومها
- **القراءة والتهجي ends at G3**, its recovered range. بوابة الطلاقة's criterion
  «قارئ مستقل ٤٠–٥٠ كلمة/دقيقة مشكولًا» *is* its completion condition; after it the
  subject has no content of its own and annexes its neighbour's.
- **اللغة العربية مستقل to G12.** The register says «throughout». The taper assumed
  النحو delivers the four skills; it does not. **Listening and spontaneous speech
  had no owner at all in the final three years.**
- **ملحة الإعراب and متن البناء struck.** With النحو restored to G7 the recovered
  sequence fits the recovered band exactly: الآجرومية G7 · قطر الندى G8–G9 ·
  الألفية G10–G12.
- **العروض وعلم القافية** — full name restored; entry moves G5 → **G9**, by ear
  first. Yoruba is tonal and English stress-timed; neither supplies a perceptual
  category for Arabic vowel quantity, so a G5 pupil would count, and count wrong.
- **الترجمة moves G6 → G10**, and its languages are named: **العربية → اليوربا**
  primary. That dissolves the §3.1 objection — nobody else teaches the child to
  render a تفسير or a khutbah into his own congregation's tongue.
- **الإنشاء restored** as a named composition strand inside اللغة العربية from G4.
  It fell out when البحث والخطابة was renamed and nothing picked it up.

### الدراسات الإسلامية
- **Strands at G1–G5 are four, not eleven:** التوحيد · الفقه · الحديث النبوي ·
  السيرة النبوية. التاريخ joins at G6. أصول الفقه، مصطلح الحديث and الدراسات
  الحديثة are **آلات** — instruments regulating a corpus the child does not yet
  hold. ACD-007 states the order itself: «حفظًا وفهمًا **ثم** آلة نقد علمي».
- **العقيدة and التوحيد are one science.** No classical discipline splits them, and
  in *this* institution keeping التوحيد as a separate named subject on the كتاب
  التوحيد model **declares structurally, in the timetable, an affiliation the
  constitution declines to declare in words.** The register's name العقيدة governs
  from G7; التوحيد remains the child-facing strand name G1–G6 and a named,
  separately-examined باب inside العقيدة thereafter.
- **RR-01 notation corrected.** مدمج is *symmetric*. v3 read `السيرة I / التاريخ ●`,
  which records التاريخ **hosting** السيرة — not the recovered merge. Both rows now
  read I at G7 and G9. **The recovered remedy «دمج السيرة بالتاريخ» was not
  actually implemented in v3.**
- **المنطق** is the row's identity; **علم الكلام** becomes a bounded G12 unit inside
  العقيدة — *دراسةٌ عن علم الكلام، لا دراسةٌ لعلم الكلام*. Not teaching it is not
  neutrality; the pupil meets these questions regardless, equipped or not.

## Unresolved — put to the Director General, not decided here

**1. The booklists contradict the madhhab ruling.** الفقه is monolithically Mālikī
(الأخضري، الرسالة، مختصر خليل، ابن عاشر); العقيدة is monolithically Najdi. The ban
on inferring doctrine from booklists was right while doctrine was open. **Now that
it is ruled, the obligation reverses:** the booklists must conform, or the
constitution declares one thing while the entire prescribed corpus teaches another.
Proposed operational test: **at least one عبادة topic per year from G10 taught from
a frame other than the year's default, minuted.**

**2. The 15-juz target.** ACD-007 priced «٢٥٤٠ ساعة» at 13 funded years — الروضة
plus G1–G12. Two Council decisions removed two of them. Funded acquisition is now
2,145 h = 84.6%, giving **12.7 juz**. Either certify at **12 juz متقنة, 15 as
امتياز**, or restore both years. Moving the gate to G12 alone yields 13.85 — not
sufficient by itself.

**3. G12 load.** You ruled the graduating class carries the most. Curriculum
science and assessment both say G12 should **fall** to 12–14 blocks, citing four
corpus signals — the 546-hour year, «الثاني عشر خفيف», the §3.2 ranges stopping at
١١, and خدمة التتويج existing as a G12-only subject. **Your ruling stands until you
change it.** The conflict is recorded rather than resolved.

**4. The child's week.** 54 hours in institutions at age 6, **no day free of
structured education**, and **no meal in the Monday–Wednesday session**. Deleting
the Sunday session at G1–G6 costs one Qur'an hour a week and buys a free day.

**5. WAEC has zero timetabled minutes at G12** while being a condition of the
certificate. The cycle balanced because a mandatory claimant was never entered.

**6. No assessment instrument exists** — no policy, no specification grid, no
specimen paper, no mark scheme, in twenty-odd documents.
