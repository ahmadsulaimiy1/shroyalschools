مِعيار عدم الحصر - THE NON-EXCLUSIVITY CONFORMITY TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A mechanical test, so that "no exclusive madhhab" is auditable rather than
aspirational. All five must hold:

    1. Every course DECLARES its frame explicitly in its documents.
    2. A BOUND REGISTER of the reasons for disagreement (سجل مدارك الخلاف) is
       maintained.
    3. All four schools appear TWICE OR MORE in the distribution, and NO SINGLE
       SCHOOL exceeds 50%.
    4. A comparative-disagreement question is worth 16% OF EVERY FIQH PAPER
       from class 10 onward, AND an answer given in a single madhhab may not
       score more than HALF the marks on it.
    5. The external reviewer is NOT from the frame school.

10.1  THE DOCTRINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instructional form determines assessment consequence MECHANICALLY. There is no
discretion. The table is in Part 3. The one rule that must never bend:

    الشرط لا يحمل درجة أبدًا  -  "A condition never carries a mark."

10.2  THE THREE-SECTION TRANSCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    SECTION A   Graded subjects. Enter the average. مستقل، مدمج، مضمّن.
    SECTION B   Units (وحدات). Achievement ratings. Do NOT enter the average.
    SECTION C   Conditions (شروط). Met / not met. NO MARKS EVER.

    Plus, for classes 1-2 only: a DESCRIPTIVE APPENDIX reading متقن / نامٍ /
    مبتدئ, with NO NUMBER. A small child gets one mark in Section A and a
    description - not a spread of numbers.

10.5  THE QUR'AN TARGET - WHY TWELVE AND NOT FIFTEEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is important and must not be quietly reverted. The original target was
FIFTEEN JUZʾ. That target was calculated on THIRTEEN funded years, including a
kindergarten (روضة). Two things then changed and the target was never
recalculated:

    - the kindergarten was abolished (L-01), removing funded years
    - the gate moved to class 11, removing another

The honest recalculation gives TWELVE JUZʾ MEMORISED TO A CERTIFIED STANDARD as
the certified requirement, with FIFTEEN retained as مرتبة الامتياز. A school
that certifies fifteen on twelve years of funding is certifying something it
did not fund. There is also an optional مسار الحفاظ (ḥuffāẓ track) from class 4
for those capable of more, sealed by a full khatmah before an EXTERNAL
committee of shaykhs.
