10.5  THE QUR'AN TARGET - WHY TWELVE AND NOT FIFTEEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is important and must not be quietly reverted. The original target was
FIFTEEN JUZʾ. That target was calculated on THIRTEEN funded years, including a
kindergarten (روضة). Two things then changed and the target was never
recalculated:

    - the kindergarten was abolished (L-01), removing funded years
    - the gate moved to class 11, removing another

The honest recalculation gives TWELVE JUZʾ MEMORISED TO A CERTIFIED STANDARD as
the certified requirement, with FIFTEEN retained as مرتبة الامتياز. A school
that certifies fifteen on twelve years of funding is certifying something it
did not fund. There is also an optional مسار الحفاظ (ḥuffāẓ track) from class 4
for those capable of more, sealed by a full khatmah before an EXTERNAL
committee of shaykhs.

================================================================================
PART 11 - THE PRESCRIBED TEXTS
================================================================================

Every subject has a named source (L-30). Where the school authors its own
material it is ADOPTED EXACTLY AS A MATN IS and enters the teacher's pack - it
is not left to the individual teacher's judgement. What is not yet written is
recorded as A DEBT OWED BY THE SCHOOL, never as a gap in the curriculum.
