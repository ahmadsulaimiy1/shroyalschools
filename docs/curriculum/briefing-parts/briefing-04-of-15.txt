PART 5 - TARTĪB AL-ʿULŪM: NO SCIENCE BEFORE ITS INSTRUMENT (L-34, L-35)
================================================================================

This is a doctrine, not a scheduling convenience. Stated in the register:

    العلمُ الذي يقوم على غيره لا يُفتَح قبل تمام ما يقوم عليه.
    وهذا ليس ترتيبًا تنظيميًّا يُتسامح فيه، بل شرطُ فهمٍ: من لقي «التقديم والتأخير»
    مصطلحًا بلاغيًّا قبل أن يعرفه إعرابًا، حفظ الاسم ولم يفهم المسمَّى.

    "A science that stands on another is not opened before the one it stands on
    is complete. This is not an organisational nicety to be relaxed; it is a
    condition of understanding. Whoever meets 'fronting and postponement' as a
    rhetorical term before knowing it as iʿrāb has memorised the name and not
    understood the thing named."

      -> first place: CLASS 9 - taste before terminology

      -> first place: class 12

    التفسير              القراءة والطلاقة        بوابة القراءة 1 · بوابة الطلاقة 3
      -> first place: CLASS 5, alongside الشهادة الابتدائية (L-36)

WHAT PRECEDES BALĀGHAH IN CLASS 9 IS النقد والآداب - chosen texts read, tasted
and memorised from, WITH NO RHETORICAL TERMINOLOGY AT ALL. The taste first; then
class 10 gives the student a name for what he had been finding and could not
name.

This correction removed balāghah from class 9 term 1 (where it had been
opening while Qaṭr al-Nadā was still unfinished that same year) and moved
النقد والآداب into the vacated term.
