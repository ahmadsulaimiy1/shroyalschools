THE SINGLE SOURCE OF ALLOCATION DATA:
    docs/curriculum/allocation-v11.json

Its structure, per class:  [cornerstones, term_slots, hosted]
    cornerstones = [subject, programme, weekly_slots, syllabus_note]
    term_slots   = [label, slots_per_week,
                      [[term, n_terms, subject, programme, note], ...]]
    hosted       = [subject, programme, host, note]

GENERATED FILES - NEVER HAND-EDIT THESE:
    docs/curriculum/CLASS-BOOK.md          <- generate-class-book.py
    docs/curriculum/SUBJECT-REGISTER.md    <- generate-subject-register.py

Both reproduce byte-for-byte from the JSON. Hand-editing them is precisely what
let the counts drift apart between documents. If you need to change the
curriculum, CHANGE THE JSON AND REGENERATE.

PART 16 - IF YOU ARE AN AI PICKING THIS UP
================================================================================

Do these things, in this order:

 1. READ docs/curriculum/00-LOCKED-DECISIONS.md IN FULL, before touching
    anything. Not the other documents - they are derived and may be stale.

 2. RUN python3 docs/curriculum/verify.py and confirm it is green, so you know
    the state you inherited.

 3. Do NOT rebuild a document from whatever is in front of you. That is the
    documented failure mode of this entire project and it has cost months.

 4. Do NOT hand-edit CLASS-BOOK.md or SUBJECT-REGISTER.md. Change the JSON and
    regenerate.

 5. Write subject names and text titles IN ARABIC.

 6. If a change would break a locked item, STOP AND ASK. Do not make the change
    and explain afterwards.

 7. If you are given a new ruling, write it into the register FIRST - in the
    change log, with its reason - and only then implement it.

 8. Do not present a mathematically balanced solution as though balance made it
    correct. A mathematical solution is not automatically a curriculum
    solution. Ask what a child of that age actually meets, what book is in his
    hand, and which science he needs before this one.

 9. Do not infer the school's creed or madhhab from any booklist.

10. When you report, be exact. Say what you verified and how. Say what you did
    not verify. The Director General has caught every unearned claim so far.
