PART 7 - TAJWĪD ACROSS THE TWELVE CLASSES (L-32, L-33)
================================================================================

This was one of the four errors the Chairman personally corrected. The
principle:

    الساعة القرآنية للحفظ والمراجعة. والتجويد علمٌ له حصته ومتنه وورقته.
    "The Qur'anic hour is for memorisation and revision. Tajwīd is a science
    with its own lesson, its own matn and its own paper."

Folding the science into the memorisation hour in the advanced classes left a
discipline that has a matn and no lesson. The Qur'anic sciences board had
already ruled that tajwīd should be independent - and that ruling was mislaid
during a rebuild.

    CLASS      FORM          SLOTS   MATN AND SYLLABUS
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1-3        مضمّن         -       No matn. Correction of makhārij and ṣifāt
                                     by ear and by talqīn only.
    4          مستقل         1       تحفة الأطفال والغلمان
    5          مستقل         1       تحفة الأطفال, completed
    6          مستقل         1       المقدمة الجزرية - introduction
    7          مستقل         1       الجزرية - المخارج والصفات
    8          مستقل         1       الجزرية - الأحكام
    9          مستقل         1       الجزرية completed · الوقف والابتدا ·
                                     فرش رواية حفص · الرسم العثماني
    10         مستقل         1       الإتقان - application across the whole
                                     muṣḥaf
    11         مستقل         1       الإقراء والتصحيح - preparation for the
                                     الثانوية القرآنية committee
    12         مضمّن         -       No matn. The science completed at class 11;
                                     here it is maintenance and checking only.

    INDEPENDENT IN EIGHT CLASSES (4-11) = 208 HOURS.

The two bolded items at class 9 - فرش رواية حفص and الرسم العثماني - are
CHAPTERS OF THIS SYLLABUS. They are not courses and must never be re-entered as
subjects.

Tajwīd's matn is bound INSIDE كتاب القرآن وعلومه, which is why making it
independent did not break the three-book floor in classes 4-6.

FUNDING: the weekly slots for tajwīd were taken one at a time from اللغة
العربية (classes 4,5,6), التربية الإسلامية (7,8), النحو (9), البحث والخطابة
(10) and أصول الفقه (11). Every class still balances exactly.

PART 8 - THE TERM-COURSE ARCHITECTURE (L-11, L-12)
================================================================================

This is one of the defining features of the design and it came directly from the
Chairman:

    "It is not necessary that you compress the subject into the advanced
    classes... some may be learned maybe even for just a term, like a course.
    Knowledge of inheritance to just be learned maybe for a term, first term of
    SS 2... like university courses. Sometimes some may drop and return."

MECHANISM: a خانة فصلية (term-slot) is ONE weekly slot that carries THREE
DIFFERENT COURSES across the three terms of a year. The slot is counted ONCE in
the weekly ledger, not three times. (An early version counted it once per
course, which produced a class 9 carrying 23 slots against a ceiling of 19.)

All term courses in a class share ONE bound volume, كراسة المقررات الفصلية,
counted as a single book under L-31.

THE TERM SLOTS AS THEY NOW STAND:

    CLASS 9  - خانة فصلية [2 slots/week]
        term 1  النقد والآداب     chosen texts, tasted, no rhetorical
                                  terminology - preparing for balāghah
        term 2  العروض وعلم القافية   the famous metres by EAR - ear training
        term 3  مصطلح الحديث      البيقونية, memorised

    CLASS 10 - خانة فصلية [1 slot/week]
        term 1  التاريخ والسيرة   الإسلام في نيجيريا - الشيخ آدم الإلوري
        term 2  النقد والآداب     pre-Islamic and Islamic texts
        term 3  الصرف             لامية الأفعال completed, applied taṣrīf

    CLASS 11 - خانة مكثفة [2 slots/week]  (an INTENSIVE slot)
        terms 1+2  الفرائض        متن الرحبية · 52 lessons · its own paper and
                                  its own certificate line (L-19)
        term 3     أصول التفسير   مقدمة ابن تيمية

    CLASS 12 - خانة فصلية (أ) [1 slot/week]
        term 1  الفرائض التطبيقية  estates · the Sharīʿah Court of Appeal ·
                                   the Marriage Act · إدي-إيجي and أوري-أوجوري
        term 2  الدراسات الحديثة   النوازل and the fiqh of coexistence
        term 3  علم الكلام         studied ABOUT, not studied FOR

    CLASS 12 - خانة فصلية (ب) [1 slot/week]
        term 1  النقد والآداب      Andalusian and African literature
        term 2  العروض وعلم القافية  علم القافية - the closing unit
        term 3  المنطق             التصورات والتصديقات

WHY THIS MATTERS: the term-course architecture is what removed the pressure
that had earlier produced indiscriminate MERGING of subjects. Before it existed,
the only way to fit a subject into a full timetable was to merge it into another
subject, which destroyed its identity. Term courses let a subject appear
properly, with its own paper, for a defined stretch.

PART 9 - MADHHAB POLICY AND THE NON-EXCLUSIVITY TEST
================================================================================

THE RULING (L-21, L-22): the school is أهل السنة with NO exclusive commitment to
one school of law, and the declared frame MAY DIFFER FROM TOPIC TO TOPIC.

A core text is a DECLARED TEACHING FRAME (إطار تعليمي مُعلَن), never an
affiliation (انتساب). And no single school frames every science. The ladder as
actually built runs:

    فروع الفقه        مالكي declared (المقدمة الأخضرية, الرسالة, بداية المجتهد)
    أصول الفقه        شافعي declared (الورقات, نظم الورقات)
    الفرائض           شافعي declared (متن الرحبية)
    مصطلح الحديث      شافعي declared (نخبة الفكر, نزهة النظر)
    أول متون الحديث   حنبلي (عمدة الأحكام, جامع العلوم والحكم)
    أول متون العقيدة  حنبلي (لمعة الاعتقاد)
    عمود العقيدة      حنفي المؤلف (العقيدة الطحاوية - the widest thing the
                      people of the Sunnah agreed upon)
    البدعة            مالكي deliberately (الاعتصام للشاطبي)
    الصوت الحنفي      الطحاوي, شرح معاني الآثار
    النظير الإقليمي   عثمان بن فودي, إحياء السنة - West African
