READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS AND HOW TO USE IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a complete, self-contained briefing on the curriculum of Sultan Hanafi
Royal Schools, a supplementary Islamic and Arabic school operating in the
Yoruba-speaking region of Nigeria (Ilorin / Ibadan). It is written to be handed
to another AI assistant, or to a human who has never seen this work before, so
that they can continue it without having to rediscover anything.

The curriculum is a single coherent twelve-year system. It is NOT twelve
separate year plans stitched together. Every decision below interacts with the
others, which is why the decisions are numbered and locked.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT THING TO UNDERSTAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project has one recurring failure mode, and it destroyed months of work
before it was diagnosed. The failure is this:

    Each document was rebuilt from whatever happened to be in front of the
    person rebuilding it. Every time that happened, decisions that had been
    settled earlier but did not appear in THAT PARTICULAR FILE were silently
    dropped. The Director General then had to restate them from memory.

These were never disagreements. They were losses. The same rulings were made
three and four times over.

The remedy is a single file, docs/curriculum/00-LOCKED-DECISIONS.md, called
"the register". It holds 35 numbered rulings, L-01 through L-35. Its governing
rule, in the original Arabic, is:

    لا يُغيَّر بند مقفل إلا بقرار صريح جديد من المدير العام.
    لا يُغيَّر بالنسيان، ولا بإعادة البناء، ولا باجتهاد لجنة.
    وما ليس في هذا السجل فليس بقرار.

    "A locked item is changed only by an explicit new ruling from the Director
    General. Not by forgetting. Not by rebuilding a document. Not by a
    committee's own judgement. And what is not in this register is not a
    decision."

If you work on this curriculum, THREE RULES BIND YOU:

  1. A locked item changes only by an explicit new ruling from the Director
     General. A board may refer a locked item back for reconsideration; it may
     not overrule one.

  2. If a change you are about to make would break a locked item, STOP. Do not
     make the change and explain afterwards. Raise it first.

  3. A new ruling is written into the register FIRST, in the change log, with
     its reason - and only then implemented in the other documents.

WORKING LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subject names, text titles and instructional forms are written IN ARABIC. This
was an explicit instruction from the Director General. English may gloss a rule;
it may never replace a name. "العروض وعلم القافية" is not "prosody". "البحث
والخطابة" is not "research and oratory". The reason given was direct: English
cannot do justice to Arabic. Keep the Arabic names throughout.

Throughout this briefing, Arabic names appear as they must be used. English
follows only as explanation.

PART 1 - THE CONSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE
================================================================================

1.1  TWELVE CLASSES, NO MORE AND NO FEWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The school runs exactly twelve classes. There is no kindergarten (روضة) and no
stage before class one. An earlier version of the plan assumed a kindergarten
existed and built the Qur'an memorisation target on thirteen funded years; when
the kindergarten was abolished, the target was not recalculated. That error is
described in Part 9.

1.2  FOUR SECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    القسم التمهيدي   - classes 1-2   (Primary 1-2)
        Theme: التهيئة وفك الحرف - preparation and unlocking the letter
        Exit gate: بوابة القراءة (the reading gate) in class 1

    القسم الابتدائي  - classes 3-6   (Primary 3-6)
        Theme: القراءة فالطلاقة فالجسر إلى عربية العلم
               reading, then fluency, then the bridge to the Arabic of
               scholarship
        Exit gates: بوابة الطلاقة (3) · الشهادة الابتدائية (5) · بوابة الجسر (6)

    القسم الإعدادي   - classes 7-9   (JSS 1-3)
        Theme: العلوم الشرعية بأسمائها والنحو على متونه
               the sharʿī sciences under their own names, and grammar on its
               classical matns
        Exit gate: الشهادة الإعدادية (9), with an external examiner

    القسم الثانوي    - classes 10-12 (SS 1-3)
        Theme: علوم الآلة والمتون الكبرى ثم سنة التتويج
               the instrumental sciences and the major matns, then the
               capstone year
        Exit gates: الثانوية القرآنية (11) · شهادة التخرج (12)

1.3  THREE PROGRAMMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    البرنامج الأول   ·  القرآن وعلومه          (the Qur'an and its sciences)
    البرنامج الثاني  ·  اللغة وعلومها          (language and its sciences)
    البرنامج الثالث  ·  الدراسات الإسلامية      (Islamic studies)

NOTE CAREFULLY: Programme Two is اللغة وعلومها. It is NOT "اللغة العربية
وعلومها". This was corrected explicitly and has been wrong in drafts before.

The binding rule:

    كل مادة في كل صف تقع تحت واحد من البرامج الثلاثة، ولا تقع خارجها.
    "Every subject, in every class, falls under one of the three programmes -
    and none falls outside them."

There is one presentational exception at class 12: WAEC and التحرير الامتحاني
are grouped under a heading called خدمة سنة التتويج (capstone-year service).
They remain accountable to the three-programme rule; the heading is an
administrative grouping for the certificate year, not a fourth programme.
