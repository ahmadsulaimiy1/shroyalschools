The load rises and the graduating class carries the most, as L-09 and L-10
require. The book floor of three holds through the whole of the lower school
even as the sciences grow - which is exactly what L-05 intends: the child meets
more knowledge without carrying more books.

WHAT IS *NOT* A SUBJECT - do not re-add these, they have been removed twice:

    الرسم العثماني        A chapter inside the class-9 tajwīd syllabus. Not a
                          course, not a subject. (L-33)
    رواية حفص عن عاصم     A description of the muṣḥaf we read from (L-23). Not
                          a subject that gets taught. Its farsh is a chapter in
                          the tajwīd paper.
    علوم القرآن           Not a standalone course. Its topics are distributed:
                          أصول التفسير sits inside التفسير, and التجويد is a
                          science in its own right.
    المصحف                A source. Never counted among the books or subjects.
