SHRS CURRICULUM BRIEFING — PART 3 OF 15  (continued — reply only "OK")
============================================================================


L-02  ٤ أقسام: التمهيدي (١–٢) · الابتدائي (٣–٦) · الإعدادي (٧–٩) · الثانوي (١٠–١٢)
      Four sections, with those exact class ranges. This was forgotten once in
      a rebuild and had to be restated angrily.

L-03  ٣ برامج: القرآن وعلومه · اللغة وعلومها · الدراسات الإسلامية
      Three programmes. Note again: اللغة وعلومها, not اللغة العربية وعلومها.

L-04  كل مادة تحت واحد من البرامج الثلاثة، ولا واحدة خارجها
      Every subject under one of the three programmes; none outside.

L-05  في التمهيدي والابتدائي كل شيء مدمج أو مضمّن. لا يُثقَل الطفل بالكتب
      In التمهيدي and الابتدائي (classes 1-6) EVERYTHING is merged or embedded.
      The child is not burdened with books. This is why classes 1-6 carry only
      three books despite meeting twelve or thirteen named sciences.

L-06  الحد الأدنى ثلاثة كتب — كتاب لكل برنامج — والمصحف. والمصحف أصلٌ لا كتابَ تعليم
      Minimum THREE books - one per programme - plus the muṣḥaf. The muṣḥaf is
      a source, not a teaching book, and is never counted as Programme One's
      book. This was originally "two books", which left Programme One with no
      book at all in the lower classes. The Director General caught it: if
      there are three programmes, there must be at least three books.

L-07  لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة — لا مادة بلا حصص ولا مضيف مسمّى
      No name without a minute. See Part 3.

L-08  الصيغ وحدها: مستقل · مدمج · مضمّن · وحدة · دوراني · مسار
      Those forms and no others.

L-09  الحمل يرتفع مع الصف
      The load rises with the class. Note: this governs PROGRAMME LOAD overall.
      It does NOT mean an individual subject can never be demoted. An earlier
      draft invented a "monotonic status" rule - once a subject rose it could
      never fall - and this was explicitly withdrawn. The Director General said
      plainly that it is not compulsory to teach every subject every term.

L-10  صف التخرج يحمل الأكثر — ولا يُخفَّض بقرار لجنة
      The graduating class carries the MOST. A board once reduced class 12's
      load on its own judgement and had to be reversed. Class 12 currently
      carries 27 sciences and 14 books - more than any other class.

L-11  لا يلزم أن تُدرَّس كل مادة كل فصل — مقررات فصلية كالجامعة
      Not every subject must be taught every term. Term courses, like a
      university. This is a central design idea; see Part 6.

L-12  يجوز أن تنقطع المادة ثم تعود — بشرط أن تعود أعلى، لا تكرارًا
      A subject may pause and return - provided it returns HIGHER, not as
      repetition.

L-13  مواد الأساس لا تنقطع: النحو · اللغة العربية · الفقه · العقيدة · الحديث · التفسير
      The cornerstones never pause. These six run as independent full-year
      subjects continuously from class 7 to class 12.

L-14  البلاغة: ستة فصول كاملة
      Balāghah gets six full terms. It currently has NINE, so this floor is
      comfortably exceeded.

L-15  العروض وعلم القافية: لا يُدرَّس في فصل واحد
      Prosody and rhyme are NOT taught in a single term. The Director General
      was explicit: three or four terms, not one.

L-16  النقد والآداب: مادة لها وزنها — لا وحدة صغيرة
      Criticism and literature is a subject with real weight, not a small unit.

L-17  الصرف: حاضر في كل صف متقدم
      Morphology is present in EVERY advanced class - 7 through 12, without
      exception.

L-18  الخطابة والتواصل أولوية — ومعها الترجمة إلى اليوربا
      Oratory and communication are a priority, and with them translation into
      YORUBA. Yoruba is the mother tongue of the students. The graduate must be
      able to render his research and his khuṭbah back into Yoruba. This has
      been silently dropped from drafts before.

L-19  الفرائض: باب مُصان بورقة وسطر مستقلين
      Inheritance law is a ring-fenced chapter with its own paper and its own
      line on the certificate.

L-20  الساعة القرآنية محمية — لا تُقتطع لأي مادة في أي صف
      The Qur'anic hour is protected in all twelve classes.

L-21  مذهب أهل السنة، بلا التزام مذهب واحد
      Ahl al-Sunnah, with NO exclusive commitment to a single school of law.

L-22  يجوز أن يختلف الإطار من باب إلى باب
      The declared frame may differ from topic to topic. See Part 8.

L-23  الرواية: حفص عن عاصم · المصحف: مطبعة الملك فهد
      The riwāyah is Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim; the muṣḥaf is the King Fahd printing.

L-24  الأسبوع: الإثنين–الأربعاء ٢–٦ مساءً · الخميس والجمعة لا دوام · السبت والأحد ٩–٣
      The operating week as in Part 2.

L-25  الحصة ٤٠ د · الفصل ١٣ أسبوعًا · السنة ٣٩ أسبوعًا في ثلاثة فصول
      The units as in Part 2.

L-26  لا يُختلق دليل مفقود
      A missing document is never invented. If evidence is absent, say it is
      absent.

L-27  لا يُخلط المستردّ بقرار المجلس الحالي
      Recovered material is never mixed with a current ruling. Each is labelled.

L-28  لا يُقدَّم الاستنتاج على أنه نصٌّ مستردّ
      An inference is never presented as recovered text.

L-29  لا تُستنبط العقيدة ولا المذهب من قوائم الكتب
      Neither creed nor school of law is inferred from a booklist. If a draft
      proposes classical texts, that does not establish the school's
      orientation. Only an explicit ruling does.

L-30  لكل مادة في كل برنامج نصٌّ مقرَّر أو مادة مصدر. ولا مادة بلا مصدر
      Every subject in every programme has a prescribed text or a named source
      material. No subject without a source. When this check was first run it
      FAILED on thirteen subjects - including التربية الإسلامية itself, the
      first book a child ever holds. Chapter Four of the register is the
      remedy.

L-31  عدّ الكتب بقاعدة واحدة معلنة
      Books are counted by one declared rule, not by hand:
        - كتاب القرآن وعلومه counts as 1 in every class, all twelve years
        - one book for every independent FULL-YEAR subject
        - all term courses share ONE volume: كراسة المقررات الفصلية
        - مدمج and مضمّن carry NO book
        - التجويد carries no separate book; its matn is bound inside
          كتاب القرآن وعلومه
        - the muṣḥaf is a source and is never counted
      This rule exists because the book counts had drifted between documents -
      the same class showing 9 books in one file and 11 in another - purely
      because each had been tallied separately by hand.

L-32  التجويد مادة مستقلة من الرابع إلى الحادي عشر — حصةٌ ومتنٌ وورقة.
      لا يُضمَّن في الساعة القرآنية في الصفوف المتقدمة
      Tajwīd is an INDEPENDENT subject from class 4 to class 11: its own weekly
      lesson, its own matn, its own paper. It is NOT folded into the Qur'anic
      hour in the advanced classes. The hour is for ḥifẓ and murājaʿah. See
      Part 7.

L-33  الرسم العثماني وفرش رواية حفص بابان داخل منهج التجويد في التاسع —
      لا مادتان ولا مقرران. والرواية وصفُ المصحف لا مادةَ تُدرَّس
      ʿUthmānī orthography and the farsh of Ḥafṣ are CHAPTERS inside the class-9
      tajwīd syllabus - not subjects and not courses. The riwāyah is a
      description of the muṣḥaf we read from (L-23), not a subject on a
      timetable.
