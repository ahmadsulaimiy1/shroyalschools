This section exists so that the next person does not spend weeks rediscovering
what has already been settled. Every item below actually happened.

12.1  STRUCTURAL AND ARITHMETIC ERRORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. A whole recovered document (SHRS-ACD-2026-007, Part 4, sections 4.1-4.4)
    containing a complete timetable and ledger was MISSED, and version 1 of the
    ledger was built on invented scenarios instead. Always read the recovered
    corpus completely before building.

 2. A "monotonic status" rule was INVENTED - once a subject rose in status it
    could never be demoted. This was never ruled and was withdrawn. It is not
    compulsory to teach every subject every term.

 3. All eleven sharʿī sciences were made STRANDS at classes 1-6, including the
    instrumental sciences (أصول الفقه، مصطلح الحديث، المنطق). A child of six
    does not have a strand of uṣūl al-fiqh. Corrected to four legitimate
    strands: التوحيد، الفقه، الحديث، السيرة.

 4. A shared term-slot was counted ONCE PER COURSE instead of once per slot,
    producing a class 9 carrying 23 slots against a ceiling of 19.

 5. The MUṢḤAF was counted as Programme One's book, which left the lower
    classes with books for only two of three programmes. The muṣḥaf is a
    source. كتاب القرآن وعلومه was created to be Programme One's actual book.

 6. WAEC had ZERO TIMETABLED MINUTES at class 12 while gating the leaving
    certificate - and a "38/38 subjects accounted for" check still balanced,
    because a mandatory claimant had never been entered into the ledger at all.
    This is the origin of L-07.

 7. A timetable was built on allocation matrix v2 while the matrix in force was
    v3 - two internally verified documents specifying different weeks.

 8. Asymmetric مدمج notation (السيرة I / التاريخ ●) was recording a HOST-GUEST
    relation, not a merge - so a ruling to merge sīrah with history had never
    actually been implemented despite appearing to be.

 9. FOUR SUBJECTS WERE SILENTLY DROPPED during an unrelated balāghah rebuild:
    أصول التفسير · التاريخ والسيرة · الترجمة · مصطلح الحديث. The Director
    General noticed. This is the single most characteristic failure of this
    project.

10. The L-30 check FAILED on its first run: thirteen subjects had no source at
    all, including التربية الإسلامية - the first book a child ever holds.

11. The three-programme structure was forgotten, and then the four sections
    were forgotten, in successive rebuilds.

12. The Qur'anic sciences board's ruling that تجويد be مستقل at classes 4-11
    was OVERRIDDEN in a rebuild, folding the science back into the memorisation
    hour. This is now L-32.

13. الرسم العثماني was carried as an independent course and رواية حفص as
    another. Neither is a course. This is now L-33.

14. البلاغة was opened in class 9 term 1 while قطر الندى was still unfinished
    that same year - teaching a science before its instrument. And within
    balāghah the order was بيان then معاني, which is inverted. These are now
    L-34 and L-35.

15. التفسير was taught from class one, embedded in the Qurʾānic hour, to
    children still unlocking the letter. Meaning before reading is a science
    before its instrument. It now begins at class five. This is now L-36.

16. Book counts drifted between documents - the same class showing 9 in one
    file and 11 in another - purely because each file was tallied by hand. This
    is now L-31.

17. A Primary 6 regression during the tajwīd fix pushed that class to FOUR
    books by splitting قواعد اللغة الوظيفية out as its own entry. It folds back
    into the language book carrying بوابة الجسر.

18. The annual ledger claimed 754 hours where 100 minutes had been booked as
    مراجعة when 60 of those minutes were wuḍūʾ and dismissal. Corrected to 715.
