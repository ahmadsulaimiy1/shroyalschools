PART 2 - TIME: THE OPERATING WEEK AND THE ANNUAL LEDGER
================================================================================

2.1  THE WEEK AS ACTUALLY OPERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Monday    14:00 - 18:00
    Tuesday   14:00 - 18:00
    Wednesday 14:00 - 18:00
    Thursday  NO SCHOOL (لا دوام)
    Friday    NO SCHOOL (لا دوام)
    Saturday  09:00 - 15:00
    Sunday    09:00 - 15:00

This is a supplementary school. Children attend other schooling on Thursday and
Friday. The week is short and every minute is contested - which is why the
arithmetic below is treated as a constitutional matter and not an
administrative one.

2.2  THE UNITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    حصة (lesson slot)     = 40 minutes
    فصل (term)            = 13 weeks
    سنة (year)            = 39 weeks, in three terms
    Weekly slots, classes 1-6   = 13
    Weekly slots, classes 7-12  = 19

2.3  THE PROTECTED QUR'ANIC HOUR - الساعة القرآنية
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A protected daily hour for memorisation and revision sits OUTSIDE the 13 or 19
weekly slots entirely. It is 290 minutes a week in classes 1-6 and 340 minutes
a week in classes 7-12.

    الساعة القرآنية محمية - لا تُقتطع لأي مادة في أي صف
    "The Qur'anic hour is protected - it is not drawn upon for any subject in
    any class."

No subject may take minutes from it. This has been violated in drafts by
quietly hosting subjects inside it; see Part 9, error 12.

2.4  THE ANNUAL LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Classes 1-6:   13 slots x 39 weeks x 40 min = 338h subject time
                   + 188h30m Qur'anic hour
                   = 526h30m per year

    Classes 7-12:  19 slots x 39 weeks x 40 min = 494h subject time
                   + 221h Qur'anic hour
                   = 715h per year

    THE WHOLE TWELVE-YEAR JOURNEY:  7,449 hours

A rule of method applies here and must be respected: never round silently, and
always separate ANALYTICAL DEMAND (how much time a subject needs) from
OPERATIONAL ALLOCATION (how much time it is actually given). A timetable period
is an operational decision; it is not the unit of curriculum design.

An earlier ledger claimed 754 hours per year and a 19h20m week. That was wrong:
100 minutes had been booked as مراجعة (revision) when 60 of those minutes were
actually wuḍūʾ and dismissal. The corrected figures - 18h20m per week and 715
hours per year - are the ones above.

PART 3 - THE VOCABULARY OF INSTRUCTIONAL FORM
================================================================================

This vocabulary is closed. These six forms and no others may be used to describe
how a subject is delivered. Inventing a seventh form is a breach of L-08.

    مستقل   INDEPENDENT
            Holds its own timetable slot. Has its own book. Has its own exam
            paper. Enters the average. Appears in Section A of the transcript.

    مدمج    MERGED
            Shares a slot with ONE named partner subject. Has no book of its
            own - it lives inside the partner's book. It gets a NAMED SECTION on
            the partner's paper with a 40% floor, so it cannot be quietly
            dropped. Enters the average, aggregated.

    مضمّن   EMBEDDED
            Taught inside a NAMED HOST subject. No slot, no book, no separate
            paper. Assessed inside the host. Still appears in Section A through
            its host.

    وحدة    UNIT
            A named, separately examined block that never holds a slot of its
            own. Consumes its HOST's minutes. Appears in Section B of the
            transcript as an achievement rating. Does NOT enter the average.

    مسار    STRAND
            A thread inside a book (used for the four strands inside كتاب
            الدراسات الإسلامية in the lower classes). No slot, no book, no
            mark. Appears only as a descriptive appendix - متقن / نامٍ / مبتدئ -
            with NO NUMBER attached.

    دوراني  ROTATING
            A slot that cycles between courses. Reserved vocabulary; used
            sparingly.

    شرط     CONDITION
            Not an instructional form but an assessment category. A شرط NEVER
            CARRIES A MARK. It appears in Section C of the transcript as met or
            not met. خدمة التتويج and the WAEC sitting requirement are شروط.

THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE (L-07):  لا اسمَ بلا دقيقة
    "No name without a minute."
    No subject may appear on any list unless it has either timetabled lessons
    or a NAMED HOST. A subject with neither is a fiction. This rule exists
    because a subject (WAEC) once gated the leaving certificate while having
    zero timetabled minutes anywhere in the school, and a "38/38 subjects
    accounted for" check still came out balanced because the claimant had never
    been entered into the ledger at all.

ASSESSMENT FOLLOWS FORM MECHANICALLY. The instructional form determines the
assessment consequence with no discretion:

    form      paper?                    book?           average?   transcript
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    مستقل     yes                       yes             yes        Section A
    مدمج      named section, 40% floor   partner's book  yes        Section A
    مضمّن     no                        no              via host   Section A
    وحدة      achievement rating         no              no         Section B
    مسار      no                        no              no         descriptive
    شرط       NEVER CARRIES A MARK       -               no         Section C
