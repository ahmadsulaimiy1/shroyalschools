# REDLINE FINDING — Timetable Arithmetic Audit
## SHRS G1–G12 · 26 subjects · 3 programmes

**Status:** Findings only. No solutions adopted. No architectural change authorised by this document.

> **SUPERSEDED IN PART — see `MASTER-TIMETABLE-LEDGER.md` §7.**
> The ceilings this audit measured demand against (7 and 19 lessons/week) were
> never derived from the clock. Rebuilt from the constitutional operating week,
> **the G6, G8 and G12 overloads reported below do not exist.** The findings
> about contradictions *between* subjects survive unchanged. This document is
> retained as the record of how the error was found, not as a source of figures.

---

## 1. Method

Every one of the 26 subject lifecycles states its own period demand. No subject
had sight of any other. This audit is the first time the 26 stated demands have
been summed against the same ledger.

Ceilings [RECOVERED]:
- G1–G2: 7 lessons/week (40 min) for all of Programme 2 + Programme 3 combined
- G3–G12: 19 lessons/week (40 min) for the same
- Qur'an hour: 60 min/day × 5 days × 39 weeks = 195 hours/year, ring-fenced at
  every grade, constitutionally protected against being cut in favour of another
  subject

---

## 2. Finding 1 — the protected Qur'an hour is over-booked

Four subjects claim minutes inside the same protected 60 minutes at G1. Each
correctly reports its own cost as "zero timetable periods." None of them knew
about the others.

| Subject | Claim at G1 |
|---|---|
| حفظ القرآن | two sub-blocks of ~25–30 min = 50–60 min |
| القراءة والتهجي | 20–25 min/day |
| النشيد والمحفوظات | ~5 min/day |
| التفسير (meaning layer) | brief, at point of memorisation |
| التجويد | correction-in-context (embedded) |
| **Total claimed** | **80–95 min** |
| **Available** | **60 min** |

**Over-booked by approximately 20–35 min/day (33–58%).**

This does not establish which claim should yield. It establishes only that the
claims cannot all simultaneously be true.

## 3. Finding 2 — three subjects each verified G6 and reached three different answers

G6 ceiling: 19 lessons/week.

| Who checked | Total reached | Why it differs |
|---|---|---|
| النحو's own feasibility check | 19 — exactly full | assumed اللغة العربية 3, الجزرية 2, الصرف 2, التوحيد والعقيدة 2, الفقه والسيرة 3, الحديث 1 |
| الحديث's own reconstruction | 17 — 2 in hand | assumed الجزرية 1, اللغة العربية 4, النحو 2, الصرف 1, الفقه والسيرة 2, الحديث 2 |
| This audit, using each subject's own stated demand | **~21–22** | no assumptions about neighbours |

Neither check was dishonest. Each guessed its neighbours' numbers, and the
guesses were lower than the neighbours' actual stated demands.

**G6 over by approximately 2–3 lessons.**

## 4. Finding 3 — overloaded years

| Grade | Available | Demanded | Over by |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1–G2 | 7 | contested — see Finding 5 | undetermined |
| G6 | 19 | ~21–22 | 2–3 |
| **G8** | 19 | **~23** | **~4** |
| G12 | 19 | ~22 | ~3 |

G8 is the worst-loaded grade in the ladder. The cause is structural, not
careless: nearly every subject independently and correctly declined to place
itself at G6–G7 on the grounds that G6–G7 were already the heaviest matn-entry
years. The deferred load landed on G8, and no subject checked what else had
deferred there.

## 5. Finding 4 — direct contradiction at G1–G2

| Subject | States |
|---|---|
| اللغة العربية | indicative fit is "اللغة العربية 3 + القراءة والتهجي 3 + 1 shared = 7 exactly. There is no slack." |
| القراءة والتهجي | "ZERO periods from the contested pool at every single grade" — hosted inside the Qur'an hour instead |

Both cannot be true. The consequence is material:

- If القراءة والتهجي costs 0 of the 7, then **4 lessons remain** at G1–G2 for
  الفقه التطبيقي and التوحيد العملي.
- If اللغة العربية's fit is correct, **0 lessons remain** for either.

Every Programme 3 subject at G1–G2 independently declared zero claim on the 7
periods, on the assumption that embedded time would be available inside
existing blocks. If Programme 2 holds all 7, that assumption fails.

## 6. Finding 5 — العقيدة and التوحيد disagree about their own shared block

| Subject | States |
|---|---|
| العقيدة | "the التوحيد والعقيدة block needs 3 periods of the 19" (G5–G7) |
| التوحيد | "الزوج = حصتان/أسبوع" — 2 periods (G5–G9) |

Separately, and more seriously, they disagree about the eventual relationship:

- العقيدة: "G8 — العقيدة becomes مستقل **and التوحيد merges into it**"
- التوحيد: "G10 — يصير التوحيد مادة مستقلة، **وتنفصل عنه العقيدة** إلى مادة شقيقة"

Mirror-image claims. Each specialist wrote believing its own subject was the
parent that absorbs the other. They disagree on direction and on timing.

---

## 7. Finding 6 — the systemic cause

The four overloads and three contradictions above share one cause:

**Each subject was optimised independently, and each was permitted to treat
another subject's time as free.**

Recurring patterns in the stated demands:

- "ZERO additional periods — مضمّن inside [another subject]'s existing block"
  appears in the majority of the 26 lifecycles. Embedded content still consumes
  teacher minutes; those minutes were nowhere debited.
- "budget-neutral, because [another subject] releases periods at this point"
  assumes a release that the releasing subject did not confirm.
- "independently assessed" is repeatedly attached to demands that request no
  period to be assessed in.

**Conclusion:** the arithmetic is not a collection of local errors to be patched.
It is a systemic consequence of independent optimisation without a shared ledger.

---

## 8. Consequent principle proposed for adoption

> **No programme, subject or strand may claim timetable space independently.
> Every period, and every embedded minute, must be drawn from one authoritative
> G1–G12 master ledger. Time belonging to another subject is not free unless the
> ledger records it as free.**

## 9. What this audit does NOT establish

It does not establish that حفظ القرآن should receive fewer minutes.
It does not establish that القراءة والتهجي should move inside or outside the hour.
It does not establish which subjects should yield at G6, G8 or G12.
It does not establish that the protected hour must be 60 minutes on every day.

Those are solutions. This document records only that the present claims are
mutually inconsistent and quantifies by how much.
