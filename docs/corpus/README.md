**These four documents are the foundation. Everything else derives from them.**

They were uploaded on 10 September 2026, read in full, and then existed **only
in the session's temporary upload cache** — an ephemeral container that is
reclaimed when the session ends. They were never committed. Had this session
closed, the four founding documents of the institution would have been lost,
and the corpus would have been rebuilt from derived material a twelfth time.

They are committed here, with their original PDFs untouched and a UTF-8 text
extraction beside each for searching and citation.

extractions — a reading and grep aid, **not** an authority. Where the text
extraction and the PDF disagree, the PDF governs.

Adjudication Register** — which names and rules on ten prior documents by ID.
It is the evidentiary base for the whole recovery, and it is the reason we know
that «الدستور الأعلى وميثاق التصميم» (PUB-004/005) existed and was absorbed
rather than abandoned.

**Do not edit these files. Ever.** They are historical record, not working
documents. A correction to their substance is a new ruling in a master
