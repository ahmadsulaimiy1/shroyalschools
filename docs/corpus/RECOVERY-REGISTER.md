### The five Articles of Supremacy — [RECOVERED], transcribed

1. **السيادة** — the highest editorial document in the house. What contradicts
   it from a prior system is **منسوخ**; what agrees is **مستوعَب**. Amended only
   by a Council decision recorded in the version register.
2. **النطاق الجامع** — governs *everything bearing the school's name without
   exception*: curriculum documents, frameworks, detailed curricula, teacher
   guides, lesson plans, assessment books, student books, examination papers,
   certificates, student reports, notebooks, exercise books, diaries, posters,
   guides, policies, research and council reports. **«وما ليس تحته فليس من
   الدار.»**
3. **المحاكمة الرجعية** — everything prior is adjudicated: موافق · يعاد إصداره
   بموعده · يُسحب. **«ولا يُعفى عمل لقدمه أو لحسن سمعته.»**
4. **الصدق التقريري** — nothing may be described in any report or colophon as
   having met a standard until a documented check through the gates; **a claim
   exceeding its check is a violation recorded in the quality register under the
   name of whoever wrote it.**
5. **سؤال العقود** — read as an adjudication every two years. **«ولا يوصف
   الدستور نفسه بالكمال أبدًا.»**

> Article 4 is, word for word, the discipline the curriculum verifier already
> enforces — «فلا يُقال ‹تمّ التحقق› حتى تُقرأ». The convergence is worth
> recording: the curriculum apparatus was built in ignorance of PUB-006 and
> arrived at its rule independently.

### صيغة الانتساب الواحدة — [RECOVERED]

The attribution line is fixed and *does not vary from book to book*:

- **Arabic:** الهيئة الأكاديمية العالمية للدراسات العربية والإسلامية والدعوة والمناهج والبحوث (GACAIS)
