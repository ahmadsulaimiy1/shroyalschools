## 1.1 مادة السيادة · Supremacy

**[RECOVERED — PUB-006, Part 1 §1.1, المادة الأولى]**

**How this Master stands to that Article.** PUB-006 remains the controlling
recovered source. This Master **reorganises** its provisions into the
three-document architecture; it does not replace them, and it does not acquire

that provision now sits.** *(AD-01 rule 3)*

with it.

## 2.5 Educational philosophy — hybrid by design, not compromise

**[RECOVERED — `editorial-bible.md` Part I]**

The Royal College and Nursery/Primary run the **Nigerian National Curriculum**,
enriched with entrepreneurship, financial intelligence, leadership and technology
electives. The School of Islamic and Arabic Studies and the Qur'an College run an
**Islamic and Arabic curriculum** on classical texts.

Both report to **one board, one Head of Schools / Administrator, and one
disciplinary and pastoral framework.** That single line of account — rather than
the subjects taught — is the Institution's actual point of difference from
either a purely secular school or a purely Islamiyyah school in the same market.

**A limit that binds Master 02 — [RECOVERED — `assessment/ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md`,
scope]:** as respects the Islamic and Arabic corpus, the Institution is
**supplementary**. Pupils attend a conventional school in the morning. It does
not teach mathematics, science or English **and must never assess them**, with
the single exception of WAEC, which appears on a transcript only as **إثبات
الجلوس — proof that the candidate sat it**, and is never a subject of this
Institution's own marking.

## 3.1 The Founder — a permanent constitutional status

**[RECOVERED — Charter Art. 27A]** The status of "the Founder" is **personal to
the natural person recorded in the Authority Register**. It is **not an office**;
it is not held by virtue of holding the office of Head of Schools /
Administrator; and it **cannot be created, granted, transferred or conferred** on
any other person by this Charter, the Board, the Founder, or any authority. It is
permanent, is not subject to appointment, confirmation, election or removal, and
continues for the Founder's lifetime. It ends only on the Founder's passing, and
even then the identity remains permanently recorded; **it does not pass to, and
cannot be assumed by, any successor.**

**[RECOVERED — Charter Arts. 27C–27F]** The Founder's reserved powers and
ceremonial and constitutional entitlements stand as those Articles provide,
adopted here without modification and without creating a second source of the
same status *(Constitution Art. 9.1)*.

the office of **Chairman of the Board** and the office of **Head of Schools /
Administrator**, and exercises the Founder's constitutional authority solely

## 4.2 Certification philosophy

**[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION — curriculum corpus; detail in Master 02]**

Three principles belong at constitutional level. Their machinery does not.

1. **A certificate states what was measured, and no more.** The transcript has
   three sections and a thing is reported in exactly one: **(أ)** a graded mark ·
   **(ب)** a completion judgement · **(ج)** a condition satisfied. *"A thing
   measured twice is counted twice, and a curriculum that counts twice is lying
   about its own load."*
2. **الشرط لا يحمل درجةً أبدًا.** A condition is satisfied or not satisfied; it
   never carries a mark.
3. **The Institution does not make its own award hostage to a third party.**
   WAEC is recorded as **إثبات الجلوس** — proof of sitting, not of passing.

## 4.3 The graduate

**[UNRECOVERED — and it must stay visible]**

Graduation is defined as **الكفايات العشر + مناقشة التخرج + إثبات الجلوس لـ
WAEC**. Of the Ten Competencies, **only the tenth has recovered text** —
**الخدمة والأمانة**, `[RECOVERED]`, *«مشروع خدمة منفَّذ موثَّق الأثر وسجل سلوك
بتقدير جدارة فأعلى»*, with the ruling *«والعاشرة شرطُ استلام شهادة التخرج لا
زينةَ فيها»*.

> Institution's own graduation standard. **They are not invented here** — PUB-006

# PART SIX · AMENDMENT, CHANGE CONTROL, AND VERSION EVENTS

## 6.1 Which procedure applies

There is no single amendment rule, and pretending otherwise would be the silent

## 8.1 The rules of evidence

**[CURRENT COUNCIL DIRECTION, consolidating the curriculum corpus's L-26–L-29
and PUB-006 Art. 4]**

1. **لا يُختلق دليلٌ مفقود.** A missing source is recorded as missing. Where a
   text does not exist, the document says so **in the same register as everything
   else** — never in an apologetic tone, and never by quietly supplying something
   plausible.
2. **لا يُخلط المستردُّ بقرار المجلس الحالي.** Recovered material is never blended
   with a present-day ruling.
3. **لا يُقدَّم استنتاجٌ على أنه نصٌّ مستردّ.** An inference is never presented as
   a text.
4. **Every provision carries its provenance tag**, and the tag travels with the
   provision when it moves.
