## Legal & Compliance Policies Declaration {.unnumbered}

::: {custom-style="MessageByline"}
Presented for approval by the Board of Trustees
:::

This Legal & Compliance Handbook contains the full text of the eleven legal and compliance policies (LEG-001 through LEG-011) identified in the Institutional Governance Compendium's Legal & Compliance Policies registry (Section 18.5, AMIU-IGC-004). Each policy is presented in full, in the format a Board of Trustees, legal counsel, and an accreditation body would expect: purpose, scope, definitions, binding policy statement, procedural detail, enforcement, and cross-references to related governance documents.

Where the Constitution (AMIU-CON-001) sets the University's supreme legal architecture and the Institutional Governance Compendium (AMIU-IGC-004) tracks which policies exist and who is accountable for them, this Handbook is where the University's regulatory exposure is actually managed: FERPA and GDPR compliance, state and multi-country regulatory filings, intellectual property ownership, non-discrimination and Title IX, cross-border payment systems, and the immigration-compliance dimension of international faculty and student support.

Approval authority for individual policies in this Handbook follows the Constitution's separation between academic and non-academic governance (Article 9, Section 9.1): the Board of Trustees approves the compliance, privacy, and regulatory-filing policies that carry institutional legal risk; the Deputy Vice-Chancellor, Administration & Finance approves international faculty recruitment, matching the domestic Recruitment & Hiring Policy it mirrors; and the University Senate approves the International Student Support Policy, consistent with its identically-titled academic-affairs sibling in the Student Policies Handbook.
