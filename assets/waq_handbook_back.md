## Publication Certification Statement {.unnumbered}

This Waqf & Research Handbook is certified by the AMIU Office of Institutional Planning as the authoritative and current statement of the nine Waqf and research policies it contains, effective as of the date shown on the Document Control page. It supersedes all prior drafts and non-final versions.

Every policy's citations to the Constitution and cross-references to other University documents are checked against the Constitution's actual Article and Section headings and the Institutional Governance Compendium's Doc Code registry, so every citation in this Handbook resolves to the provision or document it names. Approval authority is assigned by subject matter: the Board of Trustees for the Waqf & Endowment Board's founding Charter and the institutional-risk trio; the Waqf & Endowment Board itself for its own donor and fundraising operating plans; and the University Senate or Deputy Vice-Chancellor, Academic Affairs for the three research and publication policies, consistent with the Constitution's separation of academic authority from financial and operational authority.

With this Handbook, all 107 documents of the Institutional Governance Compendium's original source registry (Section 17, AMIU-IGC-004) have been drafted in full across nine flagship publications. Document 108 — the Institutional Effectiveness & Administrative Unit Assessment Framework, adopted after the original registry as a subsequent governance enhancement — remains outstanding.

A reader who identifies an issue is asked to report it to info@edu.amiu.com for consideration in the next revision.

**Certified for Executive Release** — AMIU Office of Institutional Planning, Founding-Decade Edition.
