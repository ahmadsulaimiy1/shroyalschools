## Academic Declaration {.unnumbered}

::: {custom-style="MessageByline"}
Presented for approval by the University Senate
:::

This Academic Handbook contains the full text of the nineteen academic policies (ACA-001 through ACA-019) identified in the Institutional Governance Compendium's Academic Handbook registry (Section 18.2, AMIU-IGC-004). Each policy is presented in full, in the format a Board of Trustees, legal counsel, and an accreditation body would expect: purpose, scope, definitions, binding policy statement, procedural detail, enforcement, and cross-references to related governance documents.

Where the Constitution (AMIU-CON-001) sets the University's supreme legal architecture and the Institutional Governance Compendium (AMIU-IGC-004) tracks which policies exist and who is accountable for them, this Handbook is where the substance of academic governance actually lives: the credit-hour definition, the academic integrity sanctions schedule, the curriculum approval workflow, and every other rule a student, instructor, or administrator must actually follow.
