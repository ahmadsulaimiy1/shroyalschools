## Digital Experience & Editorial Bible Declaration {.unnumbered}

::: {custom-style="MessageByline"}
Presented for approval by the Board of Trustees, the President & Vice-Chancellor, and the Deputy Vice-Chancellor, Administration & Finance
:::

This Digital Experience & Editorial Bible contains the full text of ten standards (DXB-001 through DXB-010) governing the University's public website, its Learning Management System, and the six operating portals built upon it. Each standard is presented in the format a Board of Trustees, legal counsel, and an accreditation body would expect: purpose, scope, definitions, binding policy statement, procedural and functional detail, enforcement, and cross-references to related governance documents.

This Bible does not describe software that has been built; it specifies, at the standard of a serious international institution, what shall be built and to what editorial and design discipline. Where a portal or capability is not yet operational, this Bible says so plainly, consistent with the Never-Display Rule it itself establishes (DXB-001, Section 6.0): the University's public-facing surfaces present only what is true and complete, and its internal governance documents &#8212; this one among them &#8212; are where the honest state of a still-developing capability belongs.

Every office named across these ten standards is a title already established in the Constitution or the existing document suite &#8212; the Director, Communications; the Director, Information Technology; the Deputy Vice-Chancellors; the Dean of Students; the Registrar; the Board of Trustees &#8212; and no new executive office is created by this Bible. Approval authority for individual standards follows the Constitution's separation between academic and non-academic governance (Article 9, Section 9.1), with the Fee and Pricing Presentation Policy (DXB-010, Section 7.0) reserved to the Board of Trustees given its direct financial-policy consequence, matching the approval standard already applied to the Compensation, Honoraria, Stipends, Allowances & Revenue Participation Policy (OPS-010).
