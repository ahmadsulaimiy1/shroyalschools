## Publication Certification Statement {.unnumbered}

This Digital Experience & Editorial Bible is certified by the AMIU Office of Communications as the authoritative and current statement of the ten digital-experience standards it contains, effective as of the date shown on the Document Control page. It supersedes all prior drafts and non-final versions.

Every standard's citations to the Constitution and cross-references to other University documents are checked against the Constitution's actual Article and Section headings and the existing document suite's real titles and offices, so every citation in this Bible resolves to the provision, office, or document it names. No provision of this Bible asserts that the public website, the Learning Management System, or any of its six operating portals is currently operational; each is specified as a standard to be built against, and the public website's own Never-Display Rule (DXB-001, Section 6.0) governs what this Bible's own honesty about that fact may and may not say on the public surfaces it governs.

Registration disclosure. Standards DXB-001 through DXB-010 are a new document series and are not yet entered as rows in the Institutional Governance Compendium's 108-document registry. This is a deliberate, disclosed scope boundary, not an oversight: the Compendium's registry (assets/igc_data.py) is the single source of truth that generates the Compendium's own roster, phase-roadmap, and responsibility-matrix tables, and adding ten rows by hand, without regenerating those dependent tables in the same pass, would recreate the exact drift the Compendium's data module was built to prevent. Formal registration of DXB-001 through DXB-010 into the Compendium is recommended for the Compendium's next revision cycle.

Language-policy correction disclosure. An earlier working version of the University's homepage artifact presented French and Urdu as fully live languages alongside English and Arabic. This Bible's Language Strategy (DXB-009, Section 4.0) establishes English and Arabic as the only two launch languages, with French, Urdu, Turkish, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Melayu, Hausa, and Swahili as a named future-readiness roadmap. The homepage artifact was corrected in the same session this Bible was produced, so that the published artifact and this Bible's stated policy do not disagree with each other.

A reader who identifies an issue is asked to report it to info@edu.amiu.com for consideration in the next revision.
