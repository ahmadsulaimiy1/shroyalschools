# The Psychology of Everyday Wisdom — Flagship Edition

This folder contains the flagship editorial edition of the manuscript "The Psychology of Everyday Wisdom: 50 Truths Backed by Science & Human Nature," restructured and typeset in the same production standard as the "76 Wisdoms of Success" flagship edition.

## What's in the file

`The-Psychology-of-Everyday-Wisdom-Flagship-Edition.docx` / `.pdf` (identical layout, 130 pages, 6"×9" book trim) include:

- Full front matter: half title, title page, copyright page, dedication, foreword, author's preface, "How to Use This Book," and an original introduction essay ("Why Ordinary Wisdom Needs Science")
- Five thematic parts (Emotional Intelligence & Inner Mastery, Boundaries/Trust/Social Dynamics, The Mind's Illusions: Cognitive Biases & Perception, Motivation/Habits/Decision-Making, Time/Meaning/Identity), each with its own divider page
- All 50 wisdoms, each restructured into: the one-line wisdom statement, Core Reflection, one or more "The Science" citation boxes, Contemporary Application, three Action Points, a Reflection Question, and a Takeaway line
- End matter: Final Reflection, Author's Closing Message, Recommended Reading (real behavioral-science books), a 32-term Glossary of the cited biases/effects, a Researcher & Concept Index, Subject Index, About the Author, About Future Publications
- A designed front and back cover (Deep Ink Navy / Warm Copper / Ivory palette — deliberately distinct from the Islamic edition's navy/gold so the two flagship books read as siblings, not twins)

## Editorial notes

- **Author credit:** Ahmad Sulaimiy, per your confirmation, matching the byline on the companion 76 Wisdoms edition.
- **Source fidelity:** Every "Science" box's research/theory citation is drawn directly from the source manuscript's "Why It's True" bullets — nothing was invented. Where a bullet had no named researcher, the box still appears but with no dangling citation line.
- **New editorial content:** Core Reflection, Contemporary Application (expanded from the source's terse action bullets), Action Points (3, one added beyond the source's 2), Reflection Questions, and Takeaways were drafted for each wisdom, grounded strictly in that wisdom's own source material.
- **Index consolidation:** The Researcher & Concept Index merges citation variants that six independent editorial passes phrased differently (e.g. "Cialdini" and "Cialdini, Influence") into single entries, the same normalization approach used for the 76 Wisdoms Qur'an/Hadith index.
- **Scope disclaimer:** Per standard practice for a popular-psychology title, the copyright page includes a note that this is educational synthesis, not clinical or therapeutic advice, and points readers to primary sources for rigor.
